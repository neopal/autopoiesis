# 001 — Subtractive Ecology

Disposable browser-art spike for Mutine. No production claim.

## Concept

A field is not painted into existence. It begins with a fixed stock of pale deposits. Removal opens a dark wound; the wound’s rim becomes the only place new green deposits can originate. Matter is neither infinite nor decorative: subtraction routes it.

## Design

- **Visual system:** chalk-white mineral threads on a black-green field; cuts expose oval scar cavities; scar-born growth is acid-lime.
- **Interaction:** press or drag across a pale thread to remove that finite body.
- **Causality indicator:** a compact top-centre strip of small material cells receives released matter; each scar has three dim rim gates that light as its redirected sprouts leave the actual scar boundary. No explanatory UI copy accompanies this relation.
- **Rule:** **Deposited marks are finite material; new marks must grow out of actual scar geometry; erasures must causally redirect future marks.**
- **Responsive:** one full-viewport canvas; pointer events work for mouse and touch.

## Code

`index.html` is standalone HTML/CSS/JS; no build system, dependency, source imagery, or generated assets.

## Preview

From this directory:

```bash
python -m http.server 4173
```

Then open `http://localhost:4173/`.

## 90-second evidence method

1. Wait two seconds and note the pale finite deposits and mostly empty material strip.
2. In the first 20 seconds, cut through three pale threads (press/drag directly across them).
3. Watch each removal: a dark oval scar remains, lime motes rise into the top strip, then one to three lime sprouts emerge from the scar rim—not from the pointer or an arbitrary field location.
4. In the next 40 seconds, repeat at two different thread angles. Confirm each later sprout follows the specific removed thread/scar orientation and begins at its perimeter.
5. The field intentionally does not regenerate: continue cutting until the original stock is exhausted; no mark appears unless a cut first supplied material.

## Verdict

**Pass as a concept spike:** the causal rule is materially enacted rather than narrated. The dark scar, rim gates, lime scar-born marks, and temporary material strip make subtraction → stored matter → redirected growth legible in one sparse system. It is intentionally disposable; calibration, accessibility copy, long-run balancing, and production integration are out of scope.
