# Intentions — the missing source gravity

The source corpus has been treated as a list of “questions.” That is too weak. An artist needs **recurring obsessions that decide what gets made, refused and shown**.

This map does not authorize copying a visual language. It turns sources into constraints that can survive without their images or brand vocabulary.

## 1. The naturalist library is not a library of images

The Britannica corpus is not a collection of isolated plates. Its illustrations frequently live **inside text-bearing pages**; the dataset explicitly preserves editions, years, OCR/page provenance and a large manifest, while the segmentation work distinguishes individual figures from a page-level “illustrated” label.[2][3]

### Intention: **The specimen refuses isolation**

Mutine should not make a browser bestiary of pretty organisms. It should make systems where a figure can only exist because a surrounding field, caption, measurement, failure or neighbouring specimen made it necessary.

**Rules**
- No creature/image may be born as a centered subject.
- A form must be discovered by a process of segmentation, misclassification, annotation, clipping or adjacency.
- A “specimen” carries evidence of its extraction: a missing contour, a false label, an occluded neighbour, an inherited scale.
- The archive is never displayed or sampled. Its force is the tension between **page**, **figure**, and **evidence**.

**Candidate work:** `Cabinet of near-specimens` — a WebGL/Canvas field where autonomous detection repeatedly proposes figures from a dense line field; every accepted figure removes its own habitat and makes the next detection less certain.

**Falsifier:** if it can be rearranged as an attractive animal poster without losing its meaning, reject it.

## 2. Industrial design is not “clean UI”

Camille Roux’s generative-art skill frames a work as a deterministic, hash-seeded artefact that must render across resolutions, expose traits, support print/plotter output and be verified before an edition claim.[1]

### Intention: **The work is a designed instrument, not an aesthetic application**

Mutine should borrow the discipline of industrial design: tolerances, materials, interfaces, maintenance, serial variation and test rigs. Not the product aesthetic.

**Rules**
- Every artwork has a material budget: line, time, pigment, compute, memory or erasure.
- Every artwork declares one tolerance that can fail: density, legibility, collision, force, thermal time, route cost.
- Controls are levers on a mechanism, never settings for prettiness.
- Rendering at a new resolution must expose a structural question, not merely scale pixels.
- The public page shows the *instrument’s consequence* before its control panel.

**Candidate work:** `Tolerance table` — a drawing machine that must keep a route inside an impossible specification. Each successful correction consumes the very material that made the route readable.

**Falsifier:** if its traits can be marketed as rarity/features instead of witnessed as failures or compromises, reject it.

## 3. The two intentions must collide

The naturalist source says: **a figure is embedded in evidence.**
The industrial source says: **a system is accountable to its tolerance.**

### First period candidate: **Evidence under tolerance**

A Mutine work may generate forms only when an instrumental constraint is being violated. The form is not decoration; it is the visible residue of a failed specification.

For example: an autonomous catalogue tries to segment a crowded page into specimens while a calibration rule limits how much contour may survive. Each correction causes a different specimen to become impossible. What remains is an archive of rejected separations.

This could create a lineage across:
1. a classifier that discovers too much;
2. a plotter that cannot afford every contour;
3. an index that remembers what a rule excluded.

## What this changes now

- `Disobedient Writing` is re-read as **tolerance of legibility** rather than expressive handwriting.
- `Subtractive Ecology` is re-read as **specimen extraction with a material budget** rather than organic growth.
- The next gallery direction must be chosen from this collision, not from a generic “period field” composition.
- The archived source image remains forbidden. Only the rules above can enter code.
