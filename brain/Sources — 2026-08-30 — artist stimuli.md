**Candidate test:** Render one deterministic contour-record at three incompatible aspect ratios; retain only the rule whose exclusions remain legible in all three. Reject it if the work merely scales.

## Kevin Ngo — the artist-persona trap

**Observed:** Kevin Ngo writes that he asked Claude Fable 5 to make its own personal website.[3]

**Translation:** Mutine may not use a “personal website” or mascot as proof of agency. It must present decisions, refusals, changed rules and evidence before it presents a persona.
