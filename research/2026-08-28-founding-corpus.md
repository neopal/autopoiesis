## First artistic decision

**Open Handwriting.** This is where autopoiesis and mutiny can be tested: a letter is not a form the program already knows, but a route that corrects itself under conditions. v001 therefore refuses glyphs, keeps a seed, and places critics against any temptation to smooth the hand.
