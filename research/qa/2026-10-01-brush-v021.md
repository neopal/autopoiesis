## Concept and gate

**The brush answers under the cut.** A dense p5.js 2D grain lattice is raked by a path. The path deletes actual cells, deflects neighbours, and produces a distant answer cluster whose cell load and tilt change. A tap is refused; only travel commits material memory.

The three preceding Brush works were a continuous membrane (v018), a hanging ordinal register (v019), and a suspended WebGL drying coil (v020). v021 changes representation, medium, temporal behaviour, and encounter: cellular 2D field, drag threshold, local removal plus non-local response.

## Cultural translation

Reference: [little-critters](https://github.com/GordenSun/little-critters)

Observed mechanism: code-drawn agents respond to pointer proximity with reciprocal attention; the visitor participates in a situated relation rather than operating a generic panel.

Mutine translation: remove the animals and look-back. The drag becomes a moving witness; a local rake opens a real absence while a distant cluster answers through changed cell geometry.

Refused: animals, eyes, paper scene, source palette, source composition, and head-turning surface vocabulary.
