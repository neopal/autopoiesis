## Concept and progression

- **Hypothesis:** if situated attention becomes language evidence, a first word should remain latent and only a different reply should make the sentence mishear elsewhere.
- **Changed rule:** one word is a latent witness; a sufficiently distant different word replies; both witnesses remain exact while three remote words become a substitution, split phrase, and fading echo.
- **Radical rupture:** v041 leaves v040's continuous relief, v039's cellular raster plate, and v038's cut-out field. It changes medium to browser-native DOM typography, composition to a scattered sentence in open dark ground, and encounter to a two-word witness/reply relation.
- **Repeated gesture refused:** single-location mark/press/hold followed by a remote material consequence.
- **Falsifier:** delete if the first word mutates, a word answers itself, remote roles are cosmetic, the blind field reads as a generic poster or node diagram, later inferences reset, or exact lifting fails.
- **Deletion condition:** the caption-free work must read as language under a bad inference, not as a generic typographic poster or control surface.

## Cultural field

- **Reference:** [little-critters](https://github.com/GordenSun/little-critters)
- **Observed mechanism:** code-drawn agents make pointer proximity legible as attention; the visitor enters a local field and the field answers without a dashboard.
- **Translated Mutine rule:** refuse animals, eyes, characters, paper scenes, head-turning, source palette, source composition, source code, examples, and API surface; translate reciprocity into a word relation whose error is spent in three remote text/layout mutations.
- **Visible consequence:** the first view is a scattered oversized sentence; a first touch is geometry-neutral; a different reply changes actual remote word content and layout.
