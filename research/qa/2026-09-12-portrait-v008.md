## Artist gate

- **Hypothesis:** a self-image can expose its decisions by routing around what it will not retain, rather than by printing a second gaze or folding its outline.
- **Changed rule:** a remembered decision becomes a blind spot. A local contour span is displaced into two lips that separate around the absence and rejoin downstream; the aperture shifts as if the face has to keep living around the missing place.
- **Visible consequence:** the portrait carries a split-and-rejoin event in its contour and a return route from the absent center. Pointer and keyboard gestures change routes, contour geometry, and aperture geometry, not only colour or text.
- **Falsifier:** if returning the latest absence leaves the counter-contour, aperture, split lips, or downstream route unchanged, the direction is decorative and fails.
- **Deletion condition:** delete v008 if a caption-free pair cannot identify a local absence with two contour edges that reunite, or if the dark spot / return line alone carries the meaning.
- **Deterministic state:** `SEED = 0x53505638`, 14 stages, 56 contour points, 13 points per lip, 16 points per return route, four blind spots maximum.
