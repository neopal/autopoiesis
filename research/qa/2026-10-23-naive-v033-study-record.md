## Changed rule

v033 abandons v032's wrong-family tree, v031's counterfeit orbit, and v030's pressure slab. One p5.js Canvas 2D proof sheet listens to three distinct keyboard witnesses without writing them. Only a complete witness commits a distant misread: one glyph changes form, another inherits the old form, a fourth opens as a blank, and the proof line breaks. Four errors are bounded, replayed, and liftable.
