## Artistic gate

### Repeated grammar refused

The last three Pure SVG records were still organized around a body/field that received a visitor event: v011 folded a hinge through the animal and routes, v012 opened a threshold in the contour, and v013 changed a radial cell field into a reciprocal gaze surface. Their repeated gesture was passage or attention travelling through a composed SVG field and receiving a visible response.

### Structural and perceptual rupture

v014 makes an explicit inversion and medium/encounter switch. The radial field of cells is abandoned for three horizontal corridors made from eighteen angular gates. The visitor no longer places a gaze that is answered by looking roles. A visitor constraint closes three neighbouring gates around the nearest point, moves one true gap to another corridor, and nudges one escape gate away. No gate looks back.

The rupture changes representation, composition, interaction, temporal memory, and viewer task: the visitor reads route closure and absence rather than reciprocal attention. The blind falsifier remains active: if the result reads as a decorative barcode, or if the gap migration is not visible in occupancy and corridor geometry, v014 must be deleted rather than polished.

### Falsifier and deletion condition

Hide labels, readout, controls, witness marks, gap marks, count, captions, and prose. The direction fails if a local passage does not materially close, if a distant gate does not disappear, or if the field still simulates a look-back through colour or decoration. Delete v014 if the blocked gates, missing gate, corridor signatures, or exact undo are not structural.

## Cultural translation

- Reference: `little-critters` — https://github.com/GordenSun/little-critters
- Observed mechanism: situated code-drawn agents respond to pointer proximity by changing attention and looking back, making the visitor part of a reciprocal encounter rather than a dashboard operator.
- Mutine translation: invert reciprocity. Remove situated agents and turn visitor proximity into a topological obstruction: the nearest local passage closes, a true gap migrates to another corridor, and the line refuses to answer with a look.
- Visible consequence: the blind field is three broken horizontal corridors of angular gates, not a radial reciprocal cell field. After input, neighbouring gate geometry closes one passage, one distant gate disappears, and the corridor signature changes.
- Refused vocabulary: animals, characters, paper style, palette, head-turning scene, composition, and source surface language.
- Direction consequence: closes v013 reciprocal-attention grammar and opens `constraint-and-topology` plus `anti-instrument`: attention becomes route closure and refusal rather than social reply.
