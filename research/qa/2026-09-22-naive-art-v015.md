## Concept and structural rupture

v015 is **The picture copies the wrong thing.** The last three Naive works retained a single-scene grammar: bridge/fork route consequences in v012–v013, then a material puncture and missing room in v014. v015 rejects that grammar in two visible dimensions:

- **Representation/composition:** one paper scene becomes four related picture panels.
- **Interaction/temporal behavior:** a visitor no longer tears, branches, or reroutes one object. A bounded misread makes one panel lean, sends a wrong motif to a distant panel, and removes that motif from a third panel. Undo rebuilds the exact preceding panel arrangement.

The changed rule is **look → local misread → distant wrong copy → blank slot**. This is a relational copy-error experiment, not a palette, texture, density, title, or caption change.

## Cultural translation

Selected reference: **little-critters** (`https://github.com/GordenSun/little-critters`). The observed mechanism is situated reciprocity: proximity changes attention and the field answers the visitor. Mutine refuses animals, characters, head-turning animation, the source paper scene, palette, composition, and surface vocabulary. The Naive translation is a family of four childlike picture panels that remember being seen by disagreeing with one another: local lean, distant wrong copy, and third-panel absence.

The visible falsifier is explicit: if caption-free viewing sees only four decorative cards, if the distant copy is only a color echo, or if the blank slot is not materially visible, the translation fails. The direction consequence is to close the Naive route/single-scene object-void grammar after v010–v014 and open a distributed picture-memory line.
