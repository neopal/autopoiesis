## Radical progression gate

The last three Pure SVG works were:

- v028: seven filled aperture plates; attention followed by withdrawal;
- v029: nine woven compound bodies; a two-place offer/answer handshake;
- v030: one negative-space contour; press-drag from near cavity to far cavity.

Their repeated gesture was attention becoming a non-local change among filled bodies or cavities. Their repeated grammar was a field/object made from broad compound SVG paths, with a continuous or carried pointer relation.

v031 breaks that invariant in three visible ways:

1. **Representation:** filled plates, compound bodies, and a cavity-bearing contour become one sparse stroked line with nine discrete joints.
2. **Encounter:** press-drag carriage becomes two separated attention events; the first event is inert, adjacent attention is refused, and distance is required.
3. **Memory:** deformation of bodies/cavities becomes a bounded ledger of path-order rewrites; the unoffered witness is inserted into the actual line route and can be lifted exactly.

## Cultural translation

- Reference: [little-critters](https://github.com/GordenSun/little-critters)
- Observed mechanism: situated code-drawn agents respond to pointer proximity with legible attention and reciprocal looking, making the visitor part of a social field without a dashboard.
- Mutine translation: refuse animals, eyes, characters, paper, and head-turning imagery. Attention becomes a temporary state on a line joint; two distant attentions cause an unoffered joint to become the line's witness and rewrite path order.
- Visible consequence: the blind first view is one thin asymmetrical line. After two distant offers, the actual line reroutes through a joint the visitor did not offer and three joint paths carry tension.
- Falsifier: first attention writing geometry, adjacent attention committing, the offered pair simply becoming the answer, witness state remaining a label/glow, history being ignored, or the blind view reading as a graph/interface.
- Anti-copy: no animals, eyes, character designs, paper scene, source palette, source composition, source code, API surface, or head-turning image is reproduced.
- Direction consequence: closes the cavity/body/singular-contour sequence and opens line-as-social-mishearing: relation is carried by path order and an absent witness.
