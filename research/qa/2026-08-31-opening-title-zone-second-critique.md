## Three-voice reading

### Formalist / experience director

The exclusion is legible as a deliberate threshold: the work now withholds evidence where reading must begin, while remaining visibly agitated elsewhere. It is materially better than a title placed atop a lively mark. The reservation is nevertheless rectangular and broad. At this desktop view the dark chamber can read as a compositional clearing, not merely a repair, because the tolerance line and colonies survive beyond it; at narrower widths that claim remains untested.

### Historian / archivist

The only supported claim is local and technical: this worktree’s renderer reserves measured title geometry and the observed local frame was clear. The public Vercel gallery remains an earlier version; no provider/revision evidence connects this correction to production. No audience, lineage, work-status or period claim follows.

### Cynic / release defender

This is the same self-authored renderer inspected through the same local run. A source test plus one pixel-sampled desktop frame does not establish five-width animated collision absence, nor does it repair the independent Handwriting and Brush D1 failures. `body { overflow-x:hidden }` remains in the existing gallery CSS and has not been treated as evidence of responsive success. The release verdict remains **hold**.

## Decision

**Retain the correction as a local, unpromoted gallery revision; do not advance it.** The accepted criticism is that a quiet region is justified only when observed surrounding activity keeps it charged. The resisted shortcut is treating 10 catalog/static tests, a clean local console, or this one frame as a production compatibility pass.

## Handoff

- **Changed rule:** an exclusion zone must be judged as a relation between silence and surviving field activity, not merely as the absence of collision.
- **Observed consequence:** at 1264×625 the title zone had zero non-background canvas samples across 270 points while colonies and the tolerance line remained visible around it.
- **Criticism accepted / resisted:** accepted the risk of a broad rectangular chamber becoming inert; resisted both a title-background patch and a local-to-production inference.
- **Next question:** after a verified deployment, does the gallery retain that relation at 320×568, 390×844, 768×1024, 1280×800 and 1920×1080, with no title collision, overflow or loss of the 44px exit?
- **Hypothesis died:** the assumption that collision absence alone establishes a successful threshold died. No art hypothesis died.
