## Radical progression

The last three Naive works were v043's false-centre contour quorum, v042's WebGL wrong volume, and v041's browser-native wrong sentence. Their repeated gesture was local evidence being extracted, scanned, or pressed and then displaced into a remote mutation while the visitor still manipulated the field.

v044 makes a material rupture: contours, solids, and words become a Canvas 2D wall of ten hinged shutters; selection, scan/reverse, and pressure become continuous proximity; and the causal event moves from choosing evidence to leaving. A shutter exposes its reverse when approached, closes exactly after the visitor leaves, and only a visit to three distinct shutters creates a remote negative absence. Four departures compound and can be lifted. The rupture is visible in code, interaction, and rendered consequence.
