## Radical progression

The last three Pure SVG works were a negative chamber sheet (v023), an asymmetrical witness-knot (v024), and one open contour with a migrating gap (v025). Their repeated grammar was one enclosing body, a scarce absence, and a spatial relation that moved topology.

v026 makes a material rupture in representation and encounter: it leaves the enclosing SVG path family for seventeen autonomous filled polygons in a dispersed constellation. It also inverts the recent reciprocal-attention translation. Attention only arms; departure is the event. The source fragment contracts, a distant relay fragment expands, and a third echo fragment changes. Four departures remain in bounded memory and alter later geometry.

## Cultural translation

- **Reference:** [little-critters](https://github.com/GordenSun/little-critters)
- **Observed mechanism:** situated code-drawn agents turn pointer proximity into reciprocal attention; local approach changes where an agent looks back, making the visitor part of a social field.
- **Mutine translation:** invert looking-back into looking-away. The visitor's attention is temporary and non-committing. Leaving withdraws the attended fragment and redistributes consequence to a non-local relay and echo.
- **Visible consequence:** the blind field is a distributed constellation of filled geometric fragments, not a body, route, chamber, knot, or contour. After departure, three actual SVG polygon geometries change and later departures inherit the altered population.
- **Falsifier:** attention writes memory; departure changes only colour or a marker; only the local fragment changes; the remote reply is a fixed neighbour; or the blind field reads as generic polygon scatter.
- **Anti-copy:** no animals, characters, eyes, paper scene, source palette, source composition, source code, API surface, or head-turning surface vocabulary is reproduced.
- **Direction consequence:** opens a discrete-population grammar for Pure SVG and closes the enclosing-body / single-boundary sequence. It abandons direct reciprocity in favour of absence as the only committed social act.
