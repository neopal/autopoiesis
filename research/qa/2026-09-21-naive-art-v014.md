## Concept and structural rupture

v014 is **The mistake refuses a room.** The last three Naive works retained a route grammar: bypass (v010), bridge (v012), and fork (v013). Their repeated gesture was a line approaching a decision, changing lane or plurality, then leaving downstream through a house/ground/route diagram.

v014 rejects that grammar in two visible dimensions:

- **Representation/composition:** one full-field object/void paper scene replaces the proposal/consequence route diptych.
- **Interaction/temporal behavior:** a visitor does not add another path; a bounded tear creates a missing room, lifts a fragment, notches the ground, and shifts the house. Undo reconstructs the exact previous material state.

The changed rule is **puncture → missing room → lifted fragment → re-cut scene**. This is a structural object/void experiment, not a palette, texture, density, title, or caption change.

## Cultural translation

Selected reference: **p5.brush** (`https://github.com/acamposuribe/p5.brush`). The observed mechanism was that material changes how geometry is produced and remembered rather than decorating fixed geometry. Mutine refuses the library's brushes, watercolor, hatching, palette, composition, and API surface. The Naive translation is a remembered wrong turn that punctures the flat scene, removes a room, lifts its fragment, and forces house/ground geometry to be re-cut around the absence.

The visible falsifier is explicit: if caption-free viewing sees an intact house with decorative stickers, or if input changes only a counter, the translation fails. The direction consequence is to close the Naive route-bundle grammar after v010–v013 and open an object/void line of inquiry.
