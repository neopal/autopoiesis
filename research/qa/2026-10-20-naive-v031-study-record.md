## Artistic direction

v031 is a structural rupture after the last three Naive works:

- v028: semantic mark strip, departure as causality, moved absence;
- v029: Canvas cut-paper picture, hesitant cross-seam drag, false relation;
- v030: continuous SVG slab, sustained pressure, remote bend and seam load.

The repeated gesture was single-field correction: one place is addressed, then a local or remote form carries a wrong relation. v031 refuses the single body and introduces a two-place attention unit in a native-WebGL orbit. The visible first render is seven irregular paper witnesses in depth, not a strip, patch picture, or continuous slab.

## Rule ledger

- **Hypothesis:** a held pair of nearby witnesses makes a naive picture misread its own evidence.
- **Changed rule:** first witness averts; second inherits the first witness's mark and becomes a counterfeit; a non-local third witness opens as a vacancy.
- **Visible consequence:** exactly three actual witness geometries change and remain changed for later pairs.
- **Interaction:** a single selection, click, or pointer proximity is inert. A distinct pair must be held for at least 420ms. Two simultaneous touch pointers and the keyboard/control path are equivalent meaningful routes.
- **Falsifier:** delete if the copied mark or vacancy is only colour/readout, if one place commits, if later pairs restart from baseline, or if exact lifting fails.
- **Deletion condition:** delete if the labels-off blind orbit reads as a generic 3D card wall rather than a field of forms that has misread itself.

## Cultural field translation

Reference: [little-critters](https://github.com/GordenSun/little-critters)

- **Observed mechanism:** a population of code-drawn animals forms a social field; pointer proximity changes attention, the animals look back, and the relation is reciprocal without a dashboard.
- **Mutine translation:** no animals, eyes, characters, or head-turning scene. Proximity becomes a two-place witness relation: the source turns away, the second copies its defect, and a remote third becomes vacant.
- **Visible consequence:** the mechanism is visible as a native-WebGL orbit and as three changed solids after a valid pair.
- **Falsifier:** if proximity or one click commits, or the third vacancy and copied defect are cosmetic, the translation fails.
- **Anti-copy:** no source code, API, assets, characters, palette, composition, or surface scene is reproduced.
- **Direction consequence:** the old pressure-memory slab direction is closed. A reciprocal misreading direction opens for Naive art: error becomes social transfer between situated forms.
