## Creative decision

- **Repeated grammar refused:** v029 radial stillness mass, v030 folded pressure register, v031 negative ribbon — all broad painted bodies absorbing a continuous offer.
- **Primary rupture:** change the medium and viewer task. v033 is native SVG rather than a p5.js Canvas 2D body; one or two quiet knocks arm an incomplete rhythm, and only the third knock can commit.
- **Changed rule:** the touched points never become marks. The completed triplet is parsed as material rhythm and relocates its consequence into three remote SVG plate geometries: hinge, counterweight, and dry void around one crooked seam.
- **Visible consequence:** nine irregular plates form a singular suspended laminate. After a valid triplet, the actual remote plate paths and seam change; the pointer trace is not retained.
- **Falsifier:** one tap changes geometry; dragging commits; the touched plate is always the answer; only colour/readout changes; fewer than three plate paths change; later triplets restart from seed; or the blind view reads as a generic grid/interface.
- **Deletion condition:** delete v033 if caption-free viewing cannot carry the dry-seam composition, three remote paths do not change, or lift does not restore the exact preceding signature.

- **Direction consequence:** closes the wound / dwell / pressure-register / negative-ribbon family and opens a dry-seam direction with a different medium and discrete encounter.
