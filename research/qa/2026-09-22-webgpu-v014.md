## Concept and radical progression

The previous three WebGPU works (`v011` deferred arrival, `v012` hinge, `v013` reciprocal looking) still made the crowd legible as a field of routes or independent angular marks. v014 rejects that grammar.

The primary rupture is a **representation, viewer-task, and memory-model switch**:

- from route strata and discrete agents to one continuous tessellated surface;
- from asking a population to look to dragging force into material;
- from downstream social consequence to retained topographic height and crease;
- from click/look events to a bounded pressure path whose geometry matters.

The new causal rule is: pressure compacts nearby nodes, raises a crease, changes tilt and grain, and stores the load in the surface itself. The visible field is not a color-coded witness above unchanged geometry.

The falsifier is caption-free: if the blind field reads as independent marks or a decorated grid, if pressure changes only color, or if the crease is not carried by changed nodes, the translation fails. The deletion condition is recorded in `studies/webgpu/v014/README.md` and the register.

## Cultural translation

- Reference: `p5-brush` — `https://github.com/acamposuribe/p5.brush`
- Observed mechanism: programmable material tools make pressure, density, grain, and directional fields alter how geometry is produced rather than being a skin applied after geometry.
- Mutine translation: pressure becomes a collective topological event; the WebGPU crowd compacts into one surface that stores force as height, tilt, grain, and crease.
- Refused surface vocabulary: brush library tools, watercolor, hatching, painterly palette, copied composition, and API surface.
- Direction consequence: closes WebGPU v009–v013's route/agent grammar and opens a pressure-surface direction.

## Critique and hold

Accepted structural critique: the work makes the representation switch real in the engine: the crowd is rendered as one surface, and pressure changes node position, height, tilt, grain, crease, and memory rather than adding a witness mark.

Unresolved doubt: independent caption-free perceptual comparison remains incomplete. The key question is whether a viewer can see a continuous topographic object, a pressure-caused crease, and retained material load before reading the controls or text. The candidate remains **held** until that perceptual doubt and production readback are resolved.
