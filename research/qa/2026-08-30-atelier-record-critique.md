## Formalist / experience-director reading

The record has a coherent, severe typographic grammar. In both proofs the warm paper, black editorial display face, monospaced evidence layer, sharp rules, and restrained oxide accent are materially aligned with `DESIGN.md`. The desktop proof gives the central sentence real scale and leaves enough unoccupied paper for the counterclaim to arrive after it. The mobile proof deliberately recomposes: the masthead shortens, the headline becomes a compact three-line block, and the evidence paragraph follows in a legible single column. No collision is visible in either supplied crop.

But this is an accountability essay before it is an encounter. Its first viewport is dominated by the statement “The studio does not advance by claiming,” not an executable work. That can be valid for a record route, yet it is not an exhibition surface in the `Explore / Inspect` sense. The uncommitted gallery change makes this record a header-level route and repeats it in the small primary study nav (`atelier record` / `record`). This contradicts the operating order that external/process documents must not be the primary exit from an exhibition surface, and it reopens the audit’s complaint that process state competes with the work.

The record also exceeds the sparse-claim discipline in one seam: it says that an “engineering drawing” method is articulated in a “studio’s source-gravity map.” A repository search found no occurrence of `source-gravity` or `source gravity map`; the wording is therefore unsupported by the inspected materials. The same page labels itself `PUBLIC ACCOUNTABILITY / DAY 001`, but the supplied evidence does not establish a public daily-record series. Neither statement is a basis for a release claim.

## Cynic reading

The passing catalogue test turns the new route into an asserted accountability requirement (`the public gallery must link its daily accountability record`) rather than independently testing whether the route belongs in the public encounter. It verifies words such as “Self portrait” and “Engineering drawing,” not the truth of the associated state claims, route availability, or responsive composition. A static screenshot can demonstrate visual placement only; it cannot ratify the route as a public exhibition path.

The page honestly states that the two v001 works remain unpromoted and have unresolved responsive QA. That honesty is worth retaining. It does not, however, compensate for elevating the record into primary gallery navigation while D1 remains blocked and the route itself has no deployed verification.

## Decision

**Hold the atelier record as an uncommitted internal/accountability draft.** Do not delete its useful factual inventory, but do not release or promote the gallery links. The visible design can be retained for a later, deliberately secondary record route after the work encounter and P0 browser matrix have been resolved.

Before any future release packet, remove or source the unsupported “source-gravity map” assertion; decide whether the record is a secondary evidence path rather than primary navigation; and verify the exact deployed route through the full D1 matrix. This critique requests no code change because the valid browser probe remains permission-blocked and the present packet is read-only.
