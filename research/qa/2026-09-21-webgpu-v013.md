## Concept and radical progression

The previous three WebGPU works (`v010` switched order, `v011` deferred arrival, `v012` hinge) all used a continuous crowd field whose memory was expressed as route geometry: crossing, holding/releasing, and convergence/fold/fan. v013 rejects that grammar.

The primary rupture is a **viewer-task and representation switch**:

- from continuous route strata to a field of discrete angular marks;
- from an absent/crossed path to a situated population that can face a visitor;
- from one-way downstream consequence to reciprocal attention;
- from route witnesses to local orientation, occupied-position shift, and social role.

The new causal rule is: a remembered look turns a local cohort toward its bounded point, a surrounding ring answers with a counter-orientation, and a third ring settles into a shared decision. The visible field changes positions, angles, and scale; it is not a color or counter-only effect.

The falsifier is caption-free: if the blind field reads as an unchanged grid with brighter marks, or if the answering ring cannot be distinguished from direct attention, this direction fails. The deletion condition is recorded in `studies/webgpu/v013/README.md` and the register.

## Cultural translation

- Reference: `little-critters` — `https://github.com/GordenSun/little-critters`
- Observed mechanism: situated agents respond to pointer proximity through attention and looking back, making the visitor part of the encounter.
- Mutine translation: a population of angular marks remembers a visitor look as direct turning, an answering ring, and a shared decision.
- Refused surface vocabulary: animals, paper scene, head-turning characters, character design, palette, composition, and surface style.
- Direction consequence: closes the WebGPU route-bundle grammar of v009–v012 and opens social attention as a distinct direction.

## Critique and hold

Accepted structural critique: the work makes reciprocal looking a real engine, not a cosmetic attention marker; the crowd changes occupied positions and orientation, and the latest look is exactly reversible.

Unresolved doubt: independent caption-free perceptual comparison remains incomplete. The key question is whether a viewer can see direct turning → answering ring → shared slant in the field itself with witnesses, labels, readout, and prose hidden. The candidate remains **held** until that perceptual doubt is resolved. Production verification is still pending.
