## Artistic gate

### Repeated grammar refused

The last three Self portrait records (v011–v013) used a continuous face contour with route-like events: braid, threshold, and inward fold. Their repeated gesture was passage through a stable portrait, with memory carried as altered paths and downstream exits.

### Structural and perceptual rupture

v014 replaces the continuous route field with a discrete 5 × 5 plate surface. A remembered look recruits one plate to turn toward the visitor, a distant plate answers from another relation, and a third plate vacates so the portrait carries an actual absence. The displaced shard remains outside its former slot; lifting the latest witness rebuilds the preceding mosaic exactly.

The rupture changes representation, composition, interaction, temporal memory, and viewer task: the visitor no longer follows a route through a face but causes one part of the self to alter another part and leave a missing place. The blind falsifier remains active: if the result reads as a regular decorative grid, the reciprocal event is not legible.

### Falsifier and deletion condition

Hide labels, readout, controls, shard witnesses, captions, and prose. The direction fails if the looking plate, answering plate, and true vacancy cannot be found in the polygon geometry itself, or if the vacancy is only a dark sticker. Delete v014 rather than polish it if caption-free comparison confirms the old route grammar remains the dominant reading.
