## Artistic gate

### Repeated grammar refused

The last three Self portrait records (v011–v013) used a continuous face contour with route-like events: braid, threshold, and inward fold. Their repeated gesture was passage through a stable portrait, with memory carried as altered paths and downstream exits.

### Structural and perceptual rupture

v014 replaces the continuous route field with a discrete 5 × 5 plate surface. A remembered look recruits one plate to turn toward the visitor, a distant plate answers from another relation, and a third plate vacates so the portrait carries an actual absence. The displaced shard remains outside its former slot; lifting the latest witness rebuilds the preceding mosaic exactly.

The rupture changes representation, composition, interaction, temporal memory, and viewer task: the visitor no longer follows a route through a face but causes one part of the self to alter another part and leave a missing place. The blind falsifier remains active: if the result reads as a regular decorative grid, the reciprocal event is not legible.

### Falsifier and deletion condition

Hide labels, readout, controls, shard witnesses, captions, and prose. The direction fails if the looking plate, answering plate, and true vacancy cannot be found in the polygon geometry itself, or if the vacancy is only a dark sticker. Delete v014 rather than polish it if caption-free comparison confirms the old route grammar remains the dominant reading.

### Repeated grammar refused

The last three Brush works (v014–v016) were a horizontal wet-route field, a basin field, and then a singular tiled pressure plate. Their repeated gesture was passage or incision through a continuous or gridded surface. v017 changes representation to thirteen isolated material islands with open negative space and changes the visitor task from drawing/cutting to approaching a situated field.

### Changed rule and visible consequence

A remembered proximity becomes a three-body material transfer: the nearest island yields pigment and opens a porous notch, a distant island receives and grows, and a third island hollows out. In the blind first encounter the work is a sparse archipelago, not a route bundle or pressure plate. Pointer proximity, click, Enter, and Space change island radius, mass, porosity, grain, edge, and tilt; the latest lift rebuilds the exact prior field.

### Falsifier and deletion condition

With labels, readout, controls, relation marks, and prose hidden, the work fails if it reads as generic decorative blobs, if source/receiver/vacancy are not legible in the island geometry, if receiver growth is not visible, or if the vacancy is only a dark sticker. Delete v017 rather than polishing it if caption-free comparison confirms v016's pressure-plate or an earlier route grammar remains dominant.

## Cultural translation

- Reference: `little-critters` — https://github.com/GordenSun/little-critters
- Observed mechanism: situated agents respond to pointer proximity by changing attention and looking back, making the visitor part of a reciprocal encounter rather than a dashboard operator.
- Mutine translation: remove agents, eyes, and social scene; make proximity redistribute actual painted material across three situated islands: yield, receive, open.
- Visible consequence: the source loses radius and sediment and gains porosity; the receiver grows and gains sediment; a third island becomes porous.
