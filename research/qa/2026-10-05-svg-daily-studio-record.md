## Changed rule

v023 breaks v020's singular compound contour, v021's pressure ribbon, and v022's six-face folding object. One monolithic even-odd SVG sheet holds five negative chambers. A press-and-hold above 520ms seals the held chamber, opens a non-local chamber, and shifts a third as a bridge. The altered topology is inherited by later dryings; Delete lifts the latest drying and `R` releases the sheet.

## Radical progression

- Repeated prior gesture: a visitor selects, travels, or departs around line/path/facet geometry and the work answers through a non-local shape change.
- Structural rupture: switch from positive paths/faces to one material sheet whose negative chambers carry the image; switch from movement/selection/departure to dwell duration as the committing input; switch from edge/width/plane memory to topology memory.
- First-render consequence: a heavy warm sheet with five punched voids, not a ribbon, contour, faceted body, or route diagram.
- Falsifier: if dwell changes only a timer/color/cursor, if the held and remote silhouettes do not change, if a tap/short hold commits, or if later holds ignore changed voids, the translation fails.
- Deletion condition: delete v023 if the blind sheet reads as a decorated plate rather than material, if sealing/opening is not legible in the voids, or if lifting the latest drying does not restore the exact prior signature.

## Cultural translation

- **Reference:** [p5.brush](https://github.com/acamposuribe/p5.brush)
- **Observed mechanism:** programmable pressure, density, grain, and fields change how drawing geometry is produced and remembered rather than decorating finished geometry.
- **Mutine translation:** refuse brushes, watercolor, hatching, and API surface. Translate material resistance into a 520ms dwell threshold in Pure SVG: duration seals one negative chamber, opens another elsewhere, and shifts a third; that topology becomes memory for the next hold.
- **Visible consequence:** the event is carried by actual compound-path void silhouettes, with no cursor trace or overlay as the causal carrier.
- **Anti-copy:** no source brush, palette, composition, surface style, character, or API vocabulary is reproduced.
- **Direction consequence:** closes the line-like pressure/faceted sequence of v019–v022 and opens topology-as-material.
