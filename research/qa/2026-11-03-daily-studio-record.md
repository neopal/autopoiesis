## Hypothesis and radical progression

If a naive model treats several acts of attention as evidence of a shared object, then a three-witness quorum should not repair any witness. It should invent a new centre from their relation: a contour that was never observed.

The last three Naive works were v042's WebGL wrong volume, v041's browser-native wrong sentence, and v040's Canvas pressure-cast relief. ## Changed rule

One attended witness is only evidence. Two are insufficient. Only three distinct witnesses can form a quorum, and the naive model spends that quorum by inventing a new nested contour in the unobserved centre. Pending attention is geometry-neutral; the witnesses never deform.

then a grain-aligned trace can spend resistance in a remote facet. The trace
stays geometrically quiet while incomplete or misaligned; a valid passage
leaves its touched cell exact and changes how an untouched cell is made.

The viewer does not select a witness, wait on a site, or close a circuit. A
pointer/touch trace is inert until its span crosses `0.58` and its local grain
alignment crosses `0.62`. Only then does a `resistance-transfer` event commit.
The touched cell remains exact; an untouched remote cell gains actual
triangulation, depth, warp, and ridge geometry. Four transfers compound and can
be lifted in reverse order.
