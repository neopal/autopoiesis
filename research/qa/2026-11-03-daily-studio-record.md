## Hypothesis and radical progression

If a naive model treats several acts of attention as evidence of a shared object, then a three-witness quorum should not repair any witness. It should invent a new centre from their relation: a contour that was never observed.

The last three Naive works were v042's WebGL wrong volume, v041's browser-native wrong sentence, and v040's Canvas pressure-cast relief. Their repeated gesture was local evidence being displaced into an existing remote object or role. v043 changes representation, composition, encounter, and memory: a Canvas 2D contour constellation replaces volume, sentence, and relief; the viewer selects three distinct witnesses rather than scanning, reversing, or pressing; commitment produces a new central object instead of mutating the evidence. The selected witnesses remain exact. Four false centres compound and can be lifted.

## Changed rule

One attended witness is only evidence. Two are insufficient. Only three distinct witnesses can form a quorum, and the naive model spends that quorum by inventing a new nested contour in the unobserved centre. Pending attention is geometry-neutral; the witnesses never deform.
