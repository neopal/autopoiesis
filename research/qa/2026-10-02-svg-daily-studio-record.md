## Artist hypothesis and rupture

If a formal constraint is made visible as a changed contour and a new negative room, a small SVG can behave like a strange animal without drawing an animal or arranging a collection of plates.

The last three Pure SVG records repeated a local/remote topology gesture: v017 relayed a wrong return through a loop, v018 transferred a negative cut between disjoint plates, and v019 changed the front/back order of a radial pressure valve. v020 breaks that family on three visible axes:

- **Representation:** several path families / radial stack → one large closed compound contour with even-odd subpaths.
- **Viewer task:** pointer attention or global pressure → choose a named formal clause.
- **Visible grammar:** network, plates, or radial lock → a pale-ground singular silhouette whose non-local answer is a negative pocket.

A chosen clause bends an actual outer contour vertex and creates a new actual pocket elsewhere in the same compound path. The last four constraints are replayable; lifting the latest restores the preceding geometry signature.

**Falsifier:** if the clause only changes a marker, if the pocket is an overlay rather than path topology, if the selected vertex does not bend, if pointer taps commit, or if the relation requires the caption, the translation fails.

**Deletion condition:** delete v020 rather than polish it if the blind image cannot be distinguished from v019's radial valve or v018's disjoint plates, or if latest-rule lifting fails to restore the exact signature.

## Cultural translation

- **Reference:** [little-critters](https://github.com/GordenSun/little-critters)
- **Observed mechanism:** situated code-drawn agents respond to proximity by changing attention and looking back, making the visitor part of a reciprocal relation rather than a controller of a dashboard.
- **Mutine rule:** remove the animals and look-back scene. In Pure SVG, a selected clause becomes a situated formal witness: one actual contour vertex bends while a displaced clause receives a new even-odd pocket.
- **Visible consequence:** one irregular compound contour becomes structurally different at two separated places: a changed silhouette and a real negative room.
- **Anti-copy:** no animals, eyes, characters, paper scene, source palette, source composition, or head-turning surface vocabulary is reproduced.
- **Direction consequence:** closes the remote relay, disjoint negative alphabet, and radial pressure valve family; opens a constraint-and-topology direction where one body carries a non-local rule.

## Critique

- **Contour critic:** the material rule lives in one actual compound path, not a badge or overlay.
- **Perceptual critic:** labels, controls, readout, annotations, and prose must not be needed to read a singular compound contour and its changed negative room.
- **Interaction critic:** pointer movement/taps are refused; clause selection plus control, Enter, Space, and C are equivalent; Delete restores; R releases.
- **Radical progression critic:** the medium/composition switch is visible in code, interaction, and resulting topology.
- **Cynic:** delete if the blind image reads as an annotated diagram or if the contour/pocket relation is only explained by the caption.
