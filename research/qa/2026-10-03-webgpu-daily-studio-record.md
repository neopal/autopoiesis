## Creative direction

The last three WebGPU records repeat a pressure-bearing field grammar: v017 is a temporal score with discrete advance, v018 is a radial witness field with sustained attention, and v020 is one oblique sheet with wheel-selected pressure. Their recurring gestures are local selection followed by a non-local geometric reply, bounded memory, and a lift/release reversal.

v021 makes a material rupture rather than another field refinement:

- **Representation:** line/band/sheet field → a three-layer lattice of 180 solid nodes with actual lift, tilt, twist, activity, and withheld geometry.
- **Encounter:** wheel selection, sustained attention, and discrete advance → pointer motion arms a place without writing; keys `1–4` tune a carrier before a call is committed.
- **Memory:** fold, aperture, and delayed cohort → local phase recognition, withheld neighbours, a distant layer return, and accumulated carrier resistance.
- **Blind visual grammar:** suspended block lattice / depth / reciprocal bridge, not a route bundle, radial constellation, score, or pressure sheet.

**Hypothesis:** a tuned call can make a code-drawn field reciprocal without depicting animals or copying their scene: some situated places should answer, some should withhold material, and a distant layer should return the signal as changed geometry.

## Cultural translation

- **Reference:** [little-critters](https://github.com/GordenSun/little-critters)
- **Observed mechanism:** situated code-drawn agents respond to pointer proximity by changing attention and looking back, making the visitor part of a reciprocal relation rather than a controller of a dashboard.
- **Mutine translation:** remove the animals and look-back scene. Treat each lattice node as a situated listener with an embedded phase. A carrier call is recognized by some local places, refused by others, and returned by a non-adjacent layer.
- **Visible consequence:** a committed call changes actual node lift, tilt, twist, activity, vacancy, and a distant bridge. Repeated calls meet resistance and change later geometry.
- **Falsifier:** if pointer motion or a tap writes history, carrier tuning only recolours the field, withheld nodes remain occupied, the remote bridge is decorative, or the blind view needs the caption to become reciprocal, the translation fails.
- **Anti-copy:** no animals, eyes, character designs, paper scene, source palette, source composition, source API, or head-turning surface vocabulary is reproduced.
- **Direction consequence:** closes the recent witness / sheet / score family and opens signal-and-ecology computation for the WebGPU current.
