## Direction

- **Last-three repetition named before coding:** v047's proof strip/correction drag, v046's suspended vessel/stillness hold, and v045's pressure slab/dwell-return all let a local gesture produce a remote or displaced error while the attended source stayed exact.
- **Changed rule:** After three distinct directional witnesses turn toward the visitor, look-back stores the stale direction of attention and reorients the entire field, including the visited witnesses. This deliberately inverts the recent source-preservation invariant.
- **Perceptual rupture:** A distributed Canvas ecology of fifteen abstract notched witnesses replaces the horizontal proof ledger, suspended vessel, and singular pressure slab; reciprocal proximity replaces drag/hold; global stale consensus replaces a remote target mutation.

## Cultural translation

- **Reference:** [little-critters](https://github.com/GordenSun/little-critters)
- **Observed mechanism:** Situated code-drawn agents make proximity legible as reciprocal attention; nearby agents turn toward the visitor and answer without a dashboard.
- **Mutine translation:** Animals, eyes, character design, paper scene, source palette, composition, code, examples, and API are refused. Reciprocal looking becomes abstract witnesses whose bodies face the visitor; three distinct notices license a stale-direction backcast through the whole field.
- **Visible consequence:** The blind view is a dark indigo field of fifteen folded directional bodies. A valid look-back changes every witness's orientation, notch, scale, stance, and position and adds a seam of false consensus; four backcasts persist.
- **Falsifier:** Delete if proximity is only a cursor effect, fewer than three distinct witnesses commit, repeated attention counts twice, the backcast only recolours a fixed image, the whole field does not change, memory resets, lifting is inexact, or the blind field reads as a generic flock/dashboard.
- **Anti-copy:** No animals, eyes, head-turning scene, character design, paper style, source palette, source composition, source code, examples, API surface, or source imagery is reproduced.
- **Direction consequence:** Closes Naive's remote misprint / suspended echo / pressure-membrane sequence and opens social misrecognition as global stale state.
