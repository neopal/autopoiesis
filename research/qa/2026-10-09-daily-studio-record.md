## Creative direction

The last three Self portrait works were a branching atlas (v022), a single observed solid (v023), and a centered self-measuring membrane (v024). v025 makes a perceptual and encounter rupture: it leaves the centered object grammar for one wide asymmetrical decision ribbon, and replaces binary choice, orbit-and-seal, and draw-release with traversal whose exit is the causal event.

The new rule is situated departure. Thirteen uneven segments share one spine. Pointer or touch proximity only accumulates attention. When the visitor leaves, the portrait chooses a non-local segment to open into a real gap and makes another remote segment bend in reply. Four departures remain in bounded memory; lifting the latest reconstructs the exact preceding ribbon.

## Cultural translation

- **Reference:** [little-critters](https://github.com/GordenSun/little-critters)
- **Observed mechanism:** Situated code-drawn agents respond to pointer proximity through changed attention and reciprocal looking; the visitor becomes part of a relation rather than a dashboard operator.
- **Mutine translation:** Refuse animals, eyes, and head-turning scenes. Translate situated reciprocity into a decision-ribbon law: the visitor's route accumulates local attention, but departure makes an unaddressed segment detour and a remote segment reply.
- **Visible consequence:** The blind field is an asymmetrical connected ribbon, not a face or population. After departure, an actual cut opens away from the directly observed area and a remote segment bends; later departures inherit bounded memory.
- **Falsifier:** If proximity changes geometry immediately, if departure always opens the same segment, if the reply is only a colour or overlay, if later departures ignore memory, or if the blind view reads as a generic chart, card array, or decorative line score, the translation fails.
- **Anti-copy:** No animals, character designs, paper scene, source palette, composition, API surface, or head-turning image is reproduced.
- **Direction consequence:** Closes Self portrait's centered object, branch, and membrane sequence. It opens a situated-departure direction in which the portrait remembers the visitor's route as an externalised detour rather than a direct target.
