## Concept and progression gate

Pure SVG v022 is a direction change, not a polish pass. The last three SVG records repeated a line-like pressure grammar: v019 was a radial occlusion valve, v020 a singular negative contour, and v021 a pressure ribbon. v022 changes representation, composition, encounter, and temporal rule at once:

- **Representation:** six solid quadrilateral faces form one faceted folded object with seams and depth shadow.
- **Encounter:** pointer proximity arms a face but does not write; leaving the field commits. A pointer tap is refused.
- **Memory:** the approached face turns away, a non-adjacent face answers through changed coordinates, and a hinge face shifts. Later edge exchanges inherit the bounded memory.

This is the concrete rupture: from path/ribbon/occlusion operations to a face-based object whose adjacency changes under approach/departure.

## Changed rule

A visitor can approach one face but cannot possess it. On departure, the nearest edge withdraws, a non-adjacent face inherits the changed plane, and the hinge between them shifts. The event is embodied in actual SVG path coordinates, not in a cursor, marker, counter, or caption. `Delete` restores the exact preceding object; `R` releases it.

## Cultural translation

- **Reference:** [little-critters](https://github.com/GordenSun/little-critters)
- **Observed mechanism:** Situated code-drawn agents respond to pointer proximity by changing attention and looking back, making the visitor part of a reciprocal encounter rather than a controller of a dashboard.
- **Mutine translation:** Refuse animals, eyes, head-turning imagery, paper scene, source palette, composition, and API. Translate reciprocal attention into topology: nearness arms one face, departure turns its edge away, and a non-adjacent face answers by inheriting the altered plane.
- **Visible consequence:** The blind field is one faceted SVG body. After departure, the approached face has a moved silhouette and a distant face has a different lean; the response is geometric rather than character animation.
- **Falsifier:** Proximity or a tap writes; the remote answer is only a glow; the remote face does not move; or the blind field reads as a polygon diagram.
- **Anti-copy:** No animals, eyes, character design, paper scene, source palette, composition, surface style, or head-turning vocabulary is reproduced.
- **Direction consequence:** Closes the line-like pressure family of v019–v021 and opens reciprocal topology: Pure SVG tests attention as an exchange of planes inside one faceted body.
