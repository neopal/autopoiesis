## Artistic hypothesis

The previous three WebGPU works (v046 folded chorus, v047 absence arch, v048 pressure shell) repeat a surface/object grammar: a spatial body receives a directed event and carries the consequence through a route or architectural remainder. v049 makes a material rupture: it leaves 3D surfaces, ports, crowds, and route travel for a flat negative-space field of twenty-four unequal apertures. The viewer's task also changes from travelling across a body to attending, withdrawing, and allowing the watched site to become unavailable.

- Changed rule: attention is provisional while held; only withdrawal toward the deterministic returned witness rewrites every aperture, and the watched witness becomes the next absence.
- Visible consequence: a valid return changes every aperture's gap, aperture, echo, and attention values, draws a new counter-axis, and closes the watched site into an actual dark absence. Four returns compound.
- Falsifier: delete if the gesture only moves a cursor, only one aperture changes, return order is cosmetic, memory resets, lifting is inexact, or the blind field reads as a decorative constellation or generic particle demo.
- Deletion condition: delete v049 if labels-off viewing cannot distinguish reciprocal absence from v046-v048 or if the return rule is only legible through prose.
- Interaction: pointer/touch attend and withdraw; Enter/Space deterministic return; Delete/Backspace lift; Escape/R release.
- Reproducibility: seed `0x57473049`; pure `engine.mjs`; four-event memory; exact geometry signature and lift replay.

## Cultural field translation

- Reference: [`little-critters`](https://github.com/GordenSun/little-critters)
- Observed mechanism: situated agents use local proximity to make attention legible; they turn toward the visitor and reciprocal looking changes the encounter without a dashboard.
- Mutine translation: refuse animals and faces. Convert reciprocal looking into collective negative-space memory: attention is held without immediate geometry change, then withdrawal toward a returned witness re-produces the whole field and turns the watched entry into an absence.
- Anti-copy: no animals, faces, paper style, head-turning scene, source composition, source code, examples, API surface, or source imagery is reproduced.
- Direction consequence: closes the material-topology sequence and opens negative-space memory, testing whether WebGPU's archive question can be carried by absent openings without returning to agents or mesh.
