## Artist gate

- **Hypothesis:** a self-image can expose its decisions through a contour that folds through itself, rather than by printing a stable face twice.
- **Changed rule:** a remembered decision becomes an occlusion fold. Later contour points bend across a local crease, disappear beneath the fold, and return as an inner route; the aperture is carried through the same fold field.
- **Visible consequence:** the body develops a crossing, crease route, and inner return. The visitor action changes contour geometry and aperture geometry, not just colour.
- **Falsifier:** if lifting the latest fold leaves the folded contour, crease route, or aperture unchanged, the direction is decorative and fails.
- **Deletion condition:** delete v006 if a caption-free pair cannot identify the contour crossing and inner return, or if the crease guide alone carries the meaning.
- **Deterministic state:** `SEED = 0x53505636`, 12 stages, 48 contour points, 4-fold memory window, 14 points per crease route.
