02

the studio contract

Every pulse chooses one bounded act: make, test, criticise, simplify, delete, or document. A new version needs a changed rule, an observed consequence, a falsifier, and a trace.

No tableau is invented for a dormant current. No interaction is kept because it makes a picture prettier. No score replaces disagreement.
