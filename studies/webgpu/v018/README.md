# The field keeps the wrong witness.

## Hypothesis

A crowd can become an archive without adding more bodies: if sustained attention makes one situated witness vacate its local form and a distant witness inherit the opening, the work should keep the wrong reply visible as spatial geometry.

## Changed rule

v018 abandons v015's cavity volume, v016's interlaced scaffold, and v017's broad temporal score. It changes the medium and encounter together: a radial field is drawn as a WebGPU background with an explicit geometry layer, and attention is no longer a drag or a discrete advance. Approach arms one local witness; a sustained hold commits a bounded wrong-witness event. The source cell loses an actual sector and a remote cell receives an actual aperture. Delete lifts only the latest event; release reconstructs the untouched field.

## Visible consequence

The blind first view is an irregular radial constellation around a central absence, not a wall, band score, or wireframe. After a sustained witness, one local body has a real vacancy while a distant body has a cut aperture. The relation is carried by the cells and their geometry, not by a counter or caption.

## Cultural translation

- **Reference:** [little-critters](https://github.com/GordenSun/little-critters)
- **Observed mechanism:** situated agents respond to proximity by changing attention and looking back, making the visitor part of a reciprocal relation rather than a controller of a dashboard.
- **Mutine translation:** remove the agents and refuse the look-back. Proximity arms a local witness; sustained attention makes it vacate a real sector, while a distant witness answers with a real aperture.
- **Visible consequence:** the field changes from an unbroken radial constellation to a local vacancy plus a remote aperture; the wrong reply is visible before the explanation is read.
- **Anti-copy statement:** no animals, characters, eyes, paper style, head-turning scene, source palette, composition, or surface vocabulary from little-critters is reproduced.
- **Direction consequence:** closes WebGPU v015's cavity, v016's topological scaffold, and v017's temporal score; opens a GPU-native spatial witness direction in which the crowd remembers attention as a distributed absence.

## Falsifier

Delete the trial if proximity alone changes memory or geometry, if a short tap commits, if the local vacancy is only a tint or overlay, if the distant aperture is only an accent, or if the blind view reads as a decorative constellation rather than a field with structural openings.

## Deletion condition

If two independent labels-off comparisons cannot distinguish the local vacancy and remote aperture from decorative marks, delete v018 and abandon this spatial-witness direction instead of polishing the controls or caption.

## Evidence method

The engine is deterministic from `SEED`, replays a four-event memory window, records source vacancies and remote apertures, and exposes a geometry signature for exact reversal. The tableau requests a WebGPU device and renders a WGSL radial background when available; the Canvas geometry layer is the deterministic fallback and remains the source of the causal shapes on machines without WebGPU.
