# The archive contracts around a missed signal.

## Hypothesis

Can a crowd become an archive without becoming a body, register, or fluid volume? If the visitor draws a cut across a braided pressure field, the mark should not remain. The field should keep the event as redistributed tension: a remote knot, a counter-knot, and a silent gap. The archive is the changed material relation, not the gesture trace.

## Changed rule

v029 abandons v028's single cavity monolith, v027's watershed basins, and v026's interrupted typographic register. The work becomes a three-strand braided field of eighteen suspended fibers. A pointer traverse only arms a cut; release is causal. A valid traverse is absorbed by the field and redistributed to three fibers that were not selected by pointer location: one knot compresses, one counter-knot slips against it, and one fiber becomes a material gap. Four redistributions remain in bounded memory; Delete lifts the latest and R or Escape releases the archive.

## Visible consequence

The blind first view is a diagonal braid of three differently colored, individually authored strands with actual pressure nodes, slips, and dark interruptions. The temporary cut disappears on release. After a valid traverse, a remote knot thickens, a counter-knot shears in the opposite direction, and a separate fiber opens a gap. Later traverses compound the changed braid rather than restarting from the quiet field.

## Falsifier

Delete the trial if pointer contact or a short cut commits, if the drawn trace remains as the work, if the pointer's entry location directly selects the visible knot, if the counter-knot or silent gap is only a glow/readout, if later cuts restart from the quiet braid, if Delete cannot restore the exact preceding geometry, or if the blind field reads as a generic line bundle, graph, or decorative particle system.

## Deletion condition

After two independent labels-off, readout-off, controls-off, and caption-off comparisons, delete v029 if viewers cannot identify one braided field, a disappearing cut, and three material consequences after one valid traverse, or if the changed field is indistinguishable from a static decorative waveform. Do not rescue a failed pressure rule with labels, counters, or a persistent gesture trail.
