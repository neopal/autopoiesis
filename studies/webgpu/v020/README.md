# The mass stores a crease.

## Hypothesis

A crowd can become an archive without remaining a crowd: if pressure enters one continuous sheet, opens a real fold, and transfers sealing load to a distant slice, the material should remember its own resistance rather than merely animate a surface.

## Changed rule

v020 abandons v016's adjacency scaffold, v017's temporal strata, and v018's situated witness field. It changes both representation and encounter: nine oblique slices form one continuous material sheet, and the visitor must wheel to choose a depth slice before committing a pressure pulse. The selected slice splits into an actual gap and fold; a non-adjacent slice seals; later pulses meet the binder load already accumulated in the sheet. Delete lifts the latest pulse; release reconstructs the untouched sheet.

## Visible consequence

The blind first view is a single oblique sheet with depth, not a radial constellation, band score, or wireframe crowd. After a pulse, the selected slice has a readable missing interval and changed fold, while a distant slice visibly closes and later pulses fold with a different resistance. The evidence lives in the sheet geometry, not in a counter or caption.

## Cultural translation

- **Reference:** [p5.brush](https://github.com/acamposuribe/p5.brush)
- **Observed mechanism:** pressure, density, grain, and directional fields operate as material forces that alter how geometry is produced rather than decorating completed geometry.
- **Mutine translation:** refuse brush appearance and translate material force into a GPU-ready sheet law: a selected pressure pulse splits one depth slice, seals a distant slice, and adds binder resistance to the next pulse.
- **Visible consequence:** one continuous sheet becomes a local fold plus a non-local seal; repeated pulses visibly change the later fold instead of repeating the first event.
- **Anti-copy statement:** no p5.brush brushes, watercolor appearance, hatching vocabulary, palette, composition, API surface, source examples, or surface style is reproduced.
- **Direction consequence:** closes the WebGPU current's social-attention and temporal-strata family; opens a material-computation direction where the crowd becomes one pressure-bearing medium.

## Falsifier

Delete the trial if wheel selection writes history, if a pointer tap commits, if the fold or gap is an overlay, if the distant seal is only a colour accent, if the second pulse behaves like the first, or if the blind view reads as a decorated stack rather than one material sheet.

## Deletion condition

If two independent labels-off comparisons cannot distinguish the selected fold, the distant seal, and the later resistance from decorative marks, delete v020 and abandon this sheet direction instead of polishing the controls or explanation.

## Evidence method

The engine is deterministic from `SEED`, replays a four-event memory window, stores a geometry signature, and rebuilds the exact preceding state on lift. The tableau requests a WebGPU device and renders a WGSL material field when available; the Canvas geometry layer is the deterministic fallback and remains the source of the causal sheet shapes on machines without WebGPU.
