# The field learns a second horizon.

## Hypothesis

Can a crowd become an archive without remaining a crowd of visible bodies? If the social mechanism is translated into a continuous spatial computation, a viewer should not receive a private answer at the point they touch. A directional relation should travel through the whole terrain: each local patch inherits from its neighbour, and the surface remembers that inheritance as relief and phase.

## Changed rule

v033 abandons v032's cellular quorum membrane, v031's granular pressure body, and v030's exposure shutters. The representation switches to one continuous 18×12 spatial terrain rendered as folded height, phase, depth, and contour. A bounded drag is a directional offer: arming and moving leave geometry exact, while release commits a propagation beat. Every sample copies a neighbour along the drag direction; the endpoint is refused as a privileged answer. Four inherited beats remain liftable.

## Visible consequence

The blind first view is a continuous topographic surface with an off-axis ridge, a shadowed basin, and contour layers—not cells, grains, shutters, or a wireframe grid. A valid sweep changes actual sample heights, phase, fold, depth, and inheritance throughout the terrain. Later beats rebuild from the altered surface rather than restarting from a quiet seed.

## Cultural translation

- **Reference:** [little-critters](https://github.com/GordenSun/little-critters)
- **Observed mechanism:** situated agents respond to proximity with legible reciprocal attention; local behaviour makes the visitor part of a social field without a dashboard.
- **Mutine translation:** refuse animals, eyes, characters, paper, and head-turning. Translate reciprocity into a local-neighbour propagation rule: a sweep sets a direction but not a target, and each place inherits from the adjacent place when the direction is released.
- **Visible consequence:** the viewer's endpoint does not become a highlighted answer. The continuous terrain acquires a traveling crease, changed phase, and inherited relief across many local samples; subsequent sweeps use that altered terrain.
- **Anti-copy statement:** no animals, character designs, paper scene, head-turning image, source palette, source composition, source code, examples, API surface, or surface vocabulary is reproduced.
- **Direction consequence:** closes WebGPU's exposure / granular / quorum family and opens a continuous spatial-computation direction. The current moves from distributed bodies to a single field, and from witness/hold/click to a directional sweep whose effect is local propagation rather than remote election.

## Falsifier

Delete the trial if arming or moving changes geometry, if the endpoint directly selects the answer, if the terrain reads as a generic wireframe or particle display, if neighbour inheritance exists only in colour or readout, if later beats reset from the seed, if Delete cannot restore the exact preceding terrain, or if the blind image needs its caption to become meaningful.

## Deletion condition

After two labels-off, readout-off, controls-off, and caption-off comparisons, delete v033 if viewers cannot identify one continuous terrain and a materially different directional propagation, or if the work is indistinguishable from a decorative mesh demo. Do not rescue a failed causal rule with labels, counters, or a cursor trail.

## Evidence method

The engine is deterministic from `SEED`, reconstructs all samples from a bounded four-beat archive, stores an exact geometry signature, and rebuilds the preceding terrain on lift. The tableau requests a WebGPU device and submits a WGSL veil when available; the Canvas 2D renderer remains the deterministic causal surface on machines without WebGPU. Reduced-motion and static states use a four-beat settlement so blind capture exposes inherited terrain without an animation race.
