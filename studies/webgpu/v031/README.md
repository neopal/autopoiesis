# The mass remembers the pressure that failed.

## Hypothesis

Can a crowd become an archive without becoming a room, braid, cavity, or register? If a visitor presses one grain and holds it, the material should stay still. Release should make the crowd remember pressure indirectly: a remote grain hollows out, another grain merges, and a fourth body slips in depth. The archive is a changed mass, not a retained gesture.

## Changed rule

v031 abandons v030's exposure shutters, v029's braided pressure field, and v028's negative-space monolith. The representation switches to a suspended granular body of thirty-six irregular grains with depth, mass, and scale. Pointer movement is only an approach. A pressure hold is geometry-neutral. Release is causal: the pressed grain collapses, a remote grain becomes a real hollow, a different grain merges, and a fourth grain receives the depth echo. Four material events remain liftable; Delete restores the exact preceding mass and R or Escape releases the archive.

## Visible consequence

The blind first view is a broad, off-centre cloud of luminous irregular grains with small bodies, hollows, and a few merged bodies. A short press leaves no deformation. After a bounded hold and release, one grain loses mass, a non-local grain opens as a dark absence, another swells, and a remote grain shifts out of plane. Later releases compound the altered mass rather than restarting from quiet.

## Cultural translation

- **Reference:** [p5.brush](https://github.com/acamposuribe/p5.brush)
- **Observed mechanism:** programmable pressure, density, grain, and directional fields participate in producing geometry rather than decorating an already finished mark.
- **Mutine translation:** refuse brushes, watercolor, hatching, grain texture, and the source API. Translate material agency into a granular crowd whose pressure is latent until release, then changes the ontology of remote bodies: absence, merger, and depth are produced by the event.
- **Visible consequence:** a hold does not deform the field; release changes actual mass, radius, and depth values in non-local grains, and later compressions inherit those values.
- **Anti-copy statement:** no brush tool, watercolor appearance, hatching vocabulary, source palette, source composition, source code, example, or API surface is reproduced.
- **Direction consequence:** closes WebGPU's exposure / braid / cavity sequence and opens a granular-material direction. It changes the medium grammar from planes and cavities to discrete bodies, and changes the viewer task from looking/leaving to holding/releasing pressure.

## Falsifier

Delete the trial if pointer motion deforms the mass, if a short press commits, if the pressed grain is the only visible consequence, if the remote hollow or merger exists only in a readout, if later pressure forgets earlier mass, if Delete cannot restore the exact preceding field, or if the blind image reads as a generic particle demo.

## Deletion condition

After two independent labels-off, readout-off, controls-off, and caption-off comparisons, delete v031 if viewers cannot identify one granular body, a neutral hold, and at least two non-local material consequences after one release, or if the work is indistinguishable from decorative particles. Do not rescue a failed pressure rule with counters, labels, or a cursor trail.

## Evidence method

The engine is deterministic from `SEED`, reconstructs all grains from a bounded four-event pressure archive, stores an exact geometry signature, and rebuilds the prior mass on lift. The tableau requests a WebGPU device and submits a WGSL veil when available; the Canvas 2D projection remains the deterministic causal renderer on machines without WebGPU. Reduced-motion and static states use a deterministic four-event settlement so blind capture exposes remembered mass without an animation race.
