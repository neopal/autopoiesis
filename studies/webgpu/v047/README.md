# WebGPU v047 — The crowd keeps an empty seat.

## Concept

An irregular arch of situated witnesses replaces v045's stratigraphic mass and
v046's folded chorus. It is not a crowd of visible characters. It is one broken
architectural body whose small produced slabs can turn toward a visitor, then
leave a real gap somewhere else.

## Hypothesis

If local attention is treated as a social production rule rather than as a
cursor effect, three distinct looks should be remembered as a remote defection.
The attended witnesses must turn in their actual geometry, nearby witnesses must
reorient, and a different slab must become absent. Repeating one witness or
ending the chain early must leave the arch exact. The absence must compound and
remain liftable.

## Changed rule

The visitor looks at three different witnesses. A complete chain is committed
only after three distinct, explicit acts of attention. The witnesses turn away
from the visitor, the surrounding arch subtly redistributes its lean, and a
remote witness leaves the structure. The source sites remain present; the
archive is carried by the absent site. Four defections compound. Delete lifts
one absence; Escape releases the whole room to the seeded quiet arch.

## Cultural translation

The source is [little-critters](https://github.com/GordenSun/little-critters),
whose observed mechanism is a population of situated agents whose proximity to
the pointer changes their attention and whose local behaviour makes a
reciprocal encounter legible. Mutine refuses animals, eyes, characters, paper,
source palette, source composition, source code, examples, and API surface. It
translates situated attention into a social refusal: being seen by three
witnesses is stored as a remote defection and a persistent absence.

## Visible consequence

The caption-free first view is a sparse, asymmetrical arch of copper, paper,
blue, and violet slabs over a dark field. It is neither a rectangular cell mass,
a diagonal score, nor a radial orbit. After a valid chain, the three visited
slabs visibly turn while a different position opens into a dark cut with a
small registration scar. Four cuts remain in one arch. The first view of the
settled archive therefore shows memory as missing structure, not as a label,
halo, or cursor trail.

## Interaction

- Pointer or touch: click three different slabs; each click is one explicit look.
- `Enter` / `Space`: perform the next deterministic three-witness chain.
- `Delete` / `Backspace`: lift the latest absence.
- `Escape` / `R`: release the room to the quiet seed.
- The three visible controls expose look, lift, and release without hover.

`?preview=1` removes editorial furniture. `?preview=1&static=1&blind=1` removes
readout, controls, status, hint, caption, and prose while retaining the settled
four-absence arch.

## Falsifier

Delete v047 if a repeated or incomplete look commits, if the source witness is
rewritten instead of turning as part of the chain, if the remote gap is only
colour or readout, if four absences do not compound, if lifting is inexact, or
if the blind image reads as a generic gate, diagram, or decorative crowd.

## Deletion condition

Delete this direction if caption-free viewing cannot distinguish the absence arch
from v044's aperture orbit, v045's stratigraphic mass, and v046's folded chorus,
or if social attention is only legible through the explanation.

## Direction consequence

This abandons WebGPU's recent additive grammars: pressure compacting a mass and
cadence folding a chorus. It opens a social-absence period in which the crowd's
archive is carried by what is no longer there. The next mutation must test
whether the missing site can influence a later chain without becoming a
reintroduced visual cursor.

## Medium and reproducibility

The executable tableau uses p5.js 1.11.3 Canvas 2D generated arch slabs, cut
scars, fine witness threads, and seeded ambient movement. `engine.mjs` is pure
and deterministic; `navigator.gpu` is reported only as a capability signal. The
same seed, witness order, and four-event memory produce the same absence
signature.
