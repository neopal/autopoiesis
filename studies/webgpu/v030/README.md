# The room keeps what you leave unseen.

## Hypothesis

Can a crowd become an archive without becoming a body, register, basin, braid, or fluid field? If attention is allowed to look without touching, the work should only change when the visitor leaves. The archive should reverse the social reflex: the most-watched shutter closes, a remote unlooked-for aperture opens, and a third plate tilts. The room keeps what the visitor did not stay with.

## Changed rule

v030 abandons v029's braided pressure field, v028's negative-space monolith, and v027's watershed volume. The medium switches to a layered exposure instrument: twelve oversized translucent shutters with actual apertures. Pointer proximity is observational and geometry-neutral. Only a departure after three distinct witnesses commits. The last watched plate closes, a least-loaded remote plate opens, and a third shutter tilts. Four exposure reversals remain liftable; Delete restores the exact prior shutter geometry and R or Escape releases the archive.

## Visible consequence

The blind first view is a suspended composition of irregular translucent plates, dark apertures, pale rims, and colored exposure halos. Moving across the room does not deform it. When the pointer leaves after three distinct witnesses, the watched plate constricts, a remote aperture widens, and another plate turns. Later departures compound the changed room rather than restarting from the seed.

## Cultural translation

- **Reference:** [little-critters](https://github.com/GordenSun/little-critters)
- **Observed mechanism:** situated code-drawn agents answer pointer proximity with legible attention and reciprocal looking; the visitor participates in a small social field without a dashboard.
- **Mutine translation:** refuse animals, eyes, characters, paper, and head-turning. Translate reciprocal attention into an exposure instrument: looking is only a temporary witness ledger, and departure makes the most-watched plate close while a remote unlooked-for aperture opens.
- **Visible consequence:** attention leaves geometry unchanged; departure changes actual shutter apertures and tilt, with later departures inheriting those cuts.
- **Anti-copy statement:** no animals, character designs, paper scene, head-turning image, source palette, source composition, source code, example, or API surface is reproduced.
- **Direction consequence:** closes WebGPU's braid / vacancy / basin sequence and opens a departure-and-exposure direction. The work changes both medium grammar and viewer task: from touching or traversing material to looking, withholding, and leaving.

## Falsifier

Delete the trial if pointer movement changes geometry, if fewer than three distinct witnesses can commit, if departure does not matter, if the watched plate is always the visible answer, if the remote aperture or tilt exists only in text/readout, if later departures forget prior exposure, if Delete cannot restore the exact preceding room, or if the blind image reads as a decorative panel array or interface.

## Deletion condition

After two independent labels-off, readout-off, controls-off, and caption-off comparisons, delete v030 if viewers cannot identify a singular exposure instrument, geometry-neutral looking, and at least two actual aperture consequences after one departure, or if the work is indistinguishable from a static dashboard of tiles. Do not rescue a failed departure rule with labels, counters, or a persistent cursor trace.

## Evidence method

The engine is deterministic from `SEED`, reconstructs all shutters from a bounded four-event exposure archive, stores an exact geometry signature, and rebuilds the prior room on lift. The tableau requests a WebGPU device and submits a WGSL veil when available; the Canvas 2D projection remains the deterministic causal renderer on machines without WebGPU. Reduced-motion and static states use a deterministic four-departure settlement so blind capture exposes remembered apertures without an animation race.
