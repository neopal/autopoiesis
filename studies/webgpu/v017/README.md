# The crowd misses its own beat.

## Hypothesis

A crowd can become an archive without adding bodies or drawing a brighter field: if one cohort is forced to arrive late, the work should keep both the missing beat and the delayed arrival visible as a temporal relation.

## Changed rule

v017 abandons v014's pressure surface, v015's cavity wall, and v016's interlaced scaffold. It also abandons drag as the causal gesture. One broad temporal score holds eight strata of nine cohorts. A discrete turn removes one actual cohort from its present band, places it later in the score, and tightens the intervening strata around the missing beat. The archive is a bounded stack of temporal deferrals. Pointer movement and short clicks are refused; Delete lifts only the latest turn; release reconstructs the untouched score.

## Visible consequence

The blind first view is a layered score of broad, irregular bands rather than points, a wall, or a wireframe. After a turn, a real slit remains in the source band, the cohort reappears in a later band, and a faint delay path makes the temporal displacement legible in the object rather than in a counter.

## Cultural translation

- **Reference:** little-critters — https://github.com/GordenSun/little-critters
- **Observed mechanism:** situated agents respond to proximity by changing attention and looking back, making the visitor part of a reciprocal relation.
- **Mutine translation:** refuse the look-back and translate situated attention into a turn-taking delay. One discrete quorum advance removes an actual cohort from the present band and lets it arrive later; the score remembers the missed beat as a gap.
- **Visible consequence:** the visitor does not steer a point or drag a graph. A committed turn changes the order and placement of a real band segment, and the gap it leaves remains visible.
- **Anti-copy statement:** no animals, characters, eyes, paper style, source palette, composition, or head-turning surface vocabulary is reproduced.
- **Direction consequence:** closes WebGPU v014's pressure-surface, v015's cavity, and v016's mutable-graph grammars; opens a temporal-computation direction where the crowd is archived as order, delay, and absence.

## Falsifier

Delete the trial if a short click or pointer movement changes the score, if the source gap is only a dark sticker, if the delayed cohort is only a glow or label, if the intervening bands do not tighten, or if the blind image still reads as v016's spatial scaffold.

## Deletion condition

If two independent labels-off comparisons cannot distinguish the source gap and delayed cohort from a decorative overlay, delete v017 and abandon the temporal-score direction rather than polishing the controls or explanation.

## Evidence method

The engine is deterministic from `SEED`, replays a four-event memory window, records removed and delayed cohort placements, and exposes a geometry signature for exact reversal. The browser tableau detects `navigator.gpu` for a future GPU path and renders a deterministic Canvas temporal fallback so the causal experiment remains observable on machines without WebGPU.
