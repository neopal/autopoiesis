# The quorum chooses a stranger.

## Hypothesis

Can a crowd become an archive without becoming a room, braid, cloud, or pressure mass? In a social field, an individual witness should not receive a private answer. Three situated cells must agree before the membrane changes, and their agreement must be spent on a remote cell that none of them touched. The archive is a distributed decision, not a trail of attention.

## Changed rule

v032 abandons v031's granular bodies, v030's exposure shutters, and v029's braided fibers. The representation switches to one cellular membrane: twenty-four interlocking chambers around a deliberate central absence. Pointer contact is a temporary witness and remains geometry-neutral. Only three distinct witnesses form a quorum; the quorum then changes three remote roles—a proxy chamber swells, a refusal chamber opens, and a bridge chamber reconnects. Four remote decisions remain liftable; Delete restores the exact preceding topology and R or Escape releases the archive.

## Visible consequence

The blind first view is a centered, asymmetrical membrane of nested polygonal chambers, taut links, and a quiet central aperture—not a field of particles or a stack of panels. One or two clicks only mark temporary witnesses. A third distinct witness makes a remote chamber become proxy, another open as a true void, and a third pull a bridge through the membrane. Later quorums inherit the altered topology.

## Falsifier

Delete the trial if a single witness changes geometry, if duplicate witnesses can vote twice, if the clicked cells are the only answer, if the remote proxy, void, or bridge exists only in a readout, if later quorums forget the changed topology, if Delete cannot restore the exact preceding membrane, or if the blind image reads as a generic polygon dashboard.

## Deletion condition

After two independent labels-off, readout-off, controls-off, and caption-off comparisons, delete v032 if viewers cannot identify one cellular membrane, neutral individual witnessing, and at least two remote topological consequences after one quorum, or if the work is indistinguishable from decorative tessellation.
