PURE SVG / V031 / DAILY WORK

The line mishears
the second touch.

Attention is not a command. Two distant offers are enough to make the line route through an unoffered witness, changing its actual articulation instead of adding a signal on top.

Candidate / held for caption-free perceptual critique. The structural test passes: a first attention is geometry-neutral, an adjacent offer is refused, a distant offer reroutes the line through an unoffered joint, memory is bounded, and lifting reconstructs the exact preceding line. Promotion depends on whether the blind work reads as a singular authored line rather than a graph, diagram, or interface.
