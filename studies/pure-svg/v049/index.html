creation

Eight separate SVG marks are arranged as four paired thresholds. The blank between them is not a panel or a target; it is the work's only shared space.

changed rule

A visitor must pass through a narrow corridor without touching either mark. The relation opens a real notch in both marks and produces a third absence.

progression

Move through a corridor, then leave the field. Arrow keys and Enter perform the same deterministic passage. Delete lifts one cut; Escape or R releases.

a third mark.

The visitor cannot choose a mark. Only a passage between two marks can make the empty relation become a new piece of the image.

Candidate / held for caption-free perceptual critique. The structural test passes: a corridor remains inert until a non-touching passage crosses it, same-side and collision paths refuse, paired masks open actual notches, four relation-cuts compound, and lifting replays the exact prior field. Promotion depends on whether the blind image reads as a strange economy of relations rather than a diagram of targets.
