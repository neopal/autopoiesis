A single SVG weight sits between two fixed banks. Its inner cut and six produced strata are the image; no route is drawn for the hand.

changed rule

Pressure is accepted only as a held pulse. The next pulse must come from the opposite bank; each accepted alternation produces a new cross-section of the block.

progression

Press and hold either bank, then release. A short pulse or same-side repeat is refused. Arrow keys, Space, and the control offer the same path.

PURE SVG / V046 / DAILY WORK

The weight is
made by waiting.

A material does not remember the hand as a mark. It changes the shape that can be produced next, but only when pressure alternates and stays long enough to pass through resistance.

Candidate / held for caption-free perceptual critique. v046 abandons the recent field of tiles, skins, and traversed bands. A singular SVG block receives an alternating held pulse: the fixed banks remain exact while its body, void, and internal layers become a new material cross-section. Four loads compound and lift exactly. Promotion depends on whether the blind block reads as a changed object rather than a pressure-control diagram.
