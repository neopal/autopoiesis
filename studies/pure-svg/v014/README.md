# Pure SVG / v014

## Status

**Candidate / held.** This is a real daily tableau, not a placeholder. It is the fourteenth mutation of the Pure SVG current and stays held until an independent caption-free comparison confirms that refusal and gap migration survive without witness marks.

## Creative record

- **Hypothesis:** if a strange animal is a social field, its next mutation can refuse the social contract entirely: the line can treat attention as an obstruction rather than a gaze to return.
- **Repeated gesture refused:** v011–v013 kept asking a bounded visitor event to travel through or be answered by a composed SVG field. v014 removes the reciprocal agent grammar and changes the viewer task from placing a gaze to closing a passage.
- **Changed rule:** a visitor constraint closes three neighbouring gates around the nearest point, moves a true gap to another corridor, and nudges one escape gate away. No gate looks back; the field changes by refusal and route breakage.
- **Visible consequence:** the first encounter is three horizontal corridors made from eighteen angular gates, not a radial field of cells. After a constraint, one local passage becomes a heavy wedge, one distant gate disappears, and the corridor signature changes.
- **Deterministic state:** `SEED = 0x53564744`; `STAGES = 14`; `PRIMITIVE_BUDGET = 18`; memory is capped to three constraints. Pointer input is bounded to `x: 0.08..0.92`, `y: 0.18..0.82`.
- **Interaction:** click or tap the field, press Enter/Space after focusing it, or use `place a constraint`. Delete/Backspace lifts the latest constraint; release returns to the seeded sequence.
- **Falsifier:** hide the constraint witnesses, labels, readout, and count. If a viewer cannot see a local passage close and a real gap migrate between corridors, the inversion fails.
- **Deletion condition:** delete v014 if the blocked gates, missing gate, or corridor signature remain unchanged after input, if the SVG still reads as a reciprocal gaze field, or if undo does not restore the exact prior topology.

## Cultural translation

- **Reference:** `little-critters` — situated code-drawn agents respond to pointer proximity by changing attention and looking back.
- **Observed mechanism:** local proximity produces reciprocal attention between a visitor and situated agents.
- **Mutine translation:** invert reciprocity. Pure SVG removes agents and eye-like response; visitor proximity becomes a topological obstruction that closes a local route, migrates a gap elsewhere, and refuses to answer with a look.
- **Visible consequence:** the encounter changes from a field that turns toward the visitor to three broken corridors whose actual gate geometry and occupancy change after a constraint.
- **Anti-copy statement:** no animals, characters, paper style, head-turning scene, palette, composition, or surface vocabulary from the source is reproduced.
- **Direction consequence:** closes the v013 reciprocal-attention experiment and opens `constraint-and-topology` plus `anti-instrument`: the next question is whether a viewer can read refusal as a structural event without a responding agent.
