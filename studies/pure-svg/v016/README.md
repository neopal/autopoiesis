# Pure SVG / v016

## Status

**Candidate / held.** This is a real daily tableau, not a placeholder. It is the sixteenth mutation of the Pure SVG current and stays held until an independent caption-free comparison confirms that the broken body and inherited echo survive without labels or readout.

## Creative record

- **Hypothesis:** if a strange animal can be one articulated body, being seen can make it give away a joint rather than turn toward the visitor.
- **Repeated gesture refused:** v013–v015 distributed cells, gates, or compound territories across a field. v016 compresses the encounter into one body with one contour grammar and a second, displaced articulation.
- **Changed rule:** pointer movement only arms a witness. A held gaze commits a bounded event: one actual body segment disappears, the nearest joint sacrifices width, and a distant joint inherits a crooked echo. Later gazes operate on the already changed body.
- **Visible consequence:** the blind first encounter is one continuous articulated organism, not a territory field, corridor, or void map. After a committed gaze, a real break opens in the body and an extra joint grows elsewhere.
- **Deterministic state:** `SEED = 0x53564746`; `STAGES = 18`; `PRIMITIVE_BUDGET = 12`; memory is capped to four held gazes; commit threshold is `0.65` seconds.
- **Interaction:** move to arm; pointer down/up commits only after the hold threshold; the witness button, Enter, and Space commit the same bounded event. Delete/Backspace lifts the latest event; R or release returns to the seeded sequence.
- **Falsifier:** hide the gaze ring, labels, readout, controls, and prose. If the viewer cannot find a real missing segment and a distant inherited articulation, the translation fails.
- **Deletion condition:** delete v016 if the body remains a continuous ribbon after a hold, if the echo is only a marker or colour change, if short taps commit like holds, or if undo does not restore the exact preceding geometry.

## Lineage

`pure-svg-v015` → v016: the work abandons distributed territories and void ownership for a single articulated body, and changes the viewer task from moving absence between bodies to sustaining a gaze until the body gives away a joint.

## Cultural translation

- **Reference:** `little-critters` — situated code-drawn agents respond to pointer proximity by changing attention and looking back.
- **Observed mechanism:** local proximity creates a reciprocal attention relation between visitor and situated agents.
- **Mutine translation:** remove the agents and refuse the look-back. A sustained visitor gaze makes one SVG body segment absent and lets a distant joint inherit the displaced articulation as a crooked echo.
- **Visible consequence:** the encounter switches from a field of bodies exchanging voids to one body that becomes less continuous and more double after attention.
- **Anti-copy statement:** no animals, characters, eyes, paper style, head-turning scene, source palette, composition, or surface vocabulary is reproduced.
- **Direction consequence:** closes the v013 reciprocal-field, v014 corridor, and v015 void-ownership grammars and opens a body/echo direction in which attention changes the organism's continuity rather than its location in a field.
