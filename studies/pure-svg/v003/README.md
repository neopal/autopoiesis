## Creative record

- **Hypothesis:** a shape can remember a refusal without keeping a wound; memory can be a topological change in what the body is attached to.
- **Changed rule:** a refused joint is inherited by every later limb whose anchor follows it. The renderer recomputes the limb's joint, knee, and foot as a detour instead of adding a scar on top of an unchanged body.
- **Visible consequence:** the pale mineral body is crossed by copper routes that kink away from their direct fall. At settled stages, all three possible limb routes can carry the detour, while the body remains a restrained, strange animal rather than a diagram.
- **Deterministic state:** `SEED = 0x53564733`; `STAGES = 10`; `PRIMITIVE_BUDGET = 16`; memory window is the five most recent refusals. A visitor refusal is bounded to the SVG field and uses `source: visitor-refusal`.
- **Interaction:** click or tap the field, press Enter/Space after focusing it, or use the named `refuse a joint` button. `remove refusal` recomputes the previous route; `let it continue` returns to the autonomous sequence.
- **Falsifier:** if a caption-free viewer sees only orange witness symbols and not a changed attachment or body weight, the direction fails.
- **Deletion condition:** delete this version if removing the latest refusal leaves the downstream joint/knee/foot routes unchanged, or if the route remains a decorative overlay in the settled comparison.
