# Pure SVG v018 — The figure keeps a negative alphabet.

## Hypothesis

A strange SVG animal can be made from disjoint plates if relation is carried by winding algebra rather than by a visible route or shared body.

## Changed rule

The previous Pure SVG works connected, articulated, or hosted their absences in a continuous topology. v018 refuses that invariant. Nine freestanding compound plates begin with an inner contour that is filled as one plate. Pointer proximity only arms one plate. A single committed attention turns the source into a notched plate and assigns a distant plate an actual `evenodd` compound-path cut. The visitor's local attention is therefore answered remotely by a change in SVG fill algebra.

## Visible consequence

The blind first view is a warm, disjoint figure made from nine angular plates, not a loop, corridor, articulated body, or field of connected cells. After one countermark, one plate has a real edge notch and a distant plate contains a true cut-out. Repeating the gesture accumulates at most four countermarks. Delete lifts only the latest mark and reconstructs the exact prior plate signature.

## Interaction

- Pointer proximity arms the nearest plate without writing memory.
- A short pointer commit, Enter, Space, or `countermark` commits one bounded remote-winding event.
- Delete, Backspace, or `lift latest` removes the latest event.
- `R` or `release plate` restores the empty plate field.
- `S` downloads the currently rendered inline SVG.

## Falsifier

The translation fails if the source notch is only a colour change, if the remote plate has no actual cut-out, if the viewer must read the caption to find the local/remote relation, or if the blind view still reads as a decorated tile board rather than a figure assembled from negative grammar.

## Deletion condition

Delete v018 rather than polish it if the caption-free blind comparison cannot distinguish disjoint winding plates from a decorative grid, or if the one-point interaction is only a selection highlight with no remote compound-path consequence.

## Cultural translation

Reference: [little-critters](https://github.com/GordenSun/little-critters).

Observed mechanism: situated agents make pointer proximity legible as reciprocal attention rather than as a dashboard control.

Mutine translation: remove the agents and refuse the look-back. A local witness does not animate a face; it alters the fill algebra of a distant plate, while the local plate records the cost as a notch.

Anti-copy statement: no animals, characters, eyes, paper scene, source palette, composition, or surface vocabulary are reproduced.

Direction consequence: closes v015's host/receiver territory, v016's articulated body/echo, and v017's connected wrong-return loop. It opens a constraint-and-topology direction in which the relation lives in compound-path parity and disjoint shape grammar.
