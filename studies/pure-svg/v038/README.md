# Pure SVG v038 — The field refuses the pair.

## Hypothesis

If a pure SVG work treats attention as a refused accord rather than a local mark, a sparse field of angular folds can keep two offered edges exact while producing a counterfold elsewhere. The visitor should feel the field reject connection and remember the refusal through geometry.

## Changed rule

v038 abandons v037's proximity tour through cut cells, v036's open filament organism, and v035's closed attention topology. The blind view is now twelve angular folds arranged on split margins around a quiet middle: no shared spine, enclosing body, or population of cells. The visitor offers two separated edges in one bounded gesture. The offered pair does not become a connector and does not deform. An unoffered counterfold opens, a remote hinge tightens, and an echo loses or gains a crease. Later accords rebuild from the altered fold field.

This is an inversion of the selected cultural mechanism: little-critters makes situated agents turn toward a visitor. Mutine refuses agents, faces, characters, paper scene, head-turning, palette, composition, source code, examples, and API surface; the abstract field turns away from the offered pair and makes its answer somewhere the visitor did not offer.

## Visible consequence

The caption-free first view is a split-margin composition of angular cut forms, faint boundary traces, and a deliberately quiet middle. It is neither a route, line bundle, body, score, nor cut-cell constellation. During the gesture a temporary accord line appears; on release it disappears. The two offered folds remain geometrically exact while three remote folds materially change through path, notch, scale, angle, crease, and role. Four refusals remain liftable and later accords inherit the changed field.

## Interaction and state

- Press one fold and release at a separated fold across the quiet middle.
- A one-edge offer, near pair, same-edge offer, or sprawling pair is refused without changing geometry.
- Enter, Space, and `offer two edges` use deterministic two-edge accords at fixed positions.
- Delete lifts the latest refusal; Escape or `R` releases the bounded memory.
- Four accords are retained. The same seed and same accord sequence produce the same SVG path signatures.
- `?preview=1&static=1&blind=1` keeps only the final counterfold field visible for caption-free inspection.

## Cultural translation

The reference is [little-critters](https://github.com/GordenSun/little-critters). Its observed mechanism is a small population of code-drawn agents whose proximity relation becomes reciprocal attention: the visitor enters the social field and agents turn toward that presence without a dashboard. The direct translation is intentionally inverted. Rather than making local forms turn toward the pointer, Pure SVG makes a pair of offered margins stay exact and turns an unoffered counterfold elsewhere.

The visible consequence is actual inline SVG geometry: the offer line is temporary; the two endpoint paths are unchanged; three remote folds change position, scale, notch, angle, and crease. The falsifier is any result in which the offer is copied, the endpoints deform, the response is only colour or readout, the field reads as a diagram or control panel, later accords forget, or lifting is inexact.

## Direction consequence

This closes Pure SVG's proximity-tour / open-filament / closed-topology family and opens a counterfold direction. The current keeps an economical SVG grammar but changes the question from “how can a relation move an absence?” to “what geometry does a refusal produce when the offered relation is not allowed to connect?” It also changes the viewer task from touring, drawing, or waiting to offering two edges and witnessing a non-local turn-away.

## Falsifier

Delete the direction if the blind composition reads as a generic grid, diagram, route, character sheet, or interface; if the offer line becomes permanent; if the pair itself changes; if the counterfold, hinge, or echo are cosmetic; if later accords restart from seed; or if exact lifting fails.

## Deletion condition

This is a candidate / held work until an independent caption-free comparison proves that the split-margin counterfold is perceptually distinct from v035–v037 and that the two-edge accord feels like a causal refusal rather than a disguised button or drag gesture.

## Critique handoff

The critic must inspect the blind field first. Ask: does the composition feel like a field with a withheld middle rather than a two-column diagram? Does the offer leave no local trace? Can a viewer see that the changed fold was not offered? Does the work ask to be encountered differently from the recent score, body, open-organism, and cut-cell grammars?
