# Pure SVG v021 — The ribbon keeps the pressure.

## Hypothesis

If pressure changes the silhouette-producing material of one SVG ribbon, rather than decorating a finished path, a minimal primitive can remember force without becoming a contour, plate field, or diagram.

## Changed rule

v021 abandons v018's disjoint negative alphabet, v019's radial paint-order valve, and v020's single compound contour. The image is now one broad, filled SVG ribbon made from seven pressure stations. A traveled gesture, not a tap or a location click, pinches the station where force enters and makes a non-adjacent station bind and swell. The altered widths and centerline are rebuilt into the ribbon path itself, and later pressure inherits the changed material.

## Visible consequence

The blind first view is a single oblique, dark ribbon on a quiet granular ground: no plate collection, radial stack, or negative-room alphabet. After one traveled pressure, the source becomes visibly narrow and kinked while the remote binder becomes visibly broad; the changed silhouette remains when labels, controls, and annotations are hidden. Three pressures are replayable, and lifting the latest reconstructs the exact preceding geometry signature.

## Interaction

- A short pointer tap is refused; drag across at least 64 CSS pixels and release to commit a traveled pressure.
- The deterministic `press once` control, Enter, and Space commit the same material event at the armed station.
- Keys `1`–`7` arm a station without writing history.
- Delete, Backspace, or `lift latest` removes only the latest pressure.
- `R` or `release` returns to the quiet ribbon.
- `S` downloads the currently rendered inline SVG.

## Falsifier

The translation fails if dragging only paints a cursor trail, if the source pinch or remote binder is a color badge rather than changed ribbon geometry, if a tap commits, if later pressure ignores the altered widths, or if the blind image reads as a styled line with captions attached.

## Deletion condition

Delete v021 rather than polish it if a caption-free viewer cannot distinguish the pressure-bearing ribbon from a decorative stroke, if the remote binder is not legible in the silhouette, or if lifting the latest pressure does not restore the exact prior signature.

## Cultural translation

Reference: [p5.brush](https://github.com/acamposuribe/p5.brush).

Observed mechanism: natural drawing tools treat pressure, density, grain, and directional fields as programmable material forces; material changes how geometry is produced and remembered instead of decorating finished geometry.

Mutine translation: refuse p5.brush's brush surface, watercolor appearance, hatching, and API. Translate material agency into one SVG ribbon whose width and centerline are regenerated from pressure stations. Force narrows its entry station, binds a distant station, and becomes inherited resistance for the next pressure.

Visible consequence: a single filled ribbon changes its actual silhouette at two separated stations after a traveled gesture; the next event starts from that altered material rather than from a reset path.

Falsifier: if pressure is only a trail, tint, cursor ring, or readout, or if the second pressure has the same geometry as the first, the translation fails.

Anti-copy statement: no p5.brush brushes, watercolor appearance, hatching vocabulary, palette, API surface, source examples, or composition are reproduced.

Direction consequence: closes Pure SVG v018's plate topology, v019's radial occlusion, and v020's compound contour. It opens a material-agency direction in which SVG's primitive budget is treated as a body with inherited width, rather than as a collection of formal rooms.
