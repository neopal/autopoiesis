# Pure SVG v037 — The witness leaves a vacancy.

## Hypothesis

If a pure SVG work treats proximity as a sequence of situated relations rather than as a mark, a field of compact cut cells can remember where it was witnessed without drawing the visitor's route. The missing part of the field should migrate away from the place that received attention.

## Changed rule

v037 abandons v036's open filament organism, v035's closed attention topology, and v034's delayed score. The first view is now an irregular field of eleven unequal, closed SVG cells with punched apertures: no shared spine, enclosing body, or temporal row. The visitor walks through at least three cells. The walk is reduced to a deterministic proximity route, not copied as geometry. The most witnessed cell contracts, a remote least-witnessed cell opens as a vacancy, and a third cell relays the displacement. Later tours rebuild from the altered field.

## Visible consequence

The caption-free first view is a broken constellation of cut forms suspended over a dark ground. It is neither a line bundle, a body, a score, nor a set of characters. After a valid tour, the attended cell becomes smaller and tighter, a remote cell opens a much larger aperture, and a third cell changes its angle and position. The temporary path disappears. Four vacancy exchanges remain liftable and later tours inherit the accumulated attention ledger.

## Interaction and state

- Walk through at least three distinct cells with a pointer or touch, then release.
- A flicker, one-cell visit, or sprawling tour is refused without changing geometry.
- Enter, Space, and `walk the field` use deterministic tours at fixed positions.
- Delete lifts the latest exchange; Escape or `R` releases the bounded field.
- Four tours are retained. The same seed and same tour sequence produce the same SVG path signatures.
- `?preview=1&static=1&blind=1` keeps only the final cut-cell field visible for caption-free inspection.

## Cultural translation

The reference is [little-critters](https://github.com/GordenSun/little-critters). Its observed mechanism is a small population of code-drawn agents whose proximity relation becomes reciprocal attention: the visitor enters the social field without a dashboard, and a local relation becomes legible. Mutine refuses animals, eyes, characters, paper style, head-turning, source palette, composition, code, examples, and API surface.

The translation is a vacancy rule in Pure SVG: proximity is accumulated as a tour through compact places, but the touched place does not answer locally. Its accumulated witness pressure causes a least-witnessed remote place to open, while a third place relays the displacement. The visible consequence is actual SVG path production: aperture, scale, rotation, and position change away from the temporary route. The falsifier is any result in which the route is copied, the local cell is the only answer, only colour or readout changes, the field forgets later tours, or lifting is inexact.

## Direction consequence

This closes Pure SVG's open filament / closed topology / delayed score family and opens a distributed cut-field direction. The current keeps SVG's primitive economy but changes the medium's question from “how can a path grow?” to “how can a relation move an absence?” It also changes the viewer task from drawing, holding, or waiting for a second beat to circulating through a field and leaving it.

## Falsifier

Delete the direction if the blind composition reads as a generic grid, route map, particle population, character sheet, or control panel; if the pointer route remains visible as a copied trace; if the local cell directly becomes the answer; if remote vacancy or relay geometry is cosmetic; if later tours restart from seed; or if exact lifting fails.

## Deletion condition

This is a candidate / held work until an independent caption-free comparison proves that the cut-cell constellation is perceptually distinct from v034–v036 and that the proximity tour feels like a causal relation rather than a disguised button or drawing gesture.

## Critique handoff

The critic must inspect the blind field first. Ask: does the first view read as an authored constellation of cuts before it reads as eleven objects? After a real tour, can a viewer locate the vacancy away from the path? Does the missing piece feel materially relocated rather than highlighted? Does the work ask to be encountered differently from the recent score, body, and open-organism grammars?
