SELF PORTRAIT / ENCOUNTER SWITCH / v024

The portrait cannot
hold a straight line.

A visitor can draw across the membrane, but cannot choose what breaks. The code measures the stroke, finds its own weak contour, and sends the remaining strain elsewhere.

OFFER STRAIN, NOT A TARGET

Drag across the contours and release. A short stroke is refused. The membrane's resistance chooses the rupture; a non-local contour receives the relay. Enter or the strain control submits a deterministic stroke. Delete lifts the latest rupture; R releases the bounded memory.

The membrane is holding its own resistance.

ANNOTATIONS / v024

changed rule
Replace v023's observed solid with one SVG membrane of nested contours. The visitor supplies a vector and length, but the portrait's internal resistance chooses which contour ruptures and where the relay travels.
visitor action
Draw and release across the field. Pointer position is a measurement, not a target. A short gesture does not write; a committed stroke changes the broken contour, a non-local relay, and the next resistance field.
falsifier
If the stroke is merely drawn on top, if the pointer location directly selects the ring, if the relay is fixed or cosmetic, or if later strokes ignore the changed field, the material translation fails.
deletion
Delete v024 if the blind membrane reads as another object, atlas, relief, or linefield, or if the rupture and exact lift cannot be found in the contour geometry without labels or readout.
