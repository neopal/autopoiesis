SELF PORTRAIT / CANVAS MEDIUM SWITCH / v031

The portrait keeps
the gap in its checksum.

It does not draw a face. It audits the pressure that reaches it, then lets an unobserved band inherit the answer.

PRESSURE IS NOT A TARGET

Hold the field and release after a short, bounded pause. A weak or over-held probe is refused. A valid pressure changes three remote band geometries: one opens, one inherits its contour, and one sheds material. The local point is only the measurement site.

The membrane is waiting for pressure.

ANNOTATIONS / v031

changed rule
Abandon v030's semantic decision population, v029's boundary shell, and v028's folded volume. The portrait becomes one p5.js Canvas 2D diagnostic membrane. A bounded pressure hold is read as resistance; the touched location never becomes the answer. Three remote bands change their actual geometry, and the accumulated load feeds the next probe.

falsifier

deletion
Delete v031 if the blind field reads as a generic bar chart, face, dashboard, or decorative raster, or if the gap, inherited contour, and opened band cannot be found in actual geometry without labels, controls, annotations, and prose.
