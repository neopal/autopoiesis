SELF PORTRAIT / MATERIAL FIELD / v039

The portrait turns
the field against itself.

Twenty-two ribbons are not marks waiting for your hand. They are a volume generated by a field. Arm two unequal sides and the portrait remakes its own curvature everywhere, as if its material rule had become the subject.

THE VOLUME NEEDS AN OPPOSITION

Tap or click the left and right halves, or use the arrow keys, to arm two opposing pressures. Neither offer changes the geometry. Press Enter / Space or “produce field” and the pair becomes a signed counterfield: every ribbon and the volume's twist are regenerated. Delete lifts the latest production; R or Escape releases the seed.

The field is waiting for a pair.

ANNOTATIONS / v039

changed rule
Abandon v038's situated clause, departure, and remote roles. A pair of unequal side pressures becomes a signed vector field that regenerates the entire volume rather than answering in a remote part.
visitor action
Arm left and right by pointer, touch, or Arrow keys; produce with Enter / Space or the control. Delete / Backspace lifts the latest field; R / Escape returns to the deterministic seed.
falsifier
If one side alone produces, if offers mutate geometry, if the result only changes colour or camera, if the whole field is not regenerated, or if later productions forget, the translation fails.
deletion
Delete v039 if caption-free viewing reads as a generic 3D ribbon sculpture or linefield rather than a self-portrait whose material rule is globally rewritten by opposition.
