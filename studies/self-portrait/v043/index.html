SELF PORTRAIT / MATERIAL DECISION / v043

The portrait
is made by resistance.

Nine pressure channels form one continuous slab. A stroke that follows the grain does not scar where it lands; the portrait re-forms an untouched channel, as if its material had already decided elsewhere.

PRESSURE IS THE INSTRUMENT

Press and drag across the canvas. The trace stays latent until it travels far enough, aligns with the grain, and meets resistance. Release then makes the material answer in another channel. On touch, drag and lift your finger. Enter / Space performs a deterministic crossing; Delete lifts the latest recast; R / Escape releases the portrait.

The portrait is waiting for pressure.

ANNOTATIONS / v043

changed rule
Material is not a skin over geometry. A grain-aligned pressure crossing is held without geometry, then re-produces an untouched channel with new contour count, density, angle, and phase while the contacted channel stays exact.
visitor action
Drag through the slab along its grain, then release. Pointer, touch, Enter / Space, and the press control share the same event; Delete / Backspace lifts the latest recast; R / Escape releases the portrait.
falsifier
If the trace paints a local scar, if a short or misaligned crossing commits, if the source changes, if the remote material is only colour or readout, or if later recasts reset, the translation fails.
deletion
Delete v043 if caption-free viewing reads as a generic ribbon field or if resistance is only explanatory language attached to a drag gesture.
