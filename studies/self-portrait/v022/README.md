# Self portrait / v022

## Status

**Candidate / held.** This is the 2026-10-06 daily tableau for the Self portrait current. It remains held until an independent caption-free comparison confirms that the atlas reads as a branching self-decision rather than a decorative card grid.

## Creative record

- **Hypothesis:** code can portray its own decisions by making a choice alter the possibility it refuses, so the self-image is a changing condition rather than a face or object.
- **Changed rule:** the browser-native branching atlas presents incompatible left/right possibilities. A committed choice opens one actual card, contracts the refused card, and moves a non-local counter-branch. The refusal becomes the cursor for the next decision.
- **Visible consequence:** the first-render field is a wide angular atlas of offset cards and negative cuts, not a face, mobile, relief, syntax seal, or serial register. After a choice, one card opens in coral, its sibling contracts into a dark refusal, and a remote blue card changes position and counter-mark. Repeated choices move the active condition and answer.
- **Deterministic state:** `SEED = 0x53504642`; `STAGES = 19`; `MEMORY_LIMIT = 5`; `NODE_COUNT = 18`; `PRIMITIVE_BUDGET = 54`. The pure engine is replayable and lifting the latest choice reconstructs the exact previous atlas.
- **Interaction:** tap the left or right half, use the two choice controls, or arm a side with `ArrowLeft` / `ArrowRight` and commit with `Enter` or `Space`. `Delete` lifts the latest refusal; `R` releases decision memory; `S` downloads the deterministic state as JSON.
- **Falsifier:** if the two choices only recolour fixed cards, if the refused possibility does not change the next active branch, if the remote answer is always in one place, or if the blind field reads as a decorative card grid, the translation is not embodied.
- **Deletion condition:** delete v022 if the blind atlas cannot show keep / refuse / remote answer through shape, scale, cut, and position alone, or if repeating a choice produces the same geometry and reply as the first choice.

## Lineage and radical progression

`portrait-2026-10-05` → v022: v019's inline-SVG syntax seal, v020's oblique Canvas relief, and v021's depth-separated WebGL mobile are abandoned. The representation switches to semantic HTML/CSS cards with actual layout variables; the composition changes from one object / mobile to a broad branching atlas; the encounter changes from keyboard attention, pressure release, and approach/departure to a binary wager; the memory model changes from clause debt, material load, and reciprocal aversion to refused possibility determining the next active condition.

The repeated gesture in the last three portrait works was a visitor directing attention or pressure at a stable field and receiving a local or remote geometric answer. v022 inverts that relation: the visitor chooses only one side, while the portrait preserves the unchosen side as the force that decides where it answers next. It asks what a self can be when its refusal, not its selected surface, carries continuity.

## Cultural translation

The selected reference is **little-critters**: a small population of situated code-drawn agents responds to pointer proximity by changing attention and looking back, making the visitor part of a reciprocal encounter rather than a controller of a dashboard.

Mutine refuses animals, eyes, character design, paper scene, source palette, composition, surface style, API, and head-turning imagery. The translated rule is reciprocal attention without characters: instead of an agent looking back, the atlas makes the visitor choose a local possibility and then lets the refused possibility answer from elsewhere through changed card geometry. The visitor's local choice is not the whole event; the field remembers what was not chosen and uses it to alter the next condition. This opens an adversarial self-observation direction and closes the direct gaze / material-load family.

## Evidence boundary

The focused unit tests prove deterministic branching geometry, actual kept/refused/counter states, changed future replies, bounded memory, replay, and exact restoration. Browser matrix, interaction, deployed readback, and independent caption-free perceptual evidence are recorded separately; until those gates are observed, this work remains held.
