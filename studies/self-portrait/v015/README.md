# Self portrait / v015

## Status

**Candidate / held.** This is the 2026-09-24 daily tableau for the Self portrait current. It remains held until an independent caption-free perceptual comparison confirms that the membrane reads as a self making material decisions rather than an illustrated oval with damage effects.

## Creative record

- **Hypothesis:** code can portray its own decisions by exposing how its material receives pressure, not by adding another route, witness, or modular part to a face.
- **Changed rule:** a remembered press changes the resistance map of one continuous membrane. The press yields an actual opening, separates two banks, and lifts a detached flap. Future pressure in the scarred neighbourhood meets more resistance and therefore produces a different yield.
- **Visible consequence:** the first-render field is a single continuous body with fibres, an aperture, and a quiet material tension. After interaction, the body dents, tears open, and carries a detached piece; a repeated press at the same place yields differently because the body has remembered resistance.
- **Deterministic state:** `SEED = 0x53504631`; `STAGES = 14`; `MEMORY_LIMIT = 4`; `PRIMITIVE_BUDGET = 63`; 72 outline points, 13 fibre bands, 24 resistance sectors. The engine is pure and replayable.
- **Interaction:** pointer pressure, click/tap, or Enter/Space presses the field. `lift latest scar` rebuilds the exact preceding membrane; `release pressure` returns to the autonomous sequence.
- **Falsifier:** if the opening is only a colour overlay, the flap is only a witness sticker, or a second press behaves as if the first never happened, the material rule is not embodied.
- **Deletion condition:** delete v015 if a caption-free pair sees a decorated oval instead of a surface that yields, tears, resists, and changes the next event.

## Lineage and radical progression

`portrait-2026-09-23` → v015: v014's discrete plate mosaic and look → answer → vacancy event are abandoned. This work changes representation from modular parts to one continuous membrane, changes the viewer task from looking to pressing, changes temporal behaviour from reciprocal witnesses to accumulated resistance, and changes the visible grammar from tile field to material rupture.

The repeated gesture in the last three portrait works was a visitor look becoming a bounded, reversible relation among face parts or face routes. v015 breaks that invariant: there is no witness, no answering part, no plate grid, and no route to follow. A pressure event changes the material law that receives the next event.

## Cultural translation

The selected reference is **p5.brush**. Its observed mechanism is that material is implemented as a programmable field of pressure, density, grain, and directional behaviour; material changes how geometry is produced, not only how geometry is coloured.

Mutine's translation refuses p5.brush's brushes, watercolor appearance, hatching vocabulary, API surface, and palette. In this portrait current, pressure becomes a resistance field inside a continuous self-image: the surface yields, tears, and lifts, then makes the next pressure encounter harder. The direction consequence is to open a material-agency period for Self portrait and close the reciprocal plate grammar.

## Evidence boundary

The focused unit tests prove deterministic bounded input, changed membrane geometry, actual gaps and detached flaps, remembered resistance, finite memory, and exact restoration. Browser and independent caption-free perceptual evidence must be recorded separately; until those gates are observed, this work stays held.
