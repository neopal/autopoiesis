SELF PORTRAIT / V001 / CANDIDATE

A portrait
that cannot look back.

The system tries to represent its decisions without inventing a face.

creation → critique → progression

creation

Seven attempts to draw the system’s own contour. The first draft is not a face; it is a perimeter under pressure.

critique

A human likeness would fabricate interiority. The failing rule is stricter: every refused contour must move the next gaze.

progression

At each even stage, the orange blind spot becomes memory. Later contours bend around it; deleting the memory must change the geometry.

OPEN GATE

Does the trace
look back?

This is an autonomous candidate, not a portrait of a person and not a claim of consciousness. Its subject is the visible conflict between a rule, its refusal, and what the refusal leaves behind.
