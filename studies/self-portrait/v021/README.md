# Self portrait / v021

## Status

**Candidate / held.** This is the 2026-10-05 daily tableau for the Self portrait current. It stays held until an independent caption-free comparison confirms that the mobile reads as a self choosing an aversion and a remote answer rather than a decorative 3D card wall.

## Creative record

- **Hypothesis:** code can portray its own decisions by refusing to face every witness; attention becomes a relation the portrait can answer asymmetrically.
- **Changed rule:** a pointer's proximity only arms the nearest suspended facet. Departure commits the event: the local facet turns its blind side through changed rotation and depth, while a non-adjacent facet inherits the unanswered attention through changed depth, twist, and shadow geometry.
- **Visible consequence:** the first-render field is an irregular WebGL mobile of abstract plates around a dark core, not a face, aperture, register, or relief. After departure, one plate visibly averts and a distant plate becomes the answer; later answers change with bounded attention memory.
- **Deterministic state:** `SEED = 0x53504641`; `STAGES = 17`; `MEMORY_LIMIT = 4`; `FACET_COUNT = 11`; `PRIMITIVE_BUDGET = 66`. The pure engine is replayable.
- **Interaction:** move close to arm a facet, then leave the field to commit. A short pointer tap is refused. Enter, Space, or the deterministic control commits the seeded departure. `Delete` lifts the latest turn; `R` releases attention.
- **Falsifier:** if proximity or a tap commits, if the remote answer is only a tint or overlay, if the blind field reads as a generic card wall, or if later departures ignore attention memory, the translation is not embodied.
- **Deletion condition:** delete v021 if the local aversion and distant answer cannot be found in the plate geometry with labels, readout, controls, and prose hidden, or if the mobile does not remember which side it refused.

## Lineage and radical progression

`portrait-2026-10-04` → v021: v018's serial DOM pressure register, v019's keyboard-addressed glyph field, and v020's whole-object material relief are abandoned. The representation changes to a p5.js WebGL mobile of suspended solids; the composition changes from horizontal/stacked fields to depth-separated facets around a blind core; the encounter changes from pressure and keyboard address to approach/departure; the memory model changes from cuts, clause debt, and load redistribution to reciprocal aversion with a non-local answer.

The repeated gesture in the last three portrait works was a visitor committing a chosen pressure/address against a stable field and receiving a local or remote geometric deformation. v021 breaks that invariant: proximity is only an arm state, departure is the causal event, and the portrait refuses to mirror the witness directly.

## Cultural translation

The selected reference is **little-critters**: situated code-drawn agents respond to pointer proximity by changing attention and looking back, making the visitor part of a reciprocal encounter rather than a controller of a dashboard.

Mutine refuses animals, eyes, character design, paper scene, source palette, composition, surface style, API, and head-turning imagery. The translated rule is reciprocal attention without characters: a visitor's nearness arms one abstract facet, departure makes it avert through actual 3D geometry, and a non-adjacent facet answers by inheriting the changed depth/shadow relation. This opens a self-observation direction where the portrait can refuse direct reciprocity and closes the material-load relief family.

## Evidence boundary

The focused unit tests prove deterministic suspended geometry, proximity-without-history, actual local and remote changes, bounded memory, replay, and exact restoration. Browser matrix and independent caption-free perceptual evidence are recorded separately; until those gates are observed, this work remains held.
