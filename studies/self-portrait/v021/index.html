SELF PORTRAIT / DIRECTION SWITCH / v021

The portrait keeps
a blind side.

A self-image does not turn toward every witness. Nearness only arms a suspended facet; leaving makes it avert, and a distant facet inherits the unanswered attention.

APPROACH, THEN DEPART

Move into the mobile and pause near a facet. Leave the field to commit the turn. A short tap is refused. Enter, Space, or “leave a blind side” uses a deterministic departure; Delete lifts the latest turn; R releases the bounded attention memory.

The mobile is not looking back.
