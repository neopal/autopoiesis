# Self portrait / v017

## Status

**Candidate / held.** This is the 2026-09-27 daily tableau for the Self portrait current. It remains held until an independent caption-free comparison confirms that the aperture reads as a self changing who can be seen, rather than a polished shutter illustration.

## Creative record

- **Hypothesis:** code can portray its own decisions by making visibility a negotiated relation among situated agents, not by adding another contour, wound, plate, or image cell.
- **Changed rule:** pointer proximity arms the nearest aperture blade. A committed gaze makes that blade refuse: its opening narrows, its hinge turns, its depth changes, and the shared aperture widens. A distant blade with less refusal debt inherits the opening, while the surrounding depth order is re-sorted.
- **Visible consequence:** the first-render field is a hard-edged, overlapping aperture with a central opening and unequal blades. After interaction, one blade visibly turns away, another becomes the new access point, and the occlusion order changes. Repeating the same gaze redirects it elsewhere because the first blade has remembered refusal debt.
- **Deterministic state:** `SEED = 0x53504637`; `STAGES = 13`; `MEMORY_LIMIT = 5`; `BLADE_COUNT = 11`; `PRIMITIVE_BUDGET = 58`. The engine is pure and replayable.
- **Interaction:** pointer movement only arms a blade. Tap, hold and release, click, or Enter/Space commits a gaze. `lift latest refusal` rebuilds the exact preceding aperture; `release the gaze` returns to the autonomous sequence.
- **Falsifier:** if the selected blade only changes tint, the redirected blade is only a marker, the depth order is unchanged, or a repeated gaze behaves as if refusal debt does not exist, the translation is not embodied.
- **Deletion condition:** delete v017 if a caption-free pair sees a polished shutter instead of a self-image that changes who can be seen after being approached.

## Lineage and radical progression

`portrait-2026-09-25` → v017: v016's raster population, v015's continuous pressure membrane, and v014's reciprocal plates are abandoned. This work changes representation from a field of image cells to an ordered aperture of overlapping hinged agents; changes the viewer task from sustained attention or pressure to approach then commitment; and changes temporal behaviour from density transfer or resistance to refusal debt that reroutes later visibility.

The repeated gesture in the last three portrait works was a visitor event changing one coherent body or one of its parts: plates answered, a membrane yielded, and raster patches redistributed density. v017 breaks that invariant. There is no face contour, tile grid, membrane, or point population. The self is staged as an access relation: one agent refuses the gaze, another inherits the opening, and the whole order of looking changes.

## Cultural translation

The selected reference is **little-critters**. Its observed mechanism is a population of situated code-drawn agents whose proximity to the pointer changes attention and produces a reciprocal encounter.

Mutine's translation refuses animals, characters, eyes, paper style, head-turning scene, source palette, composition, and surface vocabulary. In this portrait current, proximity becomes a situated selection rule for aperture blades. The selected blade does not look back as a character; it refuses, changes its depth, and redirects the opening to another agent. Its refusal debt changes the next collective decision. This opens a social-attention direction while forcing a medium switch from image field to browser-native aperture logic.

## Evidence boundary

The focused unit tests prove bounded proximity, structural blade deformation, changed depth order, non-cosmetic redirection, remembered refusal debt, finite history, deterministic timeline settling, and exact restoration. Browser and independent caption-free perceptual evidence must be recorded separately; until those gates are observed, this work stays held.
