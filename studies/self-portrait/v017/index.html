SELF PORTRAIT / V017 / DAILY WORK

The portrait
changes the order of looking.

A self-image as an aperture of situated agents: the nearest one refuses the gaze, and the rest must renegotiate who remains visible.

Candidate / held for caption-free perceptual critique. The structural rule is embodied in blade geometry, occlusion, depth order, aperture width, finite refusal debt, and exact replay. Promotion still depends on whether the blind field reads as a self changing its relation to looking rather than an elegant shutter illustration.

EVIDENCE BOUNDARY

Can a self
refuse a witness?

The work abandons the route, plate, membrane, and raster grammars of the previous three portrait trials. It is a hard-edged, ordered aperture: a small society of hinged agents that changes its own visibility order after being approached.
