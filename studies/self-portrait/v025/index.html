SELF PORTRAIT / ENCOUNTER SWITCH / v025

The portrait saves
your detour.

Move through the ribbon without choosing a segment. When you leave, the portrait sends your route elsewhere: an unaddressed cut opens, and a remote segment bends in reply.

TRAVERSE, THEN DEPART

Cross the field and leave its edge. Proximity only accumulates attention; departure is the causal event. Enter or the detour control submits a deterministic route. Delete lifts the latest detour; R releases the bounded attention.

The ribbon is waiting for a route.

ANNOTATIONS / v025

changed rule
Replace v024's centered membrane with one asymmetrical decision ribbon. A situated traverse records where the visitor was present, but departure makes the portrait choose a non-local segment to open and another segment to answer.
visitor action
Move across the ribbon, then leave the field. Pointer position is attention, not a target; the event is the departure. Touch, the detour control, Enter, and Space provide equivalent committed paths.
falsifier
If proximity changes geometry immediately, if departure always opens the same segment, if the reply is only a colour or overlay, or if later departures ignore the bounded route memory, the social-attention translation fails.
deletion
Delete v025 if the blind field reads as a chart, card array, or decorative line score, or if the cut-and-reply cannot be found in the segment geometry without labels, readout, or prose.
