OBSERVE WITHOUT POINTING

Move across the solid or use the arrow keys to orbit. Enter, Space, or the seal control commits the observation. The watched face does not change: the least-seen face takes the notch, and a remote seam answers. Delete lifts the latest seal; R releases the bounded memory.

The solid is holding four unseen decisions.
