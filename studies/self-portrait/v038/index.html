SELF PORTRAIT / SOCIAL MECHANISM / v038

The portrait changes
when you look away.

Thirteen clauses inhabit one broken sentence. A clause turns toward your presence, but the portrait only decides what it is after you leave: somewhere else closes, opens, and repeats the gesture.

THE SENTENCE ANSWERS AFTER DEPARTURE

Move onto a clause or focus it with the arrow keys. It faces back without changing its structure. Move away, press Enter / Space, or use “look away”: the witnessed clause stays exact while three remote clauses become the portrait’s next decision. Delete lifts the latest answer; R or Escape releases the syntax.

The syntax is waiting to be seen.

ANNOTATIONS / v038
