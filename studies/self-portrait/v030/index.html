SELF PORTRAIT / DOM MEDIUM SWITCH / v030

The portrait keeps
a second opinion.

The code does not look back as a face. It keeps a population of decisions, then lets the least-attended one cross the body when you ask once.

ONE PULSE, NOT A TARGET

Tap the field, press Enter or Space, or choose ask once. The point of contact is not the answer: a deterministic population rule promotes a minority decision and displaces another. Delete lifts the latest opinion; R or Escape releases the bounded memory.

The decisions are quiet.
