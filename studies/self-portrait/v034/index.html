SELF PORTRAIT / P5 CANVAS MEDIUM SWITCH / v034

The portrait guesses
before you do.

The body is gone. What remains is a prediction that hardens in the wrong place when the next decision refuses its forecast.

THE INSTRUMENT IS A FORECAST

Offer one signal to seed a forecast. Offer another signal and the loom compares your decision with its guess: agreement hardens a distant rib; mismatch splits a counterfactual rib and leaves the error in the material. The signal itself is never drawn at the point of input.

The loom is waiting for a first signal.

ANNOTATIONS / v034

changed rule
Abandon v033's reciprocal body, v032's contradiction hinge, and v031's pressure membrane. A first signal is withheld as a forecast; the next signal is compared with that forecast, and agreement or error changes a distant rib rather than the input site.
visitor action
Offer north, east, south, or west by pointer or arrow key. Enter repeats the current forecast; Delete lifts the latest decision; R or Escape releases the loom to its quiet seed.
falsifier
If the first signal changes geometry, if the input site becomes the answer, if confirmation and error are only colour or readout, if the forecast is forgotten, or if lifting is inexact, the predictive medium fails.
deletion
Delete v034 if the blind canvas reads as another body, membrane, hinge, route, or generic linefield, or if the remote hardening and counterfactual split cannot be seen without the furniture.
