creation

One continuous plate carries a hole that has not decided whether it is missing or merely unrecognised. There are no tiles, letters, witnesses, or model parts to address.

critique

The gesture must disappear. Its pressure should make a remote contour too certain while a different place loses its body; the error must remain in the plate, not in the readout.

progression

v035 leaves v034's faceted relief, v033's proof sheet, and v032's inheritance tree. The new unit is a continuous negative field and a pressure gesture that teaches the model the wrong absence.

mistakes the hole.

A pressure stroke returns as a contour that was never there.

Candidate / held for independent perceptual critique. v035 translates p5.brush's material agency into naive art: pressure changes the model's production rule, but the model makes the wrong inference. A bounded stroke is not drawn; the source compresses, a remote contour becomes too certain, a third place opens as absence, and a fourth ridge remembers the mistake.
