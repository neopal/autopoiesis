creation

A single painted route makes a promise about where its next band will land. A timed double tap makes that promise incorrectly, and the next bands inherit the mistake.

critique

Keep the mutation only if the wrong landing changes the route itself: a missing intended arrival and a visible knot elsewhere, not a badge added after the fact.

progression

Tap once to wait. Tap again before the picture forgets. Lift only the newest slip, then release the whole route back to its first instruction.

NAIVE ART / V023 / DAILY WORK

The line forgets
where it was going.

A timed double tap changes the rule of the next painted band. The route keeps the wrong arrival and carries it forward.

Candidate / held for independent perceptual critique. v023 abandons v020's pressure sheet, v021's wrong-room map, and v022's cut-out register. The medium becomes a continuous Canvas route with no slots or rooms: material memory lives in a changed landing, and later geometry is rebuilt from that wrong point. The event is temporal rather than positional: one tap only waits, two taps in time commit.
