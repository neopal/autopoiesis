NAIVE ART / V012 / DAILY WORK

The mistake
keeps a bridge.

A wrong line does not detour or rise alone. It carries itself over a hollow, lands on the other side, and keeps going.

Candidate / held for independent perceptual critique. v012 abandons the vertical bump. Each retained correction becomes a five-phase bridge—approach, rise, span, drop, reunite—replayed through the roof edge, ground, and walking route. Promotion requires that bridge grammar to survive a caption-free comparison; if it reads as an ornament or a lone arch over unchanged lines, delete this version.
