# Naive art v038 — The witness is missing where it stood.

## Hypothesis

If situated attention is treated as evidence rather than as a direct command, a naive picture will reconstruct the wrong place. The place actually watched should remain geometrically quiet; leaving it should remove a remote witness and misbuild another one. The error must remain visible after the visitor has gone.

## Changed rule

v038 leaves v037's pressure misprint, v036's false accordion page, and v035's pressure-misread plate. The medium becomes one deterministic p5.js Canvas 2D cut-out field: nine uneven forms surround a dark aperture, with no tiles, labels, or local answer. A held look is geometry-neutral. A valid departure makes a remote form become a missing witness, another inherit a false reconstruction, and a fourth carry a remembering edge. Four absences compound and can be lifted exactly.

## Interaction

- Pointer/touch: press and hold near a form, then release; short looks are refused and a held look does not change geometry.
- Keyboard: `Enter` or `Space` performs the next deterministic look-and-leave.
- Controls: **leave the wrong witness** commits the deterministic look; **lift last error** reconstructs the exact prior field; **release the field** clears memory.
- `?preview=1&static=1&blind=1` renders four settled absences with editorial furniture and controls hidden.

## Falsifier

Delete v038 if the approached form itself becomes the answer, if a short press commits, if the remote cavity or false reconstruction exists only in colour/readout, if the blind field reads as a generic crowd or diagram, if later looks reset, or if exact lifting fails.

## Deletion condition

Delete this direction if caption-free viewing cannot distinguish the cut-out field from v037's stamp relation, v036's page, and v035's pressure plate, or if the absence does not become the produced image.
