NAIVE ART / v038 / WITNESS ABSENCE

galleryjournal
gallery>naive art>v038
creation

Nine uneven cut-outs surround one dark aperture. They are not figures and do not look back; the field only keeps the place where a witness was expected.

critique

A held presence must be geometry-neutral. Leaving causes a distant witness to become missing and another to inherit a false reconstruction. The strongest answer is never where the visitor stood.

progression

v038 abandons v037's stamp relation, v036's page, and v035's pressure plate for a single cut-out field with absence as the produced image and departure as its threshold.

NAIVE ART / V038 / DAILY WORK

The witness is missing
where it stood.

The picture remembers being watched by removing the one place that was actually present.

Candidate / held for independent perceptual critique. v038 translates situated attention into a naive reconstruction error. A held look does not move the approached cut-out; departure removes a remote witness, misbuilds another, and leaves a fourth edge carrying the memory. Four absences compound in the field.

hold the place → leave it → lose another place → remember the wrong field
