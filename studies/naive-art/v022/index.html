NAIVE ART / V022 / DAILY WORK

The picture learns
the wrong slot.

A dragged cut-out is not returned to its place. The empty slot stays open, and the displaced piece is filed in the margin.

Candidate / held for independent perceptual critique. v022 abandons v019's panorama, v020's print sheet, and v021's topology map. The medium becomes browser layout itself: a drag changes actual DOM occupancy, leaves a vacancy, and moves the former occupant into a wrong margin. The picture remembers the error as reflow, not as a mark added on top.
