NAIVE ART / V015 / DAILY WORK

The picture
copies the wrong thing.

A mistake is no longer a missing piece. It becomes a bad transmission between pictures: one leans, one answers, one forgets.

Candidate / held for independent perceptual critique. v015 abandons the single-scene puncture and route grammar of v010–v014. Each remembered look makes a nearby panel lean, sends a wrong motif to a distant panel, and removes one actual pictorial slot from a third. Promotion requires the relation to read without labels, readout, or explanation.
