NAIVE ART / V020 / DAILY WORK

The picture keeps
the wrong pressure.

A press removes a real piece from one plate and forces a distant plate to print around a relief that was never there.

Candidate / held for independent perceptual critique. v020 abandons v017's pile, v018's hinge, and v019's continuous weather mural. One print sheet becomes a discrete pressure register: a press opens an actual cavity in a source plate, displaces a distant relief, and misaligns the shared register so later impressions are made by a changed rule.
