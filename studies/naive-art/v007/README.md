# Naive art v007 — The mistake keeps a shadow

Status: daily candidate, held for independent perceptual critique.

## Hypothesis

A naive drawing can keep a mistake alive without correcting it or turning it into a symbol. If a retained wrong turn leaves a short companion outline that travels beside the house, its ground, and its path before fusing back, the drawing should make memory feel spatial rather than narrated.

## Changed rule

v006 made every retained correction perform an advance, return, and continuation. v007 abandons that return rhythm. Each retained correction now becomes a three-phase shadow record: depart from the original line, run parallel for a short distance, then fuse back downstream. The same record is replayed from three origins: the house edge, the ground, and the route. The mistake does not come back; it keeps company.

## Visible consequence

- the refused panel is a direct house with a straight ground and route;
- the kept panel gives the house edge, ground, and path the same depart/parallel/fuse geometry;
- the companion line changes direction, spacing, and re-entry according to the retained point;
- the visitor can place a bounded shadow, and undo reconstructs the exact previous geometry;
- static preview and reduced motion settle on the final seeded stage without interaction.

## Art gate

- **Seed/state:** `0x4e413037`, twelve deterministic stages; `static=1` settles on stage 12 with eleven autonomous records, the latest seven retained in the finite structural window.
- **Expected consequence:** adding one shadow changes `scene.shadowRecords`, the house shadow points, ground shadow points, and route coordinates together.
- **Falsifier:** if the labels, readout, bridge, and fuse dots are removed and the kept panel reads as an ordinary double line, the changed rule is not perceptually carried.
- **Deletion condition:** delete v007 if two caption-free comparisons cannot identify the temporary parallel companion across the house and route, or if the visitor gesture changes only a counter.

## Interaction

Click the field to place a normalized shadow. The x position chooses the side and y controls its vertical lift. `Enter`/`Space` and **leave a shadow** add the same bounded visitor record. **undo latest** removes the newest record and reconstructs the earlier field. **release sequence** clears visitor memory and restarts the seeded sequence.
