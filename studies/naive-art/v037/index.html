creation

A single skewed print holds thirteen uneven marks. Their spacing refuses a neat field; the strongest mistake is allowed to occupy a place that was never tapped.

critique

The pressure chord must be visible as a material error: the three chosen marks fade, an unchosen mark swells, a vacancy opens, and a ridge keeps the pressure.

progression

v037 leaves v036's page-turn, v035's pressure plate, and v034's relief. The new unit is a three-mark relation rendered by p5.js as a misregistered print, not a page, plate, or body.

NAIVE ART / V037 / DAILY WORK

The mistake
cures the wrong edge.

Pressure leaves the chosen relation and hardens in a place that was never touched.

Candidate / held for independent perceptual critique. v037 translates p5.brush's production pressure into a naive misprint. Three marks are offered as a relation, but the raster print refuses to preserve the gesture: an unchosen mark becomes a false imprint, another collapses into a vacancy, and a third keeps a pressure ridge. Four errors remain liftable and later chords inherit the wrong print.
