creation

A single oversized proof line is made from invented glyphs. It is not a sentence, chart, or alphabet: it is a page trying to agree with itself.

critique

The typed sequence must remain absent from the image. Only the distant misheard form, transferred glyph, and opened blank can carry the error.

progression

v033 abandons v032's branch organism and pointer route. The new unit is a typographic witness: attention arrives as keys, and the page answers elsewhere.

A page can remember the wrong witness.

Candidate / held for independent perceptual critique. v033 translates little-critters' reciprocal attention into a keyboard proof: the visitor offers three distinct witnesses without writing the answer, and only a complete witness makes a distant glyph mishear its shape, transfers that form, and opens a real blank. The first two tokens remain geometrically silent.
