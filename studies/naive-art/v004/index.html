where away is.

A correction does not move the house or its door this time. It moves the point that tells every receding line where to go. The wrongness becomes a shared direction.

Candidate / held for independent perceptual critique. This v004 changes the rule rather than adding a mark: retained points bend the house's receding edge, ground, and route toward one false vanishing point. Promotion requires a caption-free observer to name the shared direction twice.
