creation

A sentence is held as a scattered typographic object. Its words are not a diagram of agents; they are the model's small inventory of what it thinks it heard.

critique

The first word must remain geometrically exact. Only a different reply can make the sentence mishear elsewhere as a changed word, a split phrase, and a fading echo.

progression

v041 abandons v040's relief, v039's raster plate, and v038's cut-out field. The viewer no longer presses, marks, or leaves a surface; they make a two-word relation inside an actual sentence.

Candidate / held for independent perceptual critique. v041 translates situated attention into a browser-native language error. The first word is only latent evidence; a different reply causes a distant word to be replaced, another to split, and another to echo. Four mishearings compound and remain liftable.
