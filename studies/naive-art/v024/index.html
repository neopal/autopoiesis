NAIVE ART / v024 / SHADOW MISREAD

galleryjournal
gallery>naive art>v024
creation

Nine childlike planes float in one paper theatre. Come close to one and leave: its hinge changes, and a far plane reconstructs itself from the shadow it misunderstood.

critique

Keep the mutation only if the error is spatial: depth, hinge, and cast shadow must change in the planes, not in a counter or a decorative cursor.

progression

Approach to arm. Depart to commit. Lift one misread, then release the theatre and watch the picture forget the witness.

NAIVE ART / V024 / DAILY WORK

The picture misreads
its own shadow.

A visitor's departure changes one paper plane. A distant plane inherits the wrong cast shadow and rebuilds itself from the mistake.

Candidate / held for independent perceptual critique. v024 abandons v021's rooms, v022's slots, and v023's painted route. The medium becomes a p5.js WebGL paper theatre: depth and hinge carry the error, while an approach-departure encounter replaces click, drag, and double tap. The work refuses to depict animals or eyes; attention is translated into a wrong reconstruction from a cast shadow.

approach → depart → misread shadow → rebuild
