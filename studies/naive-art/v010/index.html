NAIVE ART / V010 / DAILY WORK

The mistake
keeps a bypass.

A wrong turn does not enter the obstacle. It takes the longer side road, stays beside the trouble, then comes back altered.

Candidate / held for independent perceptual critique. v010 changes the spatial rule: each retained turn becomes an approach, sidestep, alongside run, and downstream return replayed through the roof edge, ground, and route. Promotion requires that bypass grammar to survive a caption-free comparison; if it reads as an offset line or the markers carry the meaning, delete this version.
