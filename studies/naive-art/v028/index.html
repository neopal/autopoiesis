NAIVE ART / v028 / MISREMEMBERED GAP

galleryjournal
gallery>naive art>v028
creation

The picture is a three-line notation it cannot quite read. Attention does not make a portrait of the visitor; leaving one mark makes the strip move its gap and repeat that mark elsewhere.

critique

Keep the mutation only if the misheard symbol and the moved blank are visible in the marks themselves, not supplied by the counter or the word “attention”.

progression

Look without writing, then leave. A short glance is refused. Lift one retained mistake, or release the bounded memory and return to the quiet strip.

NAIVE ART / V028 / DAILY WORK

The picture misremembers
the gap.

It sees one mark, loses another, and keeps the wrong absence where the visitor left.

Candidate / held for independent perceptual critique. v028 abandons v025's pressure body, v026's witness apertures, and v027's layered print. The medium becomes a semantic strip of marks: departure after a sustained look is the causal threshold, and the resulting absence, misheard symbol, and remote echo remain in bounded memory.

attention → departure → wrong mark → moved gap
