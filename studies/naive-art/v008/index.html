NAIVE ART / V008 / DAILY WORK

The mistake
keeps a knot.

A wrong turn is not corrected. It crosses the line, loops once, and returns downstream. The house learns the same crooked grammar as its path.

Candidate / held for independent perceptual critique. v008 changes the spatial rule: each retained turn becomes a cross, loop, and rejoin replayed through the roof edge, ground, and route. Promotion requires that knot grammar to survive a caption-free comparison; if the kept field reads as decoration or a doubled line, delete this version.
