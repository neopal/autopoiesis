# Naive art v008 — The mistake keeps a knot

Status: daily candidate, held for independent perceptual critique.

## Hypothesis

A naive drawing can keep a mistake alive without correcting it or turning it into a symbol. If a retained wrong turn leaves its lane, crosses it, loops once, and rejoins downstream across the house, horizon, and route, the drawing should make memory feel like a shared crooked grammar rather than a caption.

## Changed rule

v007 made every retained correction travel beside the original as a temporary shadow. v008 abandons parallel companionship. Each retained correction now becomes a four-point knot: depart, cross the original lane, loop once, and rejoin later. The same record is replayed from three origins — the roof edge, the ground, and the route — while each rejoin nudges the next origin downstream. The mistake does not double the line; it teaches the drawing how to take a late return.

## Visible consequence

- the proposal panel is a direct house with a straight horizon and route;
- the consequence panel gives the roof edge, ground, and walking route the same cross/loop/rejoin grammar;
- the visitor's x position chooses handedness and distance from the centre, while y chooses the knot's lift and tilt;
- the visitor can place a bounded knot, and undo reconstructs the exact prior geometry;
- static preview and reduced motion settle on the final seeded stage without interaction.

## Art gate

- **Seed/state:** `0x4e413038`, thirteen deterministic stages; `static=1` settles on stage 13 with the latest six autonomous knots retained in the finite structural window.
- **Expected consequence:** adding one knot changes `scene.knotRecords`, the house knot line, ground knot line, route coordinates, and return-peak geometry together.
- **Falsifier:** if the labels, readout, bridge, dots, and all prose are removed and the kept panel reads as decoration or a doubled line, the changed rule is not perceptually carried.
- **Deletion condition:** delete v008 if two caption-free comparisons cannot identify a repeated crossing-and-return in the roof, horizon, and route, or if the visitor gesture changes only a counter.

## Interaction

Click the field to place a normalized knot. The x position chooses left/right handedness and the y position controls its lift and tilt. `Enter`/`Space` and **keep the knot** add the same bounded visitor record. **undo latest** removes the newest record and reconstructs the earlier field. **release sequence** clears visitor memory and restarts the seeded sequence.
