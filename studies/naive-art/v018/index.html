NAIVE ART / V018 / DAILY WORK

The picture forgets
the hinge.

Drag one part toward another. The sculpture remembers the wrong neighbour and opens the joint it was born with.

Candidate / held for independent perceptual critique. v018 abandons v016's loose departure field and v017's load-bearing pile. One hinged picture is rebuilt as a crooked paper mechanism: a meaningful drag makes the nearest panel drift, severs its actual hinge into a gap, and makes a distant false hinge carry the stolen direction. Promotion requires that the wrong joint and opened joint survive without labels, readout, or explanation.
