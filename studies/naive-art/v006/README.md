# Naive art v006 — The mistake comes back

Status: daily candidate, held for independent perceptual critique.

## Hypothesis

A naive drawing can keep a mistake alive without turning it into a symbol. If a retained wrong turn advances, returns into its own trace, and continues, the house, its ground, and its path should repeat that three-part hesitation as one spatial rule.

## Changed rule

v005 made every retained correction a shared hinge. v006 abandons the single-turn rule. Each retained correction now becomes a reversal record with an advance, a return, and a continuation. The same record is replayed from three origins: the house edge, the ground, and the route. The mistake is not a bend; it is a small act of coming back.

## Visible consequence

- the refused panel is a direct house with a straight ground and route;
- the kept panel repeats each advance/return/continue rhythm in the house edge, ground, and path;
- the return count grows stage by stage while the primitive vocabulary stays fixed;
- the visitor can place a bounded left or right reversal, and undo reconstructs the exact previous geometry;
- static preview and reduced motion settle on the final seeded stage without interaction.

## Art gate

- **Seed/state:** `0x4e413036`, eleven deterministic stages; `static=1` settles on stage 11 with ten autonomous records, the latest eight retained in the finite structural window.
- **Expected consequence:** adding one reversal changes `scene.reversalRecords`, the house reversal points, ground reversals, and route coordinates together.
- **Falsifier:** if deleting the labels, readout, bridge, and reversal dots leaves only a pleasing generic zigzag, the changed rule is not perceptually carried.
- **Deletion condition:** delete v006 if two caption-free comparisons cannot identify the repeated return between house and path, or if the visitor gesture changes only a counter.

## Interaction

Click the field to place a normalized reversal. The x position chooses left or right; y changes its rise. `Enter`/`Space` and **bring it back** add the same bounded visitor reversal. **undo last** removes the newest reversal and reconstructs the earlier field. **release sequence** clears visitor memory and restarts the seeded sequence.
