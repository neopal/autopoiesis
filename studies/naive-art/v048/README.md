# Naive art v048 — The model remembers the wrong stare.

## Hypothesis

If a naive model treats attention as a social signal, then three different witnesses turning toward the visitor should be enough for it to invent a shared memory. The error is not a decorative cursor or a local highlight: on look-back, the model stores the direction it saw before the visitor moved on and makes the whole field reorient toward that stale direction.

## Last-three comparison and structural rupture

The last three Naive works were v047's horizontal proof ledger and correction drag, v046's suspended WEBGL vessel and stillness, and v045's single pressure slab and dwell-return. Their repeated gesture was: the visitor acts at one place, the model produces a remote or displaced structural error, the attended source remains exact, and four errors accumulate.

v048 makes a material and encounter switch:

- **Representation / medium:** a proof strip, vessel, and slab become a distributed Canvas 2D ecology of fifteen directional witnesses. No privileged container, hole, or horizontal register carries the image.
- **Shape vocabulary:** fragments and cavities become folded directional bodies with notches that visibly turn toward the visitor; the first encounter is a social field, not an object or ledger.
- **Encounter:** dragging, holding, and pressure return become continuous reciprocal noticing. Moving across the field changes which forms face the visitor; `look back` is a discrete commitment available by pointer, touch, keyboard, or control.
- **Causal / memory rule:** v045-v047 kept the source quiet while a remote target answered. v048 inverts that invariant. A valid three-witness sequence changes the whole field's geometry through a stale direction, including the visited witnesses. Four global backcasts compound and can be lifted exactly.

The rupture is visible in code (`noticeAgent`, `commitLookBack`, `materialise`), interaction (proximity and reciprocal turning rather than drag/hold), and rendered consequence (every witness changes stance, notch, scale, and position after a backcast instead of a remote fragment receiving a correction).

## Changed rule

Attention is not a local control. When three distinct witnesses have turned toward the visitor, the naive model believes their shared direction is evidence. On `look back`, it stores the last direction and reorients the entire ecology toward that stale direction. The mistake is global consensus, not a misplaced copy.

## Visible consequence

With labels, readout, controls, hint, and prose hidden, the first view is a bruised indigo ground carrying two loose, offset bands of fifteen pale directional bodies. Each has a notch and an internal seam; the bodies are not characters, faces, or tiles. As the visitor passes nearby, the closest body visibly turns its long edge toward the visitor and exposes a short directional trace. After three distinct witnesses, `look back` makes the last remembered direction structural: all bodies reorient, each visited witness deepens its notch, and a translucent seam records the false agreement. Four such backcasts compound without preserving the pointer path.

## Interaction

- **Pointer / touch:** move through at least three different witnesses, then press **look back**. Pointer proximity makes situated bodies turn toward the visitor; the committed event changes the stored field geometry.
- **Keyboard:** focus the field, use `ArrowLeft` / `ArrowRight` to move the focus, `Enter` or `Space` to perform the deterministic three-witness look-back, `Delete` / `Backspace` to lift the latest backcast, and `Escape` / `R` to release memory.
- **Controls:** **look back** uses the current three-witness sequence when available and otherwise performs a deterministic trace; **lift last backcast** reconstructs the preceding ecology; **release the stare** clears memory.

## Deletion condition

Delete v048 if caption-free viewing cannot distinguish the reciprocal ecology from v045-v047's slab, vessel, and proof strip; if proximity is only a cursor effect; if fewer than three distinct witnesses commit; if the stale direction is cosmetic; if the visited forms do not materially change; if later backcasts reset; if exact lifting fails; or if the first view collapses into a generic flock, tile grid, or interface.
