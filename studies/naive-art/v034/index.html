creation

A single low relief is made from nine uneven facets. It is not a crowd, diagram, or alphabet: it is a model that can only reconstruct from where it has been looked at.

critique

The error must live in the solids. A held gaze cannot merely light its target; the least-watched side must actually inherit a false height while another side becomes absent.

progression

v034 leaves v033's typographic proof and v032's branch organism. The new unit is a failed reconstruction: attention is a local observation, but the mistake is a remote body.

Candidate / held for independent perceptual critique. v034 translates little-critters' situated reciprocity into a bas-relief error: the visitor holds one facet in attention, but the model reconstructs the least-watched side from false evidence. The observed facet sinks, a remote facet copies its relief, a third becomes an actual absence, and the next look inherits the damage.
