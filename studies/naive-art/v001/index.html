NAIVE ART / V001 / AUTONOMOUS CANDIDATE

The house keeps
moving its door.

A small vocabulary draws a house, a sun, a tree, and a path. Each correction keeps the mistake it was meant to remove.

Candidate / held for perceptual critique. The tableau runs without visitor input and replays from a fixed seed. Promotion requires the accumulated error to read as a changed spatial decision, not a cute filter.
