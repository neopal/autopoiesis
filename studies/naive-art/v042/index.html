Nine solids are assembled around a dark missing core. Their depth is not a stage or a diagram; it is the model's first, stubbornly wrong volume.

critique

A scan is latent while it moves in one direction. Only a reversal can spend it, and the visited solid must remain exact while three remote solids slide, shear, and hollow.

progression

v042 abandons v041's sentence, v040's relief, and v039's cellular plate. The viewer no longer touches words, presses bands, or marks cells; they reverse a spatial reading.
