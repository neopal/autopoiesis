The map answers
in the wrong room.

Attention opens a doorway here and closes one elsewhere. The picture keeps the misaddressed reply as a change in topology.

Candidate / held for independent perceptual critique. v021 abandons v018's hinge, v019's weather mural, and v020's pressure sheet. A single inline SVG map becomes a field of disjoint rooms around an absence: proximity arms a situated room, and one committed look opens its boundary while a distant room returns a wrong closure. Lifting the latest look reconstructs the prior topology.
