# Naive art v019 — The picture remembers a second horizon

Status: daily candidate, held for independent caption-free perceptual review.

## Hypothesis

A naive picture can remember a mistake as a shared world-rule rather than as a damaged object. If a visitor steps the weather forward with the wheel, one form should open its contour, a distant form should inherit the wrong edge, and the common horizon should redraw itself to contain the misreading. The memory must live in the mural's geometry, not in a counter or caption.

## Cultural translation

The selected reference is `little-critters`. Its observed mechanism is situated reciprocity: proximity changes attention and code-drawn agents look back. v019 refuses the look-back and translates attention into an ordinal weather error: a wheel step makes one local form lose its edge, teaches a distant form the wrong contour, and forces the shared horizon to carry the false relation. No animals, characters, eyes, paper style, source palette, composition, or surface vocabulary are reproduced.

## Changed rule

v016 used isolated forms and departure, v017 used a load-bearing pile and sustained attention, and v018 used a hinged object and directional drag. v019 changes representation, composition, encounter, and temporal behavior together:

- the hinged sculpture becomes one continuous painted panorama with a shared horizon;
- drag and dwell are replaced by a discrete wheel/arrow step with no pointer-position state;
- the source weather form opens through changed contour geometry;
- a distant form borrows an edge instead of merely changing colour;
- the horizon redraws at a wrong slope, making the mistake a property of the whole image;
- undo removes only the latest weather misread and reconstructs the exact previous panorama.

The gesture is `wheel step → wrong weather → source contour opens → distant contour borrows → horizon tilts`.

## Visible consequence

- the blind field is one continuous naive mural rather than a pile, picture strip, loose population, or hinged object;
- pointer movement and short taps change no geometry;
- a wheel or arrow step changes one source contour, one distant contour, and the shared horizon together;
- Enter/Space and **misread one weather** commit the same deterministic event; undo restores the exact preceding panorama; release restarts the seeded sequence.

## Art gate

- **Seed/state:** `0x4e413139`, eighteen deterministic stages, four-event visible memory window; reduced motion/static preview settles on the final retained state.
- **Expected consequence:** one step changes a source form, a distant form, and the shared horizon together.
- **Falsifier:** if labels/readout are removed and the work reads as a repaired hinge, if a short tap changes the mural, or if the source opening and horizon shift are only recolouring, the translation fails.
- **Deletion condition:** delete v019 if two caption-free comparisons cannot distinguish a wheel-stepped weather error from v018's directional drag, or if the blind view collapses back into a pile, card array, or hinged object.

## Interaction

Scroll or press ArrowUp/ArrowDown. A step commits one bounded weather misread. A short tap is refused. `Enter`/`Space` and **misread one weather** place the same default event. **undo latest** removes the newest event and reconstructs the earlier panorama. **release panorama** clears visitor memory and restarts the seeded sequence.
