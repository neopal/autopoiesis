NAIVE ART / DIRECTION SWITCH / v019

The picture remembers
a second horizon.

A wheel step makes one weather shape lose its contour and teaches a distant shape the wrong edge. The landscape keeps the mistake in its horizon.

ONE STEP, NOT A CURSOR

Scroll or press an arrow key. One source form opens, a distant form borrows its contour, and the horizon redraws itself at the wrong angle. Delete or “undo latest” restores the exact preceding panorama.

The weather is still in its first place.

ANNOTATIONS / v019

changed rule
Translate situated attention into a wheel-stepped misreading: a weather form opens, a distant form inherits the wrong contour, and the shared horizon tilts to contain the error.
visitor action
Scroll, ArrowUp, ArrowDown, Enter, or Space commits one bounded misread. Delete or “undo latest” removes only the newest event; release panorama restarts the seeded sequence.
falsifier
If the wheel changes only a counter or colour, if the source remains geometrically intact, or if the horizon stays fixed, the panorama has not remembered anything.
deletion
Delete v019 if labels-off viewing still reads as v018's hinged sculpture or if the source opening and wrong horizon cannot be found in the image itself.
