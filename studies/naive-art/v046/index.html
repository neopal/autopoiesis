creation

One suspended body carries seven throats. The model treats stillness as a social signal and gives the quiet place a reply that was never there.

critique

The attended throat stays exact. Only a motionless hold long enough to be mistaken for reciprocal attention can deform a different throat into a material echo.

progression

v046 abandons v045's pressure trace, v044's shutters, and v043's contour quorum. Stillness becomes the input; an extruded vessel replaces the planar wall and slab.

NAIVE ART / V046 / DAILY WORK

The model hears
a chorus in the gap.

It mistakes one quiet attention for a reply from somewhere else.

Candidate / held for independent perceptual critique. v046 translates reciprocal attention into a naive listening error: a visitor's still hold at one throat is mistaken for a chorus and materialises an echo in another throat. Four echoes compound as liftable memory.
