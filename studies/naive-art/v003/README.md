# Naive art v003 — The house keeps its outside inside

Status: daily candidate, held for independent perceptual critique.

## Hypothesis

A reduced house vocabulary can make a remembered mistake legible by changing the house's threshold rather than merely moving the house. If a correction migrates the opening from the front wall to a side or roof, the route should become an architectural fact: it enters the house, crosses its body, and returns outside.

## Changed rule

v002 made the kept house walk away from a correction vector. v003 changes the rule: each retained correction advances the next threshold around the house. The refused panel always proposes a front door; the kept panel carries the accumulated threshold to the next wall. A visitor correction chooses one or two wall steps from the side of the field, so the topology and route change together.

## Visible consequence

- the coral proposal has a normal front door and a short path;
- the kept house can open through its left wall, right wall, or roof;
- the dark route enters that opening, travels through the house, and exits elsewhere;
- removing the latest correction closes the migrated threshold and restores the exact earlier route;
- the small transfer trace names the decision but is not required to read the architectural change.

## Art gate

- **Seed/state:** `0x4e413033`, nine deterministic stages; `static=1` settles on stage 09 with eight retained threshold steps.
- **Expected consequence:** deleting one retained correction changes the opening wall and route topology, not only a colour or label.
- **Falsifier:** if a remembered threshold can be removed while the kept route still has the same number and position of interior crossings, the memory rule is ornamental.
- **Deletion condition:** delete v003 if a caption-free observer cannot identify the migrated opening and the route's return in two comparisons, or if the roof/side opening reads as a decorative window.

## Interaction

`move the threshold` and Enter/Space on the field add a normalized visitor threshold and pause the sequence. The side of the gesture chooses one or two wall steps; its height shifts the opening on that wall. `undo last` removes the latest threshold. `release sequence` clears visitor memory and restarts the seeded route. Reduced motion and `static=1` show the final remembered topology without requiring input.

## Public loop

`creation → critique → progression`
