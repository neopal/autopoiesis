NAIVE ART / V003 / DAILY WORK

The house keeps
its outside inside.

A correction does not move the house this time. It moves the opening. The route crosses the wall, borrows the inside, and comes back out somewhere the house did not plan.

Candidate / held for independent perceptual critique. This v003 changes the rule rather than adding a mark: retained thresholds migrate around the house and carry the route through its body. Promotion requires a caption-free observer to name the opening and return twice.
