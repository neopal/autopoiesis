creation

One crowded paper body holds its own empty place. Pressure does not paint a mark; it asks every fragment to make room for a cut that was not there before.

critique

Keep the mutation only if the aperture, silhouette, and fragment geometry move together. A readout cannot stand in for a changed sheet.

progression

Press and hold to commit a sampled pressure. Lift one cut, or release the whole pressure history and return to the first body.

NAIVE ART / V025 / DAILY WORK

The picture swallows
the pressure.

A sustained press changes the cut that makes the whole paper body. The next picture inherits the absence.

Candidate / held for independent perceptual critique. v025 abandons v022's cut-out register, v023's continuous route, and v024's faceted theatre. The medium becomes one dense p5.js Canvas 2D construction sheet: pressure is a global material force, and its memory is a moving absence rather than a route, plane, or shadow.
