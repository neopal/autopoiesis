creation

A crooked accordion page occupies the room as one interrupted reading surface. Its leaves overlap too far to behave like a catalogue of objects.

critique

The page must mistake the fold it can see. A closed leaf should cause an unseen leaf to open, while a third leaf repeats the crease in the wrong direction.

progression

v036 leaves v035's pressure plate, v034's relief, and v033's proof sheet. The new unit is a browser-native page turn and a false pagination that remembers itself.

NAIVE ART / V036 / DAILY WORK

The page
reads the wrong fold.

A turn closes what is seen and opens what was never read.

Candidate / held for independent perceptual critique. v036 translates little-critters' situated reciprocal attention into a false accordion: the reader's page turn is treated as evidence, but the page answers with an unseen fold. The visible leaf shuts, a remote leaf invents an opening, and a third leaf echoes the crease. Four errors remain liftable and later turns inherit the wrong pagination.
