# Naive art v036 — The page reads the wrong fold.

## Hypothesis

If a model reads a visitor's completed page turn as evidence, but cannot preserve the turn as a mark, it will mis-paginate the page: the fold being read will shut, an unseen fold will invent an opening, and a third fold will echo the crease in the wrong direction. The error must be visible in the page's actual geometry.

## Changed rule

v036 leaves v035's pressure-misread plate, v034's faceted reconstruction, and v033's typographic proof. The work is no longer a Canvas image or a model made from discrete relief parts. It is one browser-native accordion page made from eleven overlapping DOM/CSS leaves. A complete scroll, swipe, or arrow-key turn is treated as a page-reading event. The visible fold shuts, a non-local unread fold opens, and a third leaf inherits a false crease. Four false turns remain liftable; later turns rebuild from the altered pagination.

## Cultural translation

- **Reference:** [little-critters](https://github.com/GordenSun/little-critters)
- **Observed mechanism:** situated agents turn proximity into reciprocal attention; the visitor enters a small field and local behaviour answers without a dashboard.
- **Mutine translation:** refuse animals, eyes, characters, paper scenes, head-turning, and source surface vocabulary. Translate reciprocal attention into a page that treats the reader's turn as evidence but answers from an unseen fold: the seen leaf closes, the unread leaf invents an opening, and a third leaf echoes the wrong crease.
- **Anti-copy:** no animals, characters, eyes, paper scene, source palette, source composition, source code, API, example, or head-turning image is reproduced.

## Visible consequence

The first blind view is a single crooked accordion page made from overlapping leaves, with a deliberate open interval at its centre. It must not read as a grid of cards or a control panel. After a valid turn, the leaf under the reader's attention actually narrows and closes; a remote leaf actually grows, moves, and opens; and a third leaf actually changes its fold. The turn itself is never drawn. Four errors remain visible in the settled blind view.

## Interaction

- Wheel motion accumulates into a page turn; a shallow wheel movement is refused without changing geometry.
- A touch/pointer swipe across the page is an equivalent full turn; a short swipe is refused.
- Arrow keys and the **turn the page** control provide deterministic keyboard/control paths.
- Delete lifts the latest false pagination exactly; Escape or R releases the page to the quiet seed.
- `?preview=1&static=1&blind=1` renders four settled turns with editorial furniture hidden.

## Falsifier

Delete v036 if the first view reads as a card grid, if the scroll merely animates a decorative page, if the seen leaf is the only answer, if the unseen opening or echoed crease is cosmetic, if later turns reset, or if exact lifting fails.

## Deletion condition

Delete this direction if caption-free viewing cannot distinguish the accordion page from v035's pressure plate, v034's relief, and v033's proof sheet, or if the page-turn changes only labels, colour, or readout rather than the DOM/CSS fold geometry.

## Direction consequence

This opens `false-accordion`: naive error becomes a false pagination and a browser-native page remembers the wrong reading order. It closes the recent pressure / dwell / witness sequence and forces a new medium and encounter: overlapping DOM/CSS leaves, scroll and swipe, and a page that answers from what was not seen.
