Presence is accepted this time. Stay long enough and the pile assigns you to the wrong block.

Candidate / held for independent perceptual critique. v017 leaves v015's picture panels and v016's departure field behind. One assembled canvas pile accepts a visitor's sustained presence, then misaddresses the attended block: a false face is cut into its geometry, a distant block inherits the wrong identity, and the supports expose a real gap. Promotion requires those changes to read without labels, readout, or explanation.
