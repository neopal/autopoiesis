NAIVE ART / V016 / DAILY WORK

The mistake waits
until you leave.

Presence is not enough. The field keeps the wrong part only after the visitor has gone.

Candidate / held for independent perceptual critique. v016 inverts v015's immediate reciprocal copy. The field no longer uses picture panels or a look that answers at once: proximity is refused, departure cuts one loose form, sends a wrong fragment to a distant form, and hollows a third. Promotion requires the three material consequences to read without labels, readout, or explanation.
