# Naive art v009 — The mistake keeps a doorway

Status: daily candidate, held for independent caption-free perceptual review.

## Hypothesis

A naive drawing can keep a mistake alive without correcting it or turning it into a symbol. If a retained wrong turn cuts a small passage in the line, the drawing should make memory feel like a shared permission to pass rather than a decorative scar. The roof edge, ground, and walking route should each approach, cross a threshold, travel inside, and exit downstream.

## Changed rule

v008 made each retained correction a depart/cross/loop/rejoin knot. v009 abandons that self-knotting grammar. Each retained correction now becomes a four-phase doorway record: approach, threshold, inside, and exit. The same record is replayed from three origins — the roof edge, the ground, and the route — while each exit moves the next cursor downstream. The mistake does not double or loop the line; it teaches the line how to pass through itself.

## Visible consequence

- the proposal panel is a direct house with a straight ground and route;
- the consequence panel gives the roof edge, ground, and walking route the same little passage grammar;
- the visitor's x position chooses the side and width of the doorway, while y chooses its depth and tilt;
- the visitor can place a bounded doorway, and undo reconstructs the exact previous geometry;
- static preview and reduced motion settle on the final seeded stage without interaction.

## Art gate

- **Seed/state:** `0x4e413039`, fourteen deterministic stages; `static=1` settles on stage 14 with the latest six autonomous doorways retained in the finite structural window.
- **Expected consequence:** adding one doorway changes `scene.doorRecords`, the house doorway line, ground doorway line, route coordinates, and downstream roof return together.
- **Falsifier:** if labels, readout, bridge, door dots, and all prose are removed and the kept panel reads as an offset or doubled line, the changed rule is not perceptually carried.
- **Deletion condition:** delete v009 if two caption-free comparisons cannot identify a repeated passage with threshold and exit in the roof, ground, and route, or if the visitor gesture changes only a counter.

## Interaction

Click the field to place a normalized doorway. The x position chooses the side and width; the y position controls its depth and tilt. `Enter`/`Space` and **keep the doorway** add the same bounded visitor record. **undo latest** removes the newest record and reconstructs the earlier field. **release sequence** clears visitor memory and restarts the seeded sequence.
