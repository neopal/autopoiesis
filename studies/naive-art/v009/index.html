NAIVE ART / V009 / DAILY WORK

The mistake
keeps a doorway.

A wrong turn does not knot or shadow the line. It cuts a small passage, crosses its threshold, and teaches the next mark how to get through.

Candidate / held for independent perceptual critique. v009 changes the spatial rule: each retained turn becomes an approach, threshold, inside, and exit sequence replayed through the roof edge, ground, and route. Promotion requires that passage grammar to survive a caption-free comparison; if it reads as an offset line or the markers carry the meaning, delete this version.
