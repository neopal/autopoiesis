# Naive art v044 — The model agrees on the place you left.

## Hypothesis

If a naive model mistakes reciprocal attention for evidence, then it should treat a visitor's departure from three noticed sites as proof that an unseen object occupied another site. The visited shutters must reopen exactly; the model's mistake is the remote absence it manufactures after the visitor is gone.

## Last-three comparison and structural rupture

The last three Naive works were v043's contour quorum, v042's WebGL wrong volume, and v041's browser-native wrong sentence. Their repeated gesture was local evidence being extracted, scanned, or pressed and then displaced into a remote mutation while the visitor still manipulated the field.

v044 makes a material rupture:

- **Representation / medium:** contour witnesses, solid blocks, and DOM words become a Canvas 2D wall of ten hinged shutters with paper planes, dark backs, and negative absences.
- **Composition:** broken orbit, oblique volume, and sentence become a staggered wall with no privileged centre; absences accumulate in the gaps and edges.
- **Encounter:** selection, scan/reverse, and pressure route become continuous proximity. The visitor moves across three different shutters; the decisive event is leaving, not choosing a button.
- **Temporal / causal rule:** a local approach briefly exposes a shutter's reverse but does not edit it. Only departure commits a remote absence, and four departures compound as liftable memory.

The rupture is visible in code (`noticeShutter` plus `commitDeparture`), interaction (pointer/touch movement and pointer leave, with keyboard/control equivalents), and rendered consequence (shutters close back to their exact baseline while a remote negative form appears).

## Changed rule

Presence is locally noticed but not retained where it happens. Three distinct shutters are enough for the naive model to agree that something was present elsewhere; only leaving the wall spends that agreement and produces a remote absence. The three shutters remain geometrically exact. Four absences persist and can be lifted in reverse.

## Visible consequence

With labels, readout, controls, hint, and prose hidden, the first view is a staggered wall of ten paper-like shutters. A visitor's approach exposes a dark reverse locally. After three different approaches and a departure, the shutters return to their same geometry and a new irregular negative absence appears in a gap elsewhere. Four departures form a cumulative, liftable history.

## Interaction

- **Pointer / touch:** move across three different shutters and leave the tableau. Pointer movement is the visit; pointer leave is the commit. A touch drag followed by release uses the same `commitDeparture` path.
- **Keyboard:** focus shutters with `ArrowLeft` / `ArrowRight`; `Enter` or `Space` notices the focused shutter, then commits the visit when three are pending. `Delete` / `Backspace` lifts the latest absence; `Escape` / `R` releases the wall.
- **Control:** **ask the model** commits the next deterministic three-shutter departure; **lift last absence** reconstructs the preceding wall; **release the wall** clears memory.
- `?preview=1&static=1&blind=1` renders four settled absences with editorial furniture and controls hidden.

## Deletion condition

Delete v044 if caption-free viewing cannot distinguish departure agreement from v041–v043's sentence, relief, volume, or false-centre grammars; if incomplete movement commits; if a visited shutter deforms; if the remote absence is cosmetic; if every event produces the same absence; if later memory resets; or if exact lifting fails.
