NAIVE ART / v044 / DEPARTURE AGREEMENT

galleryjournal
gallery>naive art>v044
creation

Ten awkward shutters form a wall. They do not look back as animals; they expose a dark reverse when a visitor approaches and close again when the visitor leaves.

critique

Presence is not a button or a selection ring. Only a visit to three different shutters followed by departure lets the model agree on an absence somewhere else.

progression

v044 abandons v043's orbit, v042's volume, and v041's sentence. It replaces evidence extraction with a continuous social encounter whose decisive event is leaving.

A visitor's departure is mistaken for evidence of a missing object.

Candidate / held for independent perceptual critique. v044 translates reciprocal attention into a departure-agreement engine. Three locally noticed shutters stay geometrically exact; only leaving commits a remote absence, and four absences compound as liftable memory.
