NAIVE ART / v029 / FALSE JOIN

galleryjournal
gallery>naive art>v029
creation

The picture is not a crowd and not a score. It is one crude cut-paper world whose distant parts are made to remember a relation that never happened.

critique

Keep the mutation only if the three false-joined bodies visibly move in the canvas without a cursor trail, status label, or explanatory caption.

progression

A single point does nothing. A hesitant crossing gesture is the new threshold: lift one false join, or release the bounded memory and return to the seeded picture.

NAIVE ART / V029 / DAILY WORK

The picture joins
what never touched.

A wavering hand makes the far-away parts remember one another incorrectly.

Candidate / held for independent perceptual critique. v029 abandons v026's witness apertures, v027's layered registration print, and v028's semantic mark strip. The medium becomes a raster cut-paper picture: proximity is translated into a hesitant cross-seam gesture, and release misjoins three distant parts into a persistent false relation.

approach → cross the seam → hesitate → false join
