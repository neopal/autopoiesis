NAIVE ART / V002 / DAILY WORK

The correction
walks outside.

A house corrects its doorway and keeps the error as a spatial instruction. The next threshold, wall, and path must move around what should have been erased.

Candidate / held for independent perceptual critique. This v002 changes the rule rather than adding a mark: retained corrections displace the kept house and bend its route. Promotion requires a caption-free observer to name the same changed door and path twice.
