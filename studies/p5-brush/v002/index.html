BRUSH / V002 / AUTONOMOUS CANDIDATE

A brush returns
to its own wound.

The brush paints, cuts its own field, and routes the next stroke around the wound. No visitor starts the return.

Candidate / held for perceptual critique. This version answers the v001 failure by making return autonomous and replayable. The old pointer-driven v001 remains in the archive as an interaction, not as this work’s engine.
