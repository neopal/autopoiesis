BRUSH / AUTONOMOUS MATERIAL STUDY / v010

The brush keeps
a hinge.

Take pigment away and the next wet mark does not only part around the loss. It bends through a hinge, carries the turn into its tail, then returns to the old lane with the curl still wet.

ONE MATERIAL ACTION

Touch the field to make a hinge. Later strokes pivot with alternating pressure, curl through the remembered point, and decay back into their routes. Lift the latest hinge to rebuild the exact prior field.

The wet marks are carrying a turn through the field.

ANNOTATIONS / v010

changed rule
Each retained removal becomes a signed hinge. Nearby wet routes pivot in alternating directions around it, carry a narrow curl into the downstream tail, then settle back toward their original lane.
visitor action
A pointer, Enter, or Space makes one bounded hinge. Lifting the latest hinge recomputes every pivot, curl, tail, and return from the preceding memory.
falsifier
If the routes only kink at the visible dry mouth, if the curl does not travel downstream, or if the return is a drawn witness over unchanged strokes, the hinge is a diagram rather than material memory.
deletion
Delete the hinge-curl rule if a caption-free comparison with hinges, ribbons, notation, labels, and readout hidden cannot locate a repeated pivot followed by a decaying return in the strokes themselves.
