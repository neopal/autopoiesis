BRUSH / AUTONOMOUS MATERIAL STUDY / v004

The brush remembers
what was taken.

A removal is not a scar placed on the paint. It is a dry basin that later matter gathers around, enters, and leaves with less of itself.

ONE MATERIAL ACTION

Touch the field to remove pigment. The next wet strokes are pulled into the basin, thin at its mouth, and return displaced. Lift the latest removal to rebuild the exact prior field.

The wet marks are carrying what remains.

ANNOTATIONS / v004

changed rule
Each retained removal becomes a dry basin. Later wet strokes are pulled toward its mouth, lose pigment there, then reconstitute as a thinner displaced return.
visitor action
A pointer, Enter, or the action rail removes one bounded patch of pigment. Lifting the latest removal recomputes every stroke from the preceding memory.
falsifier
If an empty-memory replay or latest-removal lift leaves route, pigment, and return width unchanged, the basin is only a drawn witness.
deletion
Delete the dry-basin rule if its rings explain the piece more clearly than the wet strokes’ changed body in a caption-free comparison.
