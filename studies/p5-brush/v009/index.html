BRUSH / AUTONOMOUS MATERIAL STUDY / v009

The brush settles
the braid.

Take pigment away and the next wet mark does not simply exchange around the loss. It splits, travels as two pressures, then settles into one deposited braid that the surface keeps.

ONE MATERIAL ACTION

Touch the field to make a braid gate. Later strokes divide around the dry point, carry their opposite pressure forward, and settle back into a common deposit. Lift the latest gate to rebuild the exact prior field.

The wet marks are splitting around what has been removed.

ANNOTATIONS / v009

changed rule
Each retained removal becomes a braid gate. Nearby wet routes split in opposite directions around it, then settle toward one downstream deposit where width and pigment density accumulate.
visitor action
A pointer, Enter, or Space makes one bounded braid gate. Lifting the latest gate recomputes every split, settlement, route, and deposit from the preceding memory.
falsifier
If the routes only kink at the visible dry mouth, if their downstream paths do not rejoin, or if the deposit is a drawn witness over unchanged strokes, the braid is a diagram rather than material memory.
deletion
Delete the split-settle rule if a caption-free comparison with gates, threads, notation, labels, and readout hidden cannot locate a repeated divide and common downstream deposit in the strokes themselves.
