# Brush v032 — The paint carries a weather system.

## Status

Spike / held. This is a deliberate Brush divergence study, not a daily catalogue record yet.

## Artistic hypothesis

If a brush is a material field rather than a stamp, then pressure should alter how pigment travels through a sheet. The visitor does not place an image at the pointer. A rake changes the weather of the painted body: bristles shear downstream, a remote band dries, another gathers wet load, and the next sweep inherits the changed current.

## Changed rule

v028–v031 repeatedly turned a gesture into a designed object: reservoir, radial mass, folded register, then closed polygon ribbon. v032 abandons those diagrammatic bodies. The first render is a full paper sheet made from eight intersecting gestures and 352 individual bristle paths. A valid drag is a rake through the material field, not a mark or pulse. Its consequence is carried by hundreds of actual fibre segments at a distance from the offered path.

## Visible consequence

The first frame must read as pigment deposited by a wide brush: uneven bristle density, dry gaps, pressure-weighted fibres, layered under-wash, and directional drift on paper. After a rake, the offered path disappears. A distant band shears, another thins, another swells, and later bristles inherit that changed flow. The interaction trace is temporary; the painted weather remains.

## Interaction

Drag across the paper and release. A weak, still, or over-held rake is refused. Enter, Space, and the rake control use a cycling deterministic sequence that does not stall when the visible weather memory reaches its limit. Delete lifts the latest rake and reconstructs the exact preceding weather, including after the visible three-rake window has rolled over. `R` releases the bounded weather memory. `S` exports a PNG.

## Cultural translation

- **Reference:** [p5.brush](https://github.com/acamposuribe/p5.brush)
- **Observed mechanism:** pressure, density, grain, and directional fields participate in producing a mark; a brush is a material instrument, not a decorative filter.
- **Mutine rule:** translate pressure into a changing weather field of bristles. Raking does not stamp a source-shaped mark; it changes remote fibre direction, drying, wet load, and later deposition.
- **Visible consequence:** hundreds of real line segments carry uneven pressure, dry gaps, and non-local drift on an unbroken paper field.
- **Anti-copy:** no p5.brush code, assets, brush presets, watercolor skin, hatching vocabulary, palette, composition, or API surface is imported or reproduced.

## Falsifier

Delete this spike if the first frame reads as a line chart, if bristles are only decorative noise over a fixed band, if the offered path remains as the main mark, if the remote weather cannot be seen without labels, or if later rakes rebuild from the seed instead of inheriting changed fibre geometry.

## Deletion condition

Do not promote v032 to a daily work until a blind first-render review, real pointer/keyboard/release test, five-viewport matrix, and independent perceptual critique all confirm that the material rule lives in the paint itself.
