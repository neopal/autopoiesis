BRUSH / BRISTLE WEATHER / v032

The paint carries
a weather system.

A rake does not leave a mark where it happens. It changes the pressure, dryness, and direction of distant fibres, and the next sweep inherits that weather.

The paper is holding its current.

SPIKE CONDITIONS

changed rule
Abandon the loop, sheet, reservoir, and radial mass. A full paper field of real bristles carries pressure as directional weather.
blind test
Hide labels, readout, hint, controls, and furniture. The image must read as deposited pigment on paper, not as a chart or interface.
deletion
Delete v032 if the bristles are decorative noise, the local rake remains visible, remote weather cannot be seen, or lift fails to restore the exact preceding field.
