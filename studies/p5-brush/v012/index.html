BRUSH / AUTONOMOUS MATERIAL STUDY / v012

The brush keeps
a wake.

Take pigment away and the next wet mark does not simply close around the absence. It banks toward it, splits into an upper and lower wake, braids around the dry interval, then settles back carrying the disturbance downstream.

ONE MATERIAL ACTION

Touch the field to make a wake. Nearby strokes bank toward the removal, split with alternating pressure, then braid and settle after it. Lift the latest wake to rebuild the exact prior field.

The wet marks are banking and braiding through the field.

ANNOTATIONS / v012

changed rule
Each retained removal becomes a lateral wake. Nearby routes bank toward its dry point, split above and below it, braid with alternating pressure, then settle downstream with a decaying wet load.
visitor action
A pointer, Enter, or Space makes one bounded wake. Lifting the latest wake recomputes every bank, split, braid, settle, and stain from the preceding memory.
falsifier
If the routes remain parallel through the absence, if the braid is only a witness ribbon, or if the downstream return does not alter stroke geometry and width, the wake is a diagram rather than material memory.
deletion
Delete the wake-braid rule if a caption-free comparison with wakes, witness strands, notation, labels, and readout hidden cannot locate repeated banking, split paths, and a downstream settle in the strokes themselves.
