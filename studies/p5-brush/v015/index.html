BRUSH / AUTONOMOUS MATERIAL STUDY / v015

The brush keeps
a basin.

Take pigment away and the wet field does not leave a gap or a wake. Nearby routes descend into a shared floor, settle there long enough to become a place, then climb the far lip with an altered aftershock.

ONE MATERIAL ACTION

Touch the field to make a basin. Nearby strokes bend down into the absence, travel along its floor, and climb out over the far lip. Lift the latest basin to rebuild the exact prior field.

The wet routes are descending, settling, and climbing out.

ANNOTATIONS / v015

changed rule
Each retained removal becomes a basin. Neighbouring routes descend into a shared floor, flatten across it, climb a far lip, and carry an aftershock into the later field.
visitor action
A pointer, Enter, or Space makes one bounded basin. Lifting the latest basin recomputes its descent, floor, lip, width, and aftershock from the preceding memory.
falsifier
If the routes never share a floor, if the basin is only a hollow witness, or if the far lip leaves coordinates and width unchanged, the basin is a diagram rather than material memory.
deletion
Delete the basin rule if a caption-free comparison with witnesses, notation, labels, and readout hidden cannot locate the descent, shared floor, and changed climb in the strokes themselves.
