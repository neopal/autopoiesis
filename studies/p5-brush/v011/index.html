BRUSH / AUTONOMOUS MATERIAL STUDY / v011

The brush keeps
a siphon.

Take pigment away and the next wet mark does not only part around the loss. It gathers toward a dry mouth, compresses into a brief throat, then fans back out carrying the absence downstream.

ONE MATERIAL ACTION

Touch the field to make a siphon. Nearby strokes gather toward the removal, share its narrow throat, then release with alternating pressure. Lift the latest siphon to rebuild the exact prior field.

The wet marks are gathering and releasing through the field.

ANNOTATIONS / v011

changed rule
Each retained removal becomes a signed capillary siphon. Nearby routes gather toward its mouth, compress into a shared throat, then fan downstream with a decaying release and altered wet load.
visitor action
A pointer, Enter, or Space makes one bounded siphon. Lifting the latest siphon recomputes every gather, throat, fan, and stain from the preceding memory.
falsifier
If the routes remain parallel through the mouth, if the throat is only a marker, or if the release is a ribbon over unchanged strokes, the siphon is a diagram rather than material memory.
deletion
Delete the siphon-release rule if a caption-free comparison with mouths, capillaries, notation, labels, and readout hidden cannot locate a repeated gather, compression, and downstream fan in the strokes themselves.
