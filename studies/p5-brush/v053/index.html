BRUSH / TENSION MEMBRANE / v053

The paint
dries toward the pressure.

A passage across the skin is not a line to keep. Drag from an edge to another, let the pressure settle, and release: the whole membrane leans toward the crossing. The hand disappears; the drying remains.

ONE CONTINUOUS SKIN

Pointer or touch: begin near one edge, cross the membrane, and release near another edge. A settled passage changes the entire continuous skin rather than leaving a local stroke. ArrowLeft or ArrowRight chooses a deterministic crossing; Enter or Space performs it. Delete lifts the latest crossing; Escape, R, or “dry the membrane” returns to the quiet skin. S downloads a PNG.

The membrane is quiet. Cross it, then let the pressure settle.

ANNOTATIONS / v053

changed rule
Pressure is not a mark. A settled edge-to-edge passage re-produces the entire membrane along its direction and becomes part of the next drying state.
visitor action
Cross the continuous skin from one edge to another and settle before releasing. Four crossings compound in order and can be lifted exactly.
falsifier
If a passage leaves a local stripe, commits without crossing, or changes only colour and readout, the pressure has not become material agency.
deletion
Delete v053 if the blind view reads as a decorated field, if crossing order is cosmetic, if pressure does not settle, or if lifting cannot restore the preceding skin.
