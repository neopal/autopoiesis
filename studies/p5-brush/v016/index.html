BRUSH / MATERIAL BREAK / v016

The brush keeps
a cut.

The route field is gone. A pressure gesture opens a finite plate: cells lift along the incision, the remainder gathers into a raised ridge, and the next grain turns around what was removed.

ONE MATERIAL ACTION

Drag across the plate. Speed and length become pressure. The cut is not a line painted on top: it changes cell angle, lift, grain, edge, and pigment on both sides. Lift only the latest cut to rebuild the exact prior plate.

The plate is holding its layered pressure.

ANNOTATIONS / v016

changed rule
A remembered removal becomes a pressure cut. The plate resolves it as a real incision and a displaced ridge; cells on both sides pivot, lift, and alter their grain.
visitor action
Drag, press Enter, or press Space to cut. A drag path is bounded and replayable; Delete or “lift latest” restores the preceding plate exactly.
falsifier
If the cut is only a dark stroke, if the ridge does not move material, or if cells keep the same angle and lift, the plate is decoration rather than remembered matter.
deletion
Delete v016 if a caption-free comparison with labels, controls, readout, and witnesses hidden still reads as the old horizontal route grammar instead of a split, lifted, pressure-shaped plate.
