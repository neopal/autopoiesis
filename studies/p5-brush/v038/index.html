BRUSH / LAMINA STACK / v038

The paint opens
between layers.

Pressure is not a mark. Choose the seam where it enters and the seam where it leaves; the interval becomes a material event.

THE GAP IS THE INSTRUMENT

Choose two non-adjacent seams around the stack. The first choice stays still. The second makes a pressure span: one lamina peels, another seals, and a third sheds its underside. Enter or “pinch interval” uses the deterministic pair. Delete lifts the latest; Escape, R, or “release layers” returns to the quiet stack.

The layers are held together.

ANNOTATIONS / v038

changed rule
Switch from a drawn pressure trace to a paired seam interval. Pressure acts between two chosen boundaries and changes the stack's layer order rather than copying the gesture.
visitor action
Choose two non-adjacent seams. The first choice is geometrically neutral; the second commits a peel, seal, and shed. Enter, Space, and the pinch control use a deterministic interval. Delete lifts the latest. Escape or R releases the layers.
falsifier
If a single seam commits, if the touched seams become the only answer, if only colour or labels change, if the layer roles do not persist, or if lifting is inexact, the material translation fails.
deletion
Delete v038 if caption-free viewing cannot show a stack of separate material skins with a real interval opening, or if it reads as a decorated menu, generic card grid, or reskinned crease sheet.
