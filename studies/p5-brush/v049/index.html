The sheet holds
a pressure chord.

One-sided pressure is refused. When both edges hold together, duration opens a new fold inside the sheet; the next chord must begin from the other side.

MATERIAL DOES NOT KEEP A SINGLE HAND

Press the left and right edge sites together, hold beyond the quiet threshold, and release. A short hold, a single side, or the same lead twice is refused. Pointer, touch, ArrowLeft plus ArrowRight, or “hold the chord” offer equivalent paths. Delete lifts the latest fold; Escape, R, or “release the sheet” returns the quiet seed. S downloads a PNG.

The sheet is quiet. Two edges must hold together.

ANNOTATIONS / v049

changed rule
Abandon the wash, island, and through-void passage. A single hand is inert. A long alternating two-site chord changes the next internal fold, while the pressure sites remain exact.
visitor action
Press both edge sites, wait beyond the material threshold, and release. The next accepted chord must lead from the opposite side; four fold states compound and remain liftable.
falsifier
If one-sided pressure commits, if a short hold commits, if the edge sites deform, if a fold changes only by colour or readout, or if the second chord ignores the alternating lead, the rule fails.
deletion
Delete v049 if the blind sheet reads as another field, monolith, or route diagram, if duration is only a button label, or if lifting cannot reconstruct the exact prior fold.
