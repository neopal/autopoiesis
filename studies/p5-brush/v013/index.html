BRUSH / AUTONOMOUS MATERIAL STUDY / v013

The brush keeps
a dry seam.

Take pigment away and the wet field refuses to close the wound. Nearby routes bend toward both banks, lose a finite interval, then return downstream carrying a quieter, displaced load.

ONE MATERIAL ACTION

Touch the field to make a seam. Nearby strokes approach the removal, leave a true gap, and re-enter after it with a changed offset. Lift the latest seam to rebuild the exact prior field.

The wet marks are approaching and re-entering through the field.

ANNOTATIONS / v013

changed rule
Each retained removal becomes a drying seam. Neighboring routes bank toward its two edges, compress at the banks, leave a finite dry gap, then re-enter downstream with a decaying offset and reduced wet load.
visitor action
A pointer, Enter, or Space makes one bounded seam. Lifting the latest seam recomputes every bank, gap, re-entry, and stain from the preceding memory.
falsifier
If the marks remain continuous through the absence, if the gap is only a dark witness, or if re-entry leaves route coordinates and width unchanged, the seam is a diagram rather than material memory.
deletion
Delete the dry-seam rule if a caption-free comparison with witnesses, notation, labels, and readout hidden cannot locate repeated approach, finite gaps, and changed re-entry in the strokes themselves.
