BRUSH / AUTONOMOUS MATERIAL STUDY / v006

The brush leaves
a wet return.

Take pigment away and the next wet mark does not simply split. It detours, keeps the bend, and comes back carrying a new pressure.

ONE MATERIAL ACTION

Touch the field to dry a seam. Later strokes bend around that absence, remain displaced, and pool as they return. Lift the latest seam to rebuild the exact prior field.

The wet marks are carrying their delayed return.

ANNOTATIONS / v006

changed rule
Each retained removal becomes a delayed return seam. A later stroke bends around its dry mouth, travels on a displaced course, then re-enters with a pooled pressure change.
visitor action
A pointer, Enter, or the action rail makes one bounded seam. Lifting the latest seam recomputes every route and pressure field from the preceding memory.
falsifier
If the later strokes do not share the displaced return, or if the route only kinks at the witness mark, the seam is merely an ornament.
deletion
Delete the delayed-return rule if a caption-free comparison cannot locate the changed course in the strokes themselves after the seam witnesses are hidden.
