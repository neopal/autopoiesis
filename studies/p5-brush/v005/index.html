BRUSH / AUTONOMOUS MATERIAL STUDY / v005

The brush remembers
its missing half.

Take pigment away and the next wet mark does not close the gap. It divides, carries the deficit across both banks, and returns with one side heavier.

ONE MATERIAL ACTION

Touch the field to remove pigment. Later marks split around that mouth and reunite with unequal density. Lift the latest removal to rebuild the exact prior field.

The wet marks are still carrying the exchange.

ANNOTATIONS / v005

changed rule
Each retained removal becomes a split delta. A later stroke forks around its dry mouth, then recombines with pigment and width exchanged between its two banks.
visitor action
A pointer, Enter, or the action rail removes one bounded patch. Lifting the latest removal recomputes every route and bank from the preceding memory.
falsifier
If an empty-memory replay or latest-removal lift leaves the fork, route shift, and bank density unchanged, the removal is only a drawn witness.
deletion
Delete the split-delta rule if the dry mouths explain the work more clearly than the visible fork and unequal return in a caption-free comparison.
