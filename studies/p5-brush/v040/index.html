BRUSH / SETTLING ARCHIVE / v040

The stain falls through
its own absence.

A fragment can be lifted out, but the empty place does not stay empty. The remaining paint carries the loss as weight and settles into a new arrangement.

REMOVAL BECOMES GRAVITY

Press a material fragment, drag upward, and release outside the archive. The press is only a lift preview. A valid upward exit removes that fragment and makes the neighbouring mass settle through the missing support. Enter or “lift a fragment” uses the deterministic lift. Delete lifts the latest absence; Escape, R, or “release archive” restores the quiet arrangement.

The fragments are suspended in their quiet weight.

ANNOTATIONS / v040
