BRUSH / DRY-SEAM LAMINATE / v033

The paint sets
the wrong seam.

Three knocks are offered to the surface. None of them becomes a mark. The laminate chooses a distant hinge, counterweight, and dry void instead.

A TRIPLET, NOT A STROKE

Tap the field three times without dragging. The first two knocks are only a held rhythm. On the third, material resistance resolves the rhythm elsewhere: a remote plate hinges, another counters it, and a third opens a dry void. Later triplets inherit the moved laminate; Delete reconstructs the exact preceding state.

The seam is refusing the offered points.
