# Brush v033 — The paint sets the wrong seam.

## Hypothesis
If material agency can parse rhythm rather than simply absorb pressure, then three quiet knocks should become a structural decision made somewhere else: the touched points remain empty while a remote hinge, counterweight, and dry void change the producing laminate.

## Changed rule
v029 made stillness gather a radial sediment mass, v030 made a bounded pulse buckle a folded sheet, and v031 made a drag refuse a local mark inside a closed ribbon. v033 abandons continuous drag, waiting, pressure, and the broad painted body. The work is a suspended laminate of nine irregular plates crossed by one crooked seam. A single tap does not count; a complete three-knock rhythm is parsed as an event. The material relocates its consequence into three remote plate geometries.

## Visible consequence
The blind first render is a singular suspended laminate: nine uneven painted plates held around a bright, crooked dry seam. After a valid triplet, the offered points remain visually unmarked. One remote plate hinges, another counters it, and a third becomes a dark outlined void. The gesture is not retained as a trace; later triplets act on the altered laminate.

## Interaction
Tap the field three times without dragging. The first two taps arm a rhythm but leave geometry unchanged. The third commits the triplet. Dragging, a tap shorter than 35ms, and a hold longer than 420ms are refused. Enter, Space, and the knock control use deterministic triplets. Delete or “lift latest” reconstructs the exact preceding laminate. `Escape` or `R` releases memory. `S` downloads the current SVG.

## Cultural translation
- **Reference:** [p5.brush](https://github.com/acamposuribe/p5.brush)
- **Observed mechanism:** pressure, density, grain, and directional fields are programmable material forces that participate in producing geometry rather than decorating finished geometry.
- **Mutine translation:** explicitly invert the recent Brush sequence. Refuse brush marks, watercolor, hatching, grain, and library vocabulary; translate material agency into a discrete threshold system. Rhythm is the material input, and resistance relocates the result to a hinge, counterweight, and dry void around a seam.
- **Visible consequence:** three actual SVG plate paths and the seam change only after the third knock; the touched points do not receive a mark, and later triplets inherit the altered geometry.
- **Anti-copy:** no p5.brush brush, watercolor appearance, hatching vocabulary, source palette, source composition, example, or API surface is reproduced.

## Repeated grammar refused
The last three Brush works all used a broad painted body that absorbed a continuous visitor offer: v029 used stillness over a radial mass, v030 used a bounded pressure pulse over a folded sheet, and v031 used drag-and-release inside a negative ribbon. v033 changes both medium and encounter: native SVG plates and a discrete three-knock rhythm replace Canvas 2D bodies and continuous gestures. The viewer must build a sequence before the material answers.

## Falsifier
The translation fails if one tap changes geometry, if dragging commits, if the touched plate is always the changed plate, if the seam or plates only change colour/readout, if fewer than three actual plate paths change, if later triplets restart from the seed laminate, or if the blind field reads as a generic grid or interface.

## Deletion condition
Delete v033 if labels-off and controls-off viewing cannot show a singular suspended laminate with a consequential dry seam, if the three remote roles are not materially visible after a triplet, or if lifting the latest triplet does not restore the exact preceding laminate.
