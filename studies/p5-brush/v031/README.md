# Brush v031 — The paint declines to become a mark.

## Hypothesis
If material agency can remember pressure by refusing a local mark, then a bounded stroke should leave an absence somewhere else in the producing body: one remote segment thins, another swells as counterweight, and a third keeps the scar.

## Changed rule
v028 made a mend transfer a wound through a continuous reservoir, v029 made stillness gather a radial sediment mass, and v030 made a quick pulse buckle a vertical folded sheet. v031 abandons the wound, the radial field, and the sheet. The work is one closed irregular paint ribbon. A drag is an offer, not a drawing instruction: the offered location is refused, and the material translates pressure into a remote subtraction, a counterweight, and a scar in three actual ribbon segments. Four negative strokes remain in bounded memory.

## Visible consequence
The blind first render is one luminous, looped paint body with a deep central opening, not a population, a reservoir, a radial chart, or a folded register. After a valid offer, the place that received the pointer does not receive a mark. A remote ribbon segment visibly thins, another swells outward, and a third kinks. The gesture trace disappears on release; only the altered body remains.

## Interaction
Drag across the field and release. A stroke shorter than 140ms, longer than 1100ms, or nearly still is refused. Enter, Space, and the offer control use deterministic strokes. Delete or “lift latest” reconstructs the exact preceding ribbon. `R` releases the bounded absence memory. `S` exports a PNG.

## Cultural translation
- **Reference:** [p5.brush](https://github.com/acamposuribe/p5.brush)
- **Observed mechanism:** pressure, density, grain, and directional fields are programmable material forces that participate in producing geometry rather than decorating finished geometry.
- **Mutine translation:** invert the recent pressure-debt sequence. Refuse brushes, watercolor, hatching, grain, and library vocabulary; treat the stroke as an unfulfilled offer. Material resistance moves the consequence away from the offered point and produces a remote subtraction, counterweight, and scar in a closed ribbon.
- **Visible consequence:** the loop's actual segment polygons change at three non-local roles; the offered point stays unmarked, and later offers replay against the changed ribbon.
- **Anti-copy:** no p5.brush brush, watercolor appearance, hatching vocabulary, source palette, source composition, example, or API surface is reproduced.

## Repeated grammar refused
The last three Brush works repeated material as a broad painted body that absorbs a visitor gesture: v028 was a wound-bearing reservoir, v029 a radial stillness mass, and v030 a folded pressure register. v031 makes a perceptual and encounter switch: one closed loop instead of a field/sheet, drag-and-release instead of mending/waiting/pulsing, and negative displacement instead of visible local accumulation. The viewer cannot make a mark where they act.

## Falsifier
The translation fails if the offered point directly selects the changed segment, if the pointer gesture remains as a decorative trace, if only a readout or colour changes, if fewer than three actual segment geometries change, if later offers restart from the seed ribbon, or if the blind field reads as a generic loop or interface diagram.

## Deletion condition
Delete v031 if labels-off and controls-off viewing cannot show one authored loop with a consequential absence, if a valid offer does not change one remote subtraction, one counterweight, and one scar in the ribbon geometry, or if lifting the latest offer does not restore the exact preceding loop.
