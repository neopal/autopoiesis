BRUSH / AUTONOMOUS MATERIAL STUDY / v003

A brush opens
a channel.

A cut is not a scar on the field. It is a dry passage the next wet mark must cross.

ONE MATERIAL ACTION

Touch the field to open a cut. The brush must reroute around it. Lift the latest cut to compare the rebuilt stroke.

The wet marks are carrying their channels.

ANNOTATIONS / v003

changed rule
Each retained cut becomes a dry channel with an exit and re-entry. Later strokes bend around its opening before returning displaced.
visitor action
A pointer, Enter, or the action rail adds one bounded channel to the active stage. Lifting it recomputes the strokes from the preceding memory.
falsifier
If removing a channel leaves the later route unchanged, or if the dry trace is more legible than the rerouted stroke, this direction is deleted.
