# Brush v018 — departure fold

## Status

**Candidate / held.** This is the 2026-09-24 daily tableau for the Brush current. It remains held until a caption-free comparison confirms that the continuous membrane and its far-side fold read as material memory rather than a polished hole-and-ribbon diagram.

## Hypothesis

If paint remembers removal as a refusal, the material should not answer the visitor's approach. It should wait for departure, open the front skin, and carry the missing load to a visible back-side fold.

## Repeated grammar refused

The last three Brush works moved through route fields, a tiled pressure plate, and a sparse population of islands. Their repeated encounter was approach, incision, or transfer between visible bodies. v018 refuses those grammars: the first render is one continuous membrane, and the causal gesture is a discrete leave rather than a proximity field or drawing action.

## Changed rule

v018 inverts `little-critters`' reciprocal attention. The material does not turn toward the visitor. A pointer entering and moving only arms a witness; leaving commits the event. The membrane then changes its own geometry: an actual aperture is cut through the front skin, the surrounding surface buckles, and the displaced load rises as a far-side back-fold. The viewer's absence is the material event.

## Visible consequence

The blind first render is a single asymmetrical sheet with a raised rear edge and layered pigment. After a departure, the sheet contains a real hole whose edge changes the surrounding contour, while a distant folded flap grows and turns. Repeated departures accumulate bounded apertures. Lifting the latest departure reconstructs the exact prior membrane.

## Interaction and reversibility

Enter the field and hold still. Leave the canvas to register one bounded departure. Enter or Space commits the armed witness for keyboard and assistive paths; Delete/Backspace or “lift latest” removes only the latest departure. `release sequence` clears visitor memory and restarts the autonomous sequence. The engine is pure, seeded, and replayable.

## Cultural translation

- **Reference:** `little-critters` — https://github.com/GordenSun/little-critters
- **Observed mechanism:** situated agents change attention through proximity and look back, making the visitor part of a reciprocal encounter rather than a dashboard operator.
- **Mutine translation:** invert reciprocity in the Brush current: the surface refuses to answer an approach and only acts when the visitor departs, opening its front and lifting the removed load to its back.
- **Visible consequence:** leaving causes a structural aperture and surrounding buckle in one continuous membrane, plus a non-local rear fold; hovering alone does not commit a change.
- **Falsifier:** if pointer leave is indistinguishable from hover, if the aperture is only a dark sticker, or if the rear fold is only a line or colour shift, the inversion fails.
- **Anti-copy:** no animals, characters, paper style, head-turning scene, source palette, composition, or surface vocabulary is reproduced.
- **Direction consequence:** this closes the Brush current's route/basin/pressure-plate/island grammar and opens a temporal absence direction: departure as a state transition, continuous skin as medium, and front/back as memory.

## Falsifier

If a caption-free viewer sees a decorative abstract sheet, if the event can be triggered without departure, if the far fold cannot be located in the geometry, or if undo does not restore the exact prior surface, v018 is a failed inversion.

## Deletion condition

Delete v018 if a labels-off, controls-off comparison still reads as v017's island population, v016's pressure plate, or any earlier horizontal route field. Do not rescue the direction with annotations, witness marks, colour coding, or a new title.
