BRUSH / MATERIAL BREAK / v017

The brush keeps
a vacancy.

The plate has broken into an archipelago. Approach one island and its material withdraws, arrives elsewhere, and leaves a true porous place behind.

ONE MATERIAL RELATION

Move near an island or press Enter. The nearest body yields pigment to a distant body while a third island hollows out. The gesture is proximity, not painting; lift only the latest encounter to rebuild the exact prior archipelago.

The islands are keeping their separate weight.

ANNOTATIONS / v017
