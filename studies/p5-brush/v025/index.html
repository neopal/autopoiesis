A HINGE, NOT A STROKE

Click one seam in the hanging membrane. The event commits immediately: the material below that seam folds through depth, and the next hinge inherits the displaced surface. Delete or “lift latest” reconstructs the exact preceding membrane.

The membrane is hanging under its own weight.

ANNOTATIONS / v025

changed rule
Replace v024’s flat two-endpoint removal with a single spatial hinge. A click on one material seam folds every lower row in depth; later hinges are applied to the already displaced membrane.
visitor action
Click a seam, or use Enter/Space for the seeded next hinge. Delete lifts only the latest fold. R releases the bounded hinge memory. S exports the current view.
falsifier
If a hinge changes only colour or a cursor marker, if lower rows do not move in real geometry, or if a later fold begins from the original flat state, the material rule is absent.
deletion
Delete v025 if labels-off and controls-off viewing reads as a generic card stack or flat ribbon, if the depth fold is not visible without prose, or if the membrane forgets its previous load.
