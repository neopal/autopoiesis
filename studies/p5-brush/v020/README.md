# Brush v020 — drying coil

## Status

**Candidate / held.** This is the 2026-09-26 daily tableau for the Brush current. It remains held until a caption-free comparison confirms that the fractured helical band and the deflected wet bands read as one material law rather than a decorative 3D ornament.

## Hypothesis

If paint remembers removal as a change in how the next mark is made, drying should not be a texture or an opacity fade. A drying event should fracture one continuous material band and force the still-wet bands to bend around the new seam.

## Repeated grammar refused

The last three Brush works used an archipelago of islands, a continuous membrane with a back-fold, and a hanging ordinal register. Their repeated image grammar was planar material arranged as surfaces, gaps, bars, or isolated bodies; their repeated encounter was proximity, departure, or wheel/keyboard stepping. v020 changes medium and encounter: it is a WebGL coil with a central volume, and the visitor sends a discrete drying pulse that changes the production law of later bands.

## Changed rule

v020 translates p5.brush's programmable material field into a thresholded drying engine. One pulse does not recolour a band. It converts one helical band from coherent wet continuity into separated dry fragments, then deflects the remaining wet bands around that seam. The dry seam is therefore a causal obstruction inside the coil, not a surface drawn on top of it.

## Visible consequence

The blind first render is a suspended three-dimensional coil of helical pigment bands, not a sheet, register, or island field. After a pulse, one band has actual breaks and floating dry fragments, while neighbouring bands kink and shift in phase and radius. Repeated pulses accumulate bounded seams; lifting the latest pulse reconstructs the exact preceding coil.

## Interaction and reversibility

Press the pulse button, `D`, `Enter`, or `Space` to dry one bounded band. Delete/Backspace or “lift latest” removes only the latest seam. `R` or “release coil” clears visitor memory and restarts the deterministic sequence. The engine is pure, seeded, and replayable; pointer position is not part of the visual state.

## Cultural translation

- **Reference:** `p5-brush` — https://github.com/acamposuribe/p5.brush
- **Observed mechanism:** a programmable brush engine treats pressure, density, grain, hatching, and vector fields as geometry-producing behaviour rather than as a cosmetic skin.
- **Mutine translation:** make drying the antagonist field. A discrete pulse fractures one helical band into dry fragments and bends later wet bands around the fracture; the material rule changes how future geometry is produced.
- **Visible consequence:** the work switches from planar surface/register to a WebGL coil, from pointer proximity/departure/wheel to a discrete pulse, and from a removed mark to a causal seam that deflects future bands.
- **Falsifier:** if a pulse only changes hue, opacity, labels, or decorative particles, if the selected band remains continuous, or if neighbouring bands keep their original phase and radius, the translation fails.
- **Anti-copy:** no p5.brush brushes, watercolor appearance, hatching vocabulary, API surface, palette, composition, or source examples are reproduced.
- **Direction consequence:** closes Brush's recent planar absence and ordinal-load grammar; opens a material-field direction in which drying is a spatial obstruction and the next mark must route around what was lost.

## Falsifier

If a caption-free viewer sees only a rotating wire sculpture, if a dry seam is only a colour change, if later bands do not visibly deflect, or if undo fails to restore the exact prior coil, v020 is a failed direction change.

## Deletion condition

Delete v020 if labels-off, controls-off viewing still reads as v019's register, v018's membrane, or an embellished generic 3D torus. Do not rescue the fracture with annotations, readouts, or a title.

## Determinism

Seed: `0x42525540`. Base coil, autonomous pulses, band selection, dry fragment placement, deflection, and visitor pulse directions are deterministic. Repeated pulse → pulse → lift sequences are replayable.

This is a candidate / held daily work. Engine and source checks are evidence of structure, not proof of visual success. Independent caption-free perceptual review and production readback remain explicit gates.
