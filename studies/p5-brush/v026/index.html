BRUSH / ANTAGONIST FIELD / v026

The paint sets
its own limit.

A pressure sample does not mark the place you touch. It moves the rule that makes the whole slab.

A SAMPLE, NOT A STROKE

Click the slab once. The material samples that pressure, then recasts its global threshold: dry mass, shear, and density change across the entire panel. A later sample meets the already recast field. Delete or “lift latest” reconstructs the exact preceding slab.

The slab is holding its own threshold.

ANNOTATIONS / v026

changed rule
Replace v025’s local seam hinge with a global pressure threshold. One sampled point changes the production law of every cell in the slab; subsequent samples act on the recast field.
visitor action
Click one point, or use Enter/Space for the seeded next sample. Delete lifts only the latest threshold event. R releases the bounded material memory. S exports the current view.
falsifier
If a sample only changes a local mark, cursor halo, or label, if the field does not recast globally, or if later samples begin from the baseline slab, the material rule is absent.
deletion
Delete v026 if labels-off and controls-off viewing reads as a generic grid, if the threshold is only an annotation, or if the blind slab cannot show a changed production law without prose.
