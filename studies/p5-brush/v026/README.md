# Brush v026 — The paint sets its own limit.

## Hypothesis
If material agency is real, a pressure sample should alter the rule that produces the whole pigment slab rather than leave a mark at the sampled point.

## Changed rule
v025 made one addressed seam hinge the membrane below it. v026 replaces that local spatial hinge with a global threshold field: one sampled pressure recasts every cell's mass, dryness, shear, and lift. Later samples are replayed on the already recast slab and the archive is bounded to four threshold events.

## Visible consequence
The blind first render is a stratified, irregular pigment slab with a diagonal threshold boundary, not a hanging surface or connected route. After a sample, the threshold moves and the whole slab changes its density, dry grain, and shear. The place sampled is not painted locally.

## Interaction
Click once on the slab to sample pressure. Enter, Space, and the sample control use deterministic seeded samples. Delete or “lift latest” reconstructs the exact preceding slab. `R` releases the bounded threshold archive. `S` exports a PNG.

## Cultural translation
- Reference: [p5.brush](https://github.com/acamposuribe/p5.brush)
- Observed mechanism: pressure, density, grain, and directional fields are material forces that change how geometry is produced, not a cosmetic skin.
- Mutine translation: treat pressure as an antagonist threshold that recasts the production law of the whole slab; the material answers globally instead of copying a local brush mark.
- Anti-copy: no p5.brush brushes, watercolor appearance, hatching vocabulary, source palette, composition, examples, or API surface are reproduced.

## Falsifier
The translation fails if a sample only changes a local mark, cursor halo, or label; if the global field does not recast; if later samples restart from the baseline; or if the blind image reads as a generic grid.

## Deletion condition
Delete v026 if labels-off and controls-off viewing cannot show the moved threshold and changed production law in the slab itself, or if lifting the latest event does not reconstruct the exact preceding state.
