The stain answers
from elsewhere.

The patch you approach does not take the mark. It stays exact. Only an untouched respondent elsewhere dries a new edge, as if pigment had remembered the attention without keeping the hand.

ATTENTION IS A MATERIAL FORCE

Move close to one patch and tap or click it. The visited patch remains unchanged; an unvisited patch answers by gaining a real dry notch and closing a seam in the field. Pointer, touch, ArrowRight then Enter, or “attend to a patch” offer equivalent paths. Delete lifts the latest response; Escape, R, or “release the field” returns the quiet seed. S downloads a PNG.

The field is quiet. Attention must find a patch.
