BRUSH / AUTONOMOUS MATERIAL STUDY / v008

The brush shares
the pigment.

Take pigment away and the next wet mark does not merely bend around the loss. The absence becomes porous: neighbouring strokes trade pressure across it, and the surface keeps the imbalance.

ONE MATERIAL ACTION

Touch the field to make a membrane. Later strokes exchange pigment sideways across the dry point: one lane thins as its neighbour thickens. Lift the latest membrane to rebuild the exact prior field.

The wet marks are sharing the remembered absence.

ANNOTATIONS / v008

changed rule
Each retained removal becomes a porous membrane. Later strokes on either side exchange pigment through it, altering their route, width, and wetting in opposite signs rather than making one local detour.
visitor action
A pointer, Enter, or Space makes one bounded membrane. Lifting the latest membrane recomputes every route, exchange value, and wetting field from the preceding memory.
falsifier
If the neighbouring strokes do not show an opposite gain/loss pair beyond the dry point, or if the membrane only adds a halo, the exchange is a diagram rather than material memory.
deletion
Delete the porous-exchange rule if a caption-free comparison with membrane witnesses and notation hidden cannot locate a shared sideways transfer in the strokes themselves.
