# Brush v030 — The paint stores a pressure debt.

## Hypothesis
If material agency can remember pressure without receiving a drawn line, then a bounded pulse should become a debt in the producing body: one fold buckles, a distant fold lifts, and a third seam keeps the scar.

## Changed rule
v027 made a moving scrape fracture a population, v028 made a route mend a continuous reservoir, and v029 rewarded stillness with radial sediment. v030 refuses all three grammars. The canvas is one suspended pigment sheet made from nine broad register folds. A touch is only an entry point. A pressure pulse between 120ms and 560ms is transmitted by the material's resistance to three non-local folds: a local buckle, a remote lift, and a third seam scar. A weak pulse and an over-held pulse are both refused. Four pressure debts remain in bounded memory.

## Visible consequence
The blind first render is a tall folded sheet with a dark underside seam, not a radial mass, wound reservoir, or island population. After a valid pulse, actual fold polygons change at three distinct positions and the underside thickens. The transient pressure ring disappears on release; the sheet carries the consequence.

## Interaction
Press and release the field. A short pulse is too weak; waiting too long is refused. Enter, Space, and the pulse control use deterministic pressure events. Delete or “lift latest” reconstructs the exact preceding sheet. `R` releases the bounded pressure memory. `S` exports a PNG.

## Cultural translation
- **Reference:** [p5.brush](https://github.com/acamposuribe/p5.brush)
- **Observed mechanism:** pressure, density, grain, and directional fields participate in producing geometry rather than decorating finished geometry.
- **Mutine translation:** refuse brush marks, watercolor, hatching, and library vocabulary. Translate material agency into a pressure debt: a bounded pulse enters at one point, but resistance redistributes its consequence across a local buckle, a remote lift, and a third seam scar.
- **Visible consequence:** the single folded sheet changes its actual polygon silhouettes at three non-local folds; later pulses replay against those changed folds.
- **Anti-copy:** no p5.brush brush, watercolor appearance, hatching vocabulary, source palette, composition, examples, or API surface is reproduced.

## Repeated grammar refused
The last three Brush works repeated a moving or waiting visitor gesture acting on a broad painted field: v027 was a detached-island scrape, v028 a wound-bearing mending reservoir, and v029 a radial sediment mass produced by stillness. v030 changes the encounter to a bounded quick pulse, the composition to one vertical folded register sheet, and the memory model to pressure debt that is redistributed through an underside seam. It deliberately refuses both waiting and direct location selection.

## Falsifier
The translation fails if waiting is rewarded, if the pointer directly selects the buckle, if a pressure ring/readout is the only change, if fewer than three fold geometries change, if later pulses restart from the seed sheet, or if the blind field reads as a generic strip or chart.

## Deletion condition
Delete v030 if labels-off and controls-off viewing cannot show one authored folded sheet, if a valid pulse does not change three actual fold silhouettes, or if lifting the latest pulse does not reconstruct the exact preceding register.
