BRUSH / PRESSURE DEBT / v030

The paint stores
a pressure debt.

A quick touch enters the sheet. Its resistance decides where the fold will buckle, where the far edge will lift, and which seam keeps the scar.

A PULSE, NOT A WAIT

Press and release before the pressure goes slack. The point is only an entry; the material chooses three different folds. A short pulse is too weak, a long hold is refused. Later pulses inherit the changed sheet. Delete or “lift latest” reconstructs the exact preceding register.

The sheet is carrying its own weight.

ANNOTATIONS / v030

changed rule
Abandon the radial mass and the dwell. A single folded sheet accepts a bounded pressure pulse; resistance produces a local buckle, a non-local lift, and a third seam scar in actual fold geometry.
visitor action
Press and release the field between 120ms and 560ms. Enter, Space, and the pulse control use deterministic pressure events. Delete lifts only the latest pulse. R releases the bounded pressure memory. S exports a PNG.
falsifier
If waiting is rewarded, if the pointer directly selects the buckle, if only a ring or readout changes, or if later pulses restart from the seed sheet, the material rule is absent.
deletion
Delete v030 if labels-off and controls-off viewing reads as a generic strip or chart, if three fold geometries do not change after a pulse, or if lifting the latest pulse fails to restore the exact preceding sheet.
