# Brush v044 — The brush keeps what the field cannot carry.

## Hypothesis

If a brush is a field engine rather than a surface effect, the field should be able to oppose a hand and classify the pressure that crosses it. The hand should not be painted; the strata should change according to the force that the threshold carries.

## Changed rule

v044 abandons v043's monolithic witness body, v042's bounded dwell cure plate, and v041's remote relief membrane. The work is a horizontal stack of produced pigment strata with a visible pressure threshold. A traverse must begin at the left edge, cross the threshold, and leave at the right edge. The gesture stays geometry-neutral until release. A committed traverse is classified as `shear` or `powder` from its pressure and changes other strata through actual cut and deposit geometry. The field is one-way: a return crossing is refused. Four etches compound in replay state.

## Visible consequence

The blind view is a broad layered field, not a body, relief landscape, or raster plate. The pressure threshold is a narrow antagonist inside the strata. During a scrape, the hand path is only a temporary trace and the produced field remains exact. After release, a distant layer gains an actual cut and another gains a deposit; repeated traverses build a discontinuous archive of pressure events.

## Interaction

- Pointer/touch: press near the left edge, cross the pressure field, and release beyond the right edge.
- An interior start, short traverse, reversed crossing, or threshold miss refuses without geometry change.
- `Enter` / `Space`: deterministic left-to-right traverse.
- `Delete` / `Backspace`: lift the latest etch.
- `Escape` / `R`: release the field to the quiet seed.
- `S`: save a PNG.

## Falsifier

Delete this direction if the hand path is painted, if geometry changes before release, if a short or reversed traverse commits, if the pressure threshold is merely a visual annotation, if the field answers at the cursor, or if shear and powder do not produce visibly different stratum geometry.

## Deletion condition

Delete v044 if caption-free viewing reads as a generic striped landscape or cursor trail, or if a committed traverse cannot be distinguished from a colour flash or readout change.

## Cultural translation

The selected `p5-brush` mechanism is a programmable material engine: pressure, density, grain, and vector fields alter how a mark is produced rather than decorating a finished shape. Mutine refuses p5.brush's brush presets, watercolor appearance, hatching vocabulary, palette, source code, API surface, examples, and surface style. It translates the field principle into a one-way pressure threshold whose carried force edits other strata as cuts and deposits. The hand supplies a crossing; the material decides the form.

## Radical progression gate

Across v041, v042, and v043 the repeated gestures were a continuous membrane, a timed raster plate, and a monolithic body accepting a two-site call. v044 changes at least two perceptual dimensions before explanation: representation/medium becomes layered Canvas strata instead of one surface or body, composition becomes a horizontal pressure field instead of an oblique or vertical object, and the encounter becomes a one-way edge-to-edge traverse whose pressure classifies material production rather than a dwell or reciprocal site exchange. The stable invariant that the pointer location receives the answer is removed: the hand path never becomes pigment.

## Direction consequence

This closes the witness-body / dwell / relief sequence and opens counterfield etch: brush material is an antagonist that interprets force across a threshold. It is not a declared new period; a connected experiment and a retrospective are still required.
