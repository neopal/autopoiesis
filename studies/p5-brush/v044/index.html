BRUSH / COUNTERFIELD ETCH / v044

The brush keeps
what the field cannot carry.

The hand is not painted. Cross the pressure field and the strata decide whether to shear or powderize elsewhere. The threshold is an antagonist: the field remembers the force that crossed it, not the path that was drawn.

PRESSURE HAS A ONE-WAY COST

Begin near the left edge and scrape across the layered field. While the gesture is moving, the produced strata stay exact. Only a complete left-to-right traverse that crosses the central pressure threshold commits. The field classifies the gesture as shear or powder, then changes distant strata through actual cuts and deposits. Enter or “make a traverse” uses a deterministic traverse; Delete lifts the latest etch; Escape, R, or “release field” returns the quiet seed. S downloads a PNG.

The strata are quiet. The field is waiting at the left edge.

ANNOTATIONS / v044

changed rule
Abandon the monolithic body, timed stillness, remote membrane, and two-site call. The visitor must cross a visible pressure threshold from left to right; only release commits a field-classified shear or powder event in other strata.
visitor action
Press near the left edge, cross the pressure bands, and release beyond the right edge. A short scrape, interior start, reversed crossing, or threshold miss refuses without geometry change. Pointer, touch, Enter, Space, Delete, Escape, R, and the controls provide equivalent routes.
falsifier
If the hand path is painted, if geometry changes before release, if a short or reversed scrape commits, if the threshold is only an annotation, or if shear and powder are cosmetic labels rather than produced strata, the translation fails.
deletion
Delete v044 if caption-free viewing reads as a generic striped landscape, if the field has no visible antagonist, or if a committed traverse cannot be distinguished from a cursor trail or colour flash.
