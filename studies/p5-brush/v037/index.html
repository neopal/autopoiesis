BRUSH / CREASE SHEET / v037

The paint hardens
at the touch.

A continuous stroke does not choose a remote answer. It removes the wet layer exactly where the hand passed, and the sheet dries around that incision.

PRESSURE BECOMES A BODY

Draw across the sheet and release. A short or over-held gesture is refused. A complete stroke opens a local crease, changes nearby chambers, and carries its dried pressure into the next stroke. Enter or “make a crease” uses a deterministic line. Delete lifts the latest; Escape, R, or “release sheet” returns to wet.

The sheet is still wet.

ANNOTATIONS / v037

changed rule
Invert the recent remote-consequence grammar. A continuous local pressure stroke is the event: it removes pigment along its own path and dries the neighbouring material around it.
visitor action
Draw one continuous line. Preview remains geometry-neutral; release commits only a stroke long enough to become an incision. Enter, Space, and the crease control use deterministic pressure. Delete lifts the latest. Escape or R releases the sheet. S downloads a PNG.
falsifier
If the stroke only changes a halo or readout, if the answer appears remotely instead of along the contact, if drying does not feed the next stroke, or if lifting is inexact, the material translation fails.
deletion
Delete v037 if caption-free viewing cannot show one continuous sheet with a local cut and drying boundary, or if the gesture reads as a disguised button or generic line drawing.
