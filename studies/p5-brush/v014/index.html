BRUSH / AUTONOMOUS MATERIAL STUDY / v014

The brush keeps
a tide mark.

Take pigment away and the wet field does not only leave a gap. Nearby routes gather into a loaded crest, spill sideways, then drag a delayed shear through the marks that arrive after it.

ONE MATERIAL ACTION

Touch the field to make a tide mark. Nearby strokes pool at the absence, spill around its shoulder, and shed a quieter tail downstream. Lift the latest mark to rebuild the exact prior field.

The wet routes are gathering, cresting, and shedding their load.

ANNOTATIONS / v014

changed rule
Each retained removal becomes a tide mark. Neighbouring routes are pulled into a short loaded pool, crest asymmetrically across it, spill into a fan, and retain a delayed shear that decays downstream.
visitor action
A pointer, Enter, or Space makes one bounded tide mark. Lifting the latest mark recomputes its crest, spill, tail, width, and wet load from the preceding memory.
falsifier
If the routes stay parallel through the mark, if the pool is only a halo, or if the downstream tail leaves stroke coordinates and width unchanged, the tide is a diagram rather than material memory.
deletion
Delete the tide-mark rule if a caption-free comparison with witnesses, notation, labels, and readout hidden cannot locate the pooled crest, sideways spill, and delayed shear in the strokes themselves.
