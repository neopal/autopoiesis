# Brush v034 — The paint keeps the unlooked-for.

## Hypothesis
If attention becomes a material input without becoming a direct brush mark, then five places approached by the visitor can be remembered as a collective census while the watched pigment remains still and the unlooked-for places decide the next geometry.

## Changed rule
v033 made three quiet knocks resolve into a remote hinge, counterweight, and dry void among SVG plates. v031 made a bounded drag refuse a local mark inside a closed ribbon, and v030 made pressure buckle a folded sheet. v034 abandons the plate rhythm, tap threshold, and direct local offer. It is a single raster pigment field of thirteen irregular pools and four wet ribbons. Proximity observes a place but does not deform it. Only five distinct observations form a census; then the watched places stay geometry-neutral while a remote pool dries into an absence, another rises, and a third carries a counterflow.

## Visible consequence
The blind first render is one continuous painted field with embedded pigment pools and wet connective ribbons, not a set of controls. During attention, the geometry stays exact. After a census, the approached places remain visually unchanged: a remote pocket visibly shrinks into a dark aperture, a different pocket swells, and a third bends its counterflow. Later censuses inherit those altered pools and ribbons.

## Interaction
Move or touch near five different pigment places. Repeated proximity to one place does not count. Enter, Space, and the dry-field control use deterministic five-place censuses. Delete or “lift latest” reconstructs the exact preceding field. `Escape` or `R` releases memory. `S` downloads the current PNG.

## Cultural translation
- **Reference:** [little-critters](https://github.com/GordenSun/little-critters)
- **Observed mechanism:** situated agents respond to proximity with legible attention and reciprocal consequence without a dashboard; local presence becomes a social relation.
- **Mutine translation:** refuse animals, eyes, characters, paper, and head-turning. Translate situated reciprocity into a pigment field whose proximity ledger records a collective census, then lets unobserved material—not the watched point—choose the next absence, ridge, and counterflow.
- **Visible consequence:** the watched places remain unchanged before and after commit; a remote dark aperture, raised pool, and counterflow alter actual raster geometry and persist into later censuses.
- **Anti-copy:** no animals, character designs, paper scene, head-turning image, source palette, source composition, source code, example, or API surface is reproduced.

## Repeated grammar refused
The last three Brush works all made a local continuous offer resolve through a discrete or pressure-bearing painted body: v033 used a three-knock SVG laminate, v031 used drag-and-release in a closed ribbon, and v030 used a bounded pulse in a folded sheet. v034 changes the encounter and medium at once: proximity and collective attention replace tap/drag/hold, while p5.js raster field geometry replaces the native SVG plate grammar. The viewer must build a social-like census without the work depicting agents.

## Falsifier
The translation fails if proximity directly deforms a pool, if a repeated pool counts twice, if the watched places answer themselves, if the remote absence is only a label or glow, if later censuses restart from the quiet field, if lifting is inexact, or if the blind field reads as a collection of buttons or an attention dashboard.

## Deletion condition
Delete v034 if labels-off and controls-off viewing cannot show one continuous pigment field with a real remote absence, if the five-place census is not geometry-neutral before commit, or if lifting the latest census does not restore the exact preceding field.
