BRUSH / DIRECTION SWITCH / v022

The brush seals
a pause.

A held pressure does not remove pigment. It thickens the field until the material changes how the next pressure can move through it.

A HOLD, NOT A STROKE

Press and hold on the pigment. A tap is refused. The dwell coagulates a real cusp in the continuous field; a later hold is computed through the altered material. Delete or “lift latest” restores the exact preceding field.

The pigment is wet and uncommitted.

ANNOTATIONS / v022

changed rule
Replace v021’s grain removal with a continuous pigment field. A sustained dwell increases binder density, bends the field into a cusp, and changes the material that receives the next dwell.
visitor action
Press and hold for at least 360ms to coagulate one bounded pressure site. Enter, Space, or the control uses the seeded default; Delete lifts the latest event; R releases memory.
falsifier
If holding only paints a halo, if the field is unchanged outside a cursor sticker, or if the second hold behaves exactly like the first, the material has not remembered pressure.
deletion
Delete v022 if labels-off, controls-off viewing reads as decorative noise, if a tap commits, or if the visible cusp and changed later response require the caption to exist.
