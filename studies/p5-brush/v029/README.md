# Brush v029 — The paint dries around the touch.

## Hypothesis
If material agency can remember pressure without receiving a mark, then stillness itself should become a producing force: a long wait should gather one tongue, settle another, and move a third part of the mass.

## Changed rule
v026 made one sample recast a global slab, v027 made a drag fracture a population, and v028 made a drag reroute a wound. v029 abandons the path and the aperture. The field is a radial sediment mass of eleven solid tongues. The visitor's position only offers a site; duration is the material input. A short visit evaporates. A long stillness swells one tongue, settles a distant tongue, and drifts a third. Four stillness events remain in bounded memory.

## Visible consequence
The blind first render is a broad overlapping radial mass, not a chart, a set of islands, or a wounded reservoir. After a committed wait, actual solid tongue polygons lengthen, narrow, widen, and rotate at three non-local positions. The waiting ring disappears when the pointer leaves.

## Interaction
Hold still over the canvas and release. Movement resets the waiting clock; a short hold is refused. Enter, Space, and the settling control use deterministic dwell events. Delete or “lift latest” reconstructs the exact preceding mass. `R` releases the bounded memory. `S` exports a PNG.

## Cultural translation
- **Reference:** [p5.brush](https://github.com/acamposuribe/p5.brush)
- **Observed mechanism:** pressure, density, grain, and directional fields participate in producing geometry rather than decorating finished geometry.
- **Mutine translation:** refuse the brush mark and make duration the pressure field. Stillness selects no tongue directly; material resistance chooses a swelling tongue, a distant settling tongue, and a third drift.
- **Visible consequence:** the blind radial mass changes its solid polygon bodies after a wait; one gathers, one recedes, and one angles away. Later waits replay against those changed bodies.
- **Anti-copy:** no p5.brush brush, watercolor, hatching, source palette, composition, example, or API surface is reproduced.

## Repeated grammar refused
The previous three Brush works were a detached-island scrape (`v027`), a continuous wound reservoir (`v028`), and a path-based material response. Their repeated gesture was a moving pointer route that writes through a field. v029 changes the encounter from motion to stillness, the representation from islands/reservoir to a radial sediment mass, and the memory from fracture/topology to duration-weighted redistribution.

## Falsifier
The translation fails if a moving gesture commits, if the pointer directly selects the swelling tongue, if only a timer or readout changes, if the solid bodies do not change at three positions, if later waits restart from baseline, or if the blind field reads as a generic radial chart.

## Deletion condition
Delete v029 if labels-off and controls-off viewing cannot show one material mass with distinct solid tongues, if waiting leaves no geometric swell and settling, or if lifting the latest dwell does not reconstruct the exact preceding mass.
