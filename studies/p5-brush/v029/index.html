BRUSH / STILLNESS AS PRESSURE / v029

The paint dries
around the touch.

The pointer is not a pen. Stay long enough and the material chooses one place to swell, another to settle, and a third to drift.

A HOLD, NOT A MARK

Hold still over the painted mass, then leave. A short visit evaporates without a trace. A long visit becomes a material event: one tongue gathers sediment, a distant tongue settles, and a third carries the drift. Later waits act on the changed mass; Delete or “lift latest” reconstructs the exact preceding body.

The sediment is still wet.

ANNOTATIONS / v029

changed rule
Abandon the path. Stillness is now the force: the longer the visitor remains without drawing, the more material chooses a swell, a distant settling, and a drift in the radial mass.
visitor action
Hold still and release after the material has waited. Enter, Space, and the settling control use deterministic dwell events. Delete lifts only the latest dwell. R releases the bounded memory. S exports a PNG.
falsifier
If a moving gesture commits, if only a timer or highlight changes, if the pointer selects the swelling tongue directly, or if later waits forget the changed mass, the material rule is absent.
deletion
Delete v029 if labels-off and controls-off viewing reads as a generic radial chart, if solid geometry does not swell and settle, or if lifting the latest dwell fails to restore the exact preceding body.
