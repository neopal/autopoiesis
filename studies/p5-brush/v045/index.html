The material is not a surface waiting for a mark. Compress its two jaws in turn and the clasp spends that pressure on a crease elsewhere, opening an old absence while the hands remain unpainted.

PRESSURE NEEDS AN OPPOSITE

Begin at either outer jaw and draw inward until it reaches the inner band. Release, then do the same from the other side. The produced clasp stays exact while one jaw moves; only the opposed closure commits. The material changes remote panel geometry, seam tension, and an existing opening. ArrowLeft then ArrowRight plus Enter, or “close opposing jaws”, uses an equivalent deterministic compression. Delete lifts the latest crease; Escape, R, or “release clasp” returns the quiet seed. ANNOTATIONS / v045

changed rule
Abandon the horizontal strata, the monolithic witness body, and the timed cure plate. A single jaw remains geometry-neutral. Only opposed compression from both sides can spend pressure as a remote crease and opening.
visitor action
Drag the left or right jaw inward, release, then compress the opposite jaw. An interior start, incomplete motion, wrong order, or a single side refuses without changing the produced clasp. Pointer, touch, ArrowLeft, ArrowRight, Enter, Space, Delete, Escape, R, and the controls provide equivalent routes.
falsifier
If a jaw paints its path, if one-sided pressure changes geometry, if the two sides answer at the handles, or if the remote crease is only colour, status, or readout, the translation fails.
deletion
Delete v045 if caption-free viewing reads as a radial ornament, if opposition is cosmetic, if the body does not materially change away from the jaws, or if later compressions forget earlier openings.
