BRUSH / DWELL CURE PLATE / v042

The paint cures
around the held breath.

Stillness is not a pause here. Stay long enough at one place and the plate changes elsewhere: a sealed body, a fracture, a bleed. The held place remains quiet.

A DWELL BECOMES MATERIAL TIME

Press and hold near a material well, then release after the plate has accepted the pressure. A short visit and an overcure are refused. The accepted dwell does not print beneath the pointer; it produces three remote material roles in the raster plate. Enter or “hold to cure” uses a deterministic accepted dwell. Delete lifts the latest cure; Escape, R, or “release plate” returns the quiet surface.

The plate is quiet. It accepts no mark from a passing touch.

ANNOTATIONS / v042
