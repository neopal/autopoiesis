BRUSH / BACKFACE MEMBRANE / v041

The material
answers from behind.

A look is not a mark. When one place is held and another is crossed, the surface makes its answer somewhere else: a backface rising where no hand arrived.

A SEPARATED LOOK BECOMES A RELAY

Press near one threshold, travel to a different threshold, and release. The held offer stays geometry-neutral. Only the separated visit commits, and the membrane turns at a remote answer instead of copying the path. Enter or “offer a look” uses a deterministic visit. Delete lifts the latest backface; Escape, R, or “release field” returns the quiet surface.

The membrane is intact. It does not answer a solitary look.

ANNOTATIONS / v041

changed rule
Leave the fragment archive and wound surface behind. A separated visit across two material thresholds produces a third, remote backface in one continuous relief membrane.
visitor action
Hold near one place, move to another, and release. Preview is geometry-neutral; a near-self visit or miss is refused. Enter, Space, and the offer control use a deterministic relay. Delete lifts latest; Escape or R releases. S downloads a PNG.
falsifier
If the held place moves, if the answer appears at the pointer, if a near visit commits, if the remote relief is only colour/readout, or if later visits forget the membrane's prior backfaces, the translation fails.
deletion
Delete v041 if caption-free viewing reads as a generic terrain, node field, or 3D ornament, or if the material cannot visibly answer somewhere other than the offered path.
