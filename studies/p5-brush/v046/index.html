BRUSH / DEPTH-CUT MONOLITH / v046

The brush stores
its cuts in depth.

The surface does not keep the gesture. Draw a cut through the existing dark opening and out the far bank; the mass remembers it elsewhere, by shifting a remote volume and deepening a different void.

A CUT MUST PASS THROUGH ABSENCE

Begin at an outer bank and draw through the central dark opening to the opposite bank. The mass stays exact during the passage. Only a complete through-cut commits: a remote piece moves in depth, another opening widens, and the seam field tightens. Pointer, touch, ArrowLeft then ArrowRight plus Enter, or “draw through the void” offer equivalent routes. Delete lifts the latest cut; Escape, R, or “release the mass” returns the quiet seed. S downloads a PNG.

The mass is quiet. A cut must pass through absence.

ANNOTATIONS / v046

changed rule
Abandon the planar clasp, the edge traverse, and the witness exchange. A passage is geometry-neutral until it enters the central void and exits the opposite bank. Only then does the monolith spend the cut as remote depth and a widened opening.
visitor action
Start at any outer bank, pass through the dark centre, and leave at the opposite bank. Interior starts, same-bank exits, and bypasses refuse without changing the mass. Pointer, touch, keyboard, and the cut control provide equivalent routes.
falsifier
If the path becomes a painted trace, if a bypass commits, if the entry or exit bank answers itself, or if remote displacement is only colour, status, or readout, the translation fails.
deletion
Delete v046 if caption-free viewing reads as a generic 3D block cluster, if absence is only a hole-shaped decoration, if later cuts reset, or if the through-void rule is not visible in the geometry.
