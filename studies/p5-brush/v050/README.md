# v050 — The pigment forgets its missing edge.

## Hypothesis

If material remembers removal rather than addition, a reversible lift should be the only gesture that can spend pigment: the first-render image should read as one dense relief, and each accepted return should leave an actual absence that neighbouring ribs have to close around.

## Radical progression gate

The last three Brush records repeat a displaced-answer grammar: v047 lets an attended island answer elsewhere, v048 sends a completed passage to a remote bloom, and v049 makes a two-site chord open an internal fold. Their repeated gesture is approach or passage followed by a delayed structural response; their metaphor is material remembering an event as a produced form; their interaction is route, proximity, or simultaneous hold; their visual grammar is field, wash, or upright sheet.

v050 breaks that grammar in six visible ways: it changes field / wash / folded sheet into one compact relief tablet; replaces a route or chord with a reversible subtractive lift; removes material instead of adding or displacing it; keeps the absence at the cut rather than answering elsewhere; changes the interaction from approach/hold to inward-and-return; and gives the object a cutout topology that is visible without a remote receiver. The cut is the memory.

## Changed rule

Pressure cannot add a stroke. One of three fixed upper edges must be lifted, dragged inward, reversed toward the same edge, and released. Only that returned gesture commits. The edge site remains fixed; the event removes a real cutout and changes the neighbouring ribs' width, lean, notch, and fold geometry around the absence. Four subtractions compound and remain exactly liftable.

## Visible consequence

The blind first view is a dark, warm relief made of eleven interlocking ribs: neither a wash nor a loose field nor a folded sheet. Accepted lifts carve deep, irregular openings into the relief, with ember edges and shifted neighbouring ribs. The hand path disappears; the missing edge remains as geometry.

## Cultural translation

The selected cultural reference is `p5-brush` (https://github.com/acamposuribe/p5.brush). Its observed mechanism is that pressure, density, directional behaviour, and vector fields participate in producing geometry rather than decorating a finished mark. Mutine inverts that material logic: pressure is forbidden to add anything, and only a reversed lift is allowed to alter the relief's tessellation. Material is still a production rule, but its proof is subtraction and closure, not brush surface.

## Anti-copy boundary

No p5.brush brush preset, watercolor appearance, hatching vocabulary, grain treatment, source palette, source composition, source code, examples, API surface, or source imagery is reproduced. The work uses p5.js Canvas 2D, a compact relief composition, reversible gesture logic, and generated negative cutouts in Mutine's own vocabulary.

## Interaction

- Pointer/touch: press one of the three upper lift sites, drag inward, reverse toward the edge, and release.
- A short or one-way stroke is refused without changing the relief.
- `ArrowLeft`, `ArrowUp`, or `ArrowRight` selects a site; `Enter` / `Space` performs an equivalent deterministic reversible lift.
- `Delete` / `Backspace`: lift the latest cut.
- `Escape` / `R`: release the relief to the quiet seed.
- `S`: save a PNG.

## Falsifier

Delete v050 if a short or one-way stroke commits, if the lifted edge changes, if the cutout is only colour or readout, if later removals reset earlier ones, if lifting is inexact, or if the blind view reads as another sheet, field, route, or generic cutout poster.

## Deletion condition

Delete the trial if caption-free viewing cannot carry subtractive material memory; if the reversible lift is only explanatory prose; or if the first-render relief is merely a refined version of v047-v049's displaced response grammar.

## Direction consequence

This closes Brush's remote-answer, passage, and chord sequence and opens a subtractive-material direction: paint can remember what is no longer present. It abandons the stable expectation that an interaction produces a new mark and tests whether absence can be the active material.
