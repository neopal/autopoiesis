BRUSH / SUBTRACTIVE CLOSURE RELIEF / v050

The pigment
forgets its missing edge.

Pressure cannot add a stroke. Lift an edge, move into the relief, reverse, and leave it: only a returned gesture removes material. The neighbouring ribs close around what is no longer there.

MATERIAL KEEPS THE ABSENCE, NOT THE HAND

Choose one of the three upper lift sites. Pointer or touch: press an edge, drag inward, reverse toward the edge, and release. A short or one-way stroke is refused. ArrowLeft, ArrowUp, or ArrowRight selects a site; Enter or Space makes the same deterministic reversible lift. Delete lifts the latest cut; Escape, R, or “release the relief” returns the quiet seed. S downloads a PNG.

The relief is closed. Lift an edge, move inward, return, and release.

ANNOTATIONS / v050

changed rule
Abandon remote response, wash, and folded-sheet grammars. A stroke is accepted only when it reverses: the completed lift removes a real cutout and shifts neighbouring ribs around the absence.
visitor action
Lift one fixed edge, drag inward, return toward the same edge, and release. Four subtractive cuts compound in one relief and remain exactly liftable.
falsifier
If a short or one-way stroke commits, if the lifted edge deforms, if the cut is only colour or readout, if later removals reset earlier ones, or if the prior relief cannot be reconstructed, the rule fails.
deletion
Delete v050 if caption-free viewing reads as another sheet, field, route, or generic cutout poster; if reversal is only explanatory prose; or if the first view cannot carry subtractive material memory.
