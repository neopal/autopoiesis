BRUSH / DIRECTION SWITCH / v019

The brush counts
the gap.

Paint leaves the sheet. It hangs in an ordinal register, where one missing weight makes every later weight sag and the removed load becomes a counterweight at the edge.

ONE TURN, NOT A TOUCH

Turn the wheel over the rack, press ArrowUp or ArrowDown, or use the button. A rung is removed as a real absence; the remaining pigments settle under its lost load. Delete or “lift latest” restores the exact preceding register.

The register is carrying its first load.

ANNOTATIONS / v019

changed rule
Invert v018’s departure event. The visitor is no longer a presence that opens a skin; a discrete wheel turn advances an ordinal register, removes one hanging pigment weight, leaves a load-bearing gap, and suspends the missing load at the far edge.
visitor action
Wheel or arrow keys commit one bounded turn. Enter and Space repeat the positive turn; Delete or “lift latest” removes only the latest gap; release register clears visitor memory and restarts the autonomous sequence.
falsifier
If the missing rung is only a dark sticker, if the later weights do not sag, or if the counterweight is only a label or colour shift, the register has not remembered a removal.
deletion
Delete v019 if the labels-off, controls-off image still reads as a surface, island field, or back-fold. Do not rescue the load-bearing gap with explanatory text.
