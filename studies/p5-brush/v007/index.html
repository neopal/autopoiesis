BRUSH / AUTONOMOUS MATERIAL STUDY / v007

The brush drinks
the wound.

Take pigment away and the next wet mark is drawn inward. It does not stop at the absence: it releases the pull as a shared wake further along the field.

ONE MATERIAL ACTION

Touch the field to dry a seam. Later strokes gather into that capillary point, then leave it together with a shifted wake and denser wetting. Lift the latest seam to rebuild the exact prior field.

The wet marks are drawing toward and releasing the remembered wound.

ANNOTATIONS / v007

changed rule
Each retained removal becomes a capillary pull. Later strokes are drawn inward while crossing the dry point, then release a shared wake downstream with a changed pigment density and width.
visitor action
A pointer, Enter, or Space makes one bounded seam. Lifting the latest seam recomputes every route, wetting value, and wake from the preceding memory.
falsifier
If the routes only kink at the visible dry mouth, or if each stroke releases in a different direction, the capillary is a diagram rather than material memory.
deletion
Delete the draw-in/release rule if a caption-free comparison with seam witnesses and notation hidden cannot locate a shared gather followed by a downstream wake in the strokes themselves.
