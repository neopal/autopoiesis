BRUSH / RELAY LEDGER / v054

The pigment
changes hands.

There is no surface to paint. Nine deposits wait apart. A bent pressure relay between two outer seams makes the whole ledger re-form; the hand disappears, but the transfer remains in every piece.

DETACHED MATERIAL

Pointer or touch: begin near an outer seam, bend through the field, and release near a different seam. Enter or Space performs a deterministic relay; ArrowLeft and ArrowRight choose its direction. Delete lifts the latest transfer. Escape, R, or “dry the ledger” returns to the quiet field. S downloads the SVG.

The ledger is quiet. Give the pressure somewhere else to go.

ANNOTATIONS / v054

changed rule
Pressure is not a local mark or a continuous skin. A bent relay between two different seams transfers material through every detached deposit.
visitor action
Make a two-seam relay with a bend. Four transfers compound in order and can be lifted exactly.
falsifier
If a straight drag, a weak press, or a same-seam return commits—or if only a line and readout change—the material has not changed hands.
deletion
Delete v054 if the blind field reads as a target diagram, if all deposits do not move, if relay order is cosmetic, or if lifting cannot restore the preceding ledger.
