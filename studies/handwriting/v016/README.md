# Handwriting v016 — The sentence dries toward another mouth.

## State

`v016` is a daily work candidate, recorded as **candidate / held**. It is not a finished period or an exhibition-ready claim.

## Hypothesis

The previous handwriting studies moved from continuous route relays into a discrete field of marks and social attention. This version abandons the quorum and asks whether a letter can behave like material under pressure: can one local load change the route that a later part of the sentence is allowed to take?

## Changed rule

A meaningful drag is pressure, not a selection. The nearest reservoir seals its mouth and changes scale; the adjacent spine is removed as an actual break; the overflow is carried into a later reservoir through a new bridge. Later pressure events replay through the same material builder. A short click is refused.

## Visible consequence

The first encounter is one connected capillary sentence: twelve swollen letter-like reservoirs joined by a continuous spine. After a pressure drag, the source chamber visibly closes, its old join disappears, the receiving chamber shifts and opens, and a later overflow bridge cuts across the sentence. The work is an object with a material route, not a grid of agents or a bundle of parallel handwriting paths.

## Cultural translation

- **Reference:** `little-critters` — https://github.com/GordenSun/little-critters
- **Observed mechanism:** situated code-drawn agents respond to pointer proximity by changing attention and looking back, making the visitor part of a reciprocal encounter rather than a controller of a dashboard.
- **Mutine translation:** refuse the look-back and translate relation into material pressure. The visitor's path loads one reservoir; that load seals the source, breaks its local adjacency, and forces a later mouth to accept the overflow.
- **Visible consequence:** the blind field is one connected sentence with actual chamber openings, missing spine segments, and a downstream bridge after pressure; it does not merely recolour a fixed mark field.
- **Falsifier:** if a short click changes the work, if the old spine remains below the overflow, or if the blind image needs labels to reveal the sealed mouth and reroute, the translation fails.
- **Anti-copy:** no animals, characters, paper scene, head-turning animation, source palette, composition, or surface vocabulary from `little-critters` is reproduced.

## Visitor action

In an interactive preview, drag across the sentence or use **press & reroute**. Enter and Space repeat the last pressure path. **Lift latest** removes only the latest pressure event and reconstructs the preceding material sentence; **release sequence** returns to the deterministic soft chain. The focused SVG also supports `Delete` or `U` to lift, `R` to release, and `S` to export the current SVG.

## Deletion condition

Delete v016 if the work still reads as v015's attention ecology or as a decorative line network; if the old bridge remains under the redirected load; if later reservoirs do not visibly carry pressure; or if lifting the latest pressure fails to reproduce the previous SVG signature exactly.

## Direction consequence

This closes the handwriting current's v015 attention quorum and opens a material-route direction: letters are reservoirs, adjacency can break, and the next glyph is produced by what the previous one could not contain. The next viable handwriting experiment must either make the material rule more irreversible or abandon the capillary sentence rather than polish another attention field.
