HANDWRITING / COLLECTIVE SYNTAX / v029

The quiet gets
quoted elsewhere.

A word can be noticed without being allowed to speak for itself. The census answers for the one the crowd forgot.

WANDER, THEN COUNT

Move through the word field. Attention accumulates locally but does not deform the type. When four separate words have been visited, take a census: the most-heard word goes quiet, the least-heard word inherits its phrase, and a third word carries the quiet word's old place. Enter, Space, or the button uses a deterministic walk; Delete lifts the latest census; R or Escape releases the bounded ledger.

The words have not been counted.

ANNOTATIONS / v029

Abandon the counter body, pressure loom, and depth rack. Twelve situated word islands now carry attention as a latent social state; a census rewrites actual DOM text and placement instead of drawing a reply line.

Wander across at least four words, then count. Contact is observational. The deterministic census, Enter, and Space use the same collective event; Delete lifts the latest census; R or Escape releases the four-event ledger.
falsifier
If hovering or movement changes the words before a census, if the selected word answers itself, if the quietest word does not inherit real text, if the third relay is cosmetic, or if the blind field reads as another route, rack, stack, or letter grid, the translation fails.
deletion
Delete v029 if the collective rule is only an attention glow, if a deterministic census cannot change actual word bodies, if later censuses forget the changed vocabulary, or if lifting cannot restore the exact preceding archipelago.
