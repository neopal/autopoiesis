# Handwriting v029 — The quiet gets quoted elsewhere.

## Status

Candidate / held. This is a real executable study with a deterministic engine, a bounded interaction ledger, and a canonical daily record. It is not called exhibition-ready until the caption-free perceptual and production gates are observed.

## Hypothesis

If language is treated as a situated population rather than a line to read, then a visitor's attention can become a latent social syntax: nothing moves while attention wanders, but a collective census can make the most-heard word go quiet and let the word least attended inherit its phrase.

## Changed rule

Abandon v028's two-place counter exchange, v027's global duration pressure, and v026's approach/withdraw route custody. Twelve actual DOM word islands hold an attention ledger. Pointer movement only records which islands were visited. A census is the only causal threshold: it silences the most-attended island, gives its phrase to the least-attended island, and gives a third relay the quiet island's former phrase. The rewrite is performed in text content and CSS geometry, not in a trace, glow, or explanatory label.

## Visible consequence

The blind first render is an open archipelago of twelve oversized words, not a sentence corridor, stacked loom, letter body, or route sculpture. After a valid census, one word becomes a dash, a remote word speaks the former speaker's phrase, a third word inherits the former quiet phrase, and nearby islands shift cadence and position. Later censuses begin from the altered vocabulary. Delete reconstructs the exact preceding state; the ledger never exceeds four events.

## Interaction

- Wander or drag through the field: attention accumulates, geometry stays exact.
- Visit at least four different words, then choose **take census**.
- Enter and Space use a deterministic five-word walk followed by the same census rule.
- Delete / Backspace lifts the latest census.
- R / Escape releases the field.

## Cultural translation

**Reference:** [little-critters](https://github.com/GordenSun/little-critters)

**Observed mechanism:** a population of situated code-drawn agents changes attention in response to pointer proximity; the visitor becomes part of a reciprocal social field without a dashboard.

**Mutine translation:** refuse animals, eyes, characters, and the look-back scene. Translate situated attention into language-as-population: words can be locally noticed but cannot answer at the moment of notice. A collective census elects roles from the attention ledger and rewrites actual word bodies through a speaker, a quiet receiver, and a relay.

**Visible consequence:** movement alone leaves the word geometry and text exact. After a census, a real word goes silent, a distant word receives its phrase, and a third word receives the former quiet phrase. Repeated censuses inherit the changed vocabulary.

**Falsifier:** if pointer movement changes text or geometry before the census, if the selected word answers itself, if the remote quotation is only a glow or readout, if later censuses restart from the seed vocabulary, or if the blind field reads as another route, rack, stack, or letter grid, the translation fails.

**Anti-copy:** no animals, eyes, characters, paper style, head-turning scene, source palette, source composition, source code, API surface, or surface vocabulary is reproduced.

**Direction consequence:** closes Handwriting's counter-topology / pressure-loom / depth-custody sequence and opens a collective-syntax direction in which handwriting is a social field of words whose latent attention becomes a grammar only at census. It changes both the medium and the viewer task.

## Radical progression gate

The last three typography records repeatedly used non-local transfer inside a composed word/letter body: v026 moved route custody after withdrawal, v027 redistributed space after a hold, and v028 exchanged counters between two gaps. v029 breaks that invariant in four visible ways:

1. **Representation:** from connected body, stacked bands, or 3D routes to separated DOM word islands and actual text mutation.
2. **Composition:** from corridor/rack/field-body to open negative space with no reading line or bridge.
3. **Encounter:** from withdrawal, duration, or two-place crossing to distributed wandering followed by an explicit census.
4. **Memory:** from direct event transfer to a latent attention ledger whose stored state is inert until a collective threshold.

The work can do something the previous three could not: it lets attention accumulate without changing the image, then makes the crowd—not the addressed word—rewrite the vocabulary.

## Deletion condition

Delete v029 if the caption-free field is only a generic word cloud; if the first four observations are not geometry-neutral; if the census does not change actual DOM text in three roles; if later censuses forget earlier vocabulary; if exact lift fails; or if the little-critters translation is only a decorative attention halo.
