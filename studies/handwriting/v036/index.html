HANDWRITING / SOCIAL SYNTAX / v036

The sentence answers
from its margins.

A phrase is not inserted where it is offered. The fragments that have heard least become its new mouth.

SPEECH IS A DISTRIBUTION RULE

Move over a fragment to give it attention without changing its form. Then type a phrase and submit it. The phrase is refused at the input and redistributed to the least-heard fragment: one receives a new mouth, one falls quiet, and one carries the chorus. Four utterances remain liftable; later speech inherits the altered sentence.

The field is quiet. No phrase has entered the ecology.

ANNOTATIONS / v036

changed rule
Abandon v035's pressure rack, v034's double register, and v033's material plate. Attention increments a private ledger but never deforms a fragment. A complete utterance is redistributed to the least-heard margin as produced text, absence, and chorus.
visitor action
Attend with pointer or arrow keys, type a phrase, and press speak. Enter, Space, the speak control, Delete, R, and Escape remain deterministic equivalents; four utterances stay liftable and later utterances inherit the changed ecology.
falsifier
Delete if attention changes geometry, if the phrase appears at the input, if the least-heard fragment is not changed, if silence is only colour, if the blind field reads as a generic word cloud, or if later speech forgets.
deletion
Delete v036 if caption-free viewing cannot distinguish the phoneme ecology from a type specimen, rack, register, or decorative word cloud, or if its redistribution needs prose to be perceived.
