ANNOTATIONS / v009

changed rule
A remembered refusal becomes a lane switch. Two adjacent routes approach one seam, cross, exchange vertical order, and keep the displacement downstream.
visitor action
A pointer, Enter, or Space places one bounded switch at the last pointer position. Route points are recomputed from memory; lifting the latest switch rebuilds the exact preceding field.
falsifier
Hide the switch witnesses, labels, and readout. If the lines do not visibly cross and leave in exchanged order, the switch is only a diagram.
deletion
Delete v009 if a caption-free comparison finds only a coloured accent, if the pair does not cross once, or if the copy explains more lane exchange than the handwriting can show.
