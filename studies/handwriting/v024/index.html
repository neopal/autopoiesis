HANDWRITING / MATERIAL SWITCH / v024

The margin
pushes back.

A word is not placed on a page. It is made by the resistance left behind by the word before it.

PRESS THE SENTENCE

Click inside the field to make a local pressure event. The event does not mark the place and disappear: it bends the actual word field, rewrites one anchor and a distant reply, and leaves resistance for the next pressure. Enter or Space uses the deterministic centre. Delete lifts the latest event; R releases the material.

The sentence is quiet.
