# Handwriting v045 — The word takes attendance.

## Hypothesis

If situated attention can become a relation between agents rather than a control panel, then a phrase can treat its letters as a population: three distinct looks should remain provisional, and their ordered bearing should change the phrase's reading axis rather than decorate the looked-at glyphs.

## Changed rule

v045 abandons v044's material carrier, v043's social room, v042's remote route fusion, and v041's negative-gap reading. The first encounter is a stacked Canvas typographic block, not a beam or a distributed room. A pointer or touch tap selects one letter as a witness. The first two witnesses remain geometrically exact. On the third distinct witness, the three-letter order becomes a collective bearing: every letter is re-positioned, re-angled, re-scaled, and re-ordered along a segmented reading axis. The visitor does not move a cursor-shaped decoration and no looked-at letter answers locally; the phrase changes who comes first.

## Visible consequence

The blind first view is a dark three-line phrase, `ATTEND · THE · WORD ·`, with a quiet syntax grid and uneven typographic weight. After a valid attention sequence, the block collapses into a diagonal, segmented reading axis. The letters keep their identity but change their actual route, turns, widths, and collective order. Four assemblies compound as one inherited phrase and lifting replays the preceding history exactly.

## Interaction

- Pointer or touch: tap three distinct letters. The third witness commits the assembly.
- Pending attention leaves all letter geometry exact; a repeated or incomplete look is refused.
- Keyboard: focus the field, use ArrowRight / ArrowLeft to attend letters, then Enter or Space to commit a pending trio; with no pending trio, Enter performs the deterministic sequence.
- `assemble witnesses`: deterministic equivalent action.
- Delete / Backspace: lift the latest assembly exactly.
- Escape / `R`: release the archive to the quiet seed.
- Static previews replay four assemblies and remove furniture and controls.

## Radical progression gate

The last three real Handwriting records were v044's load-bearing Canvas carrier, v042's fused SVG routes, and v041's remote negative readings (with v043's social room as the intervening record): all made language a non-local event in a distributed route grammar. v045 breaks that family in five visible dimensions:

1. **Representation / medium:** the carrier beam and route bodies become a generated Canvas population of independent letter agents.
2. **Composition:** one baseline and one social room become a stacked three-line phrase with a syntax grid, then a broken collective axis.
3. **Encounter:** dragging a material rail or attending a room becomes explicit, order-sensitive looking at distinct letters; no hover-only path carries the work.
4. **Temporal behaviour:** material accumulation becomes a remembered witness sequence that changes the reading order itself.
5. **Viewer task:** the visitor is not asked to load, join, or leave a route. They decide which letters are allowed to count together.

The work can do something the previous three could not: it makes the *order of attention* a structural reading operation. The same letters remain present, but the phrase becomes a different route because a population has collectively taken attendance.

## Falsifier

Delete v045 if caption-free viewing reads as a styled word poster or generic crowd diagram; if pending attention moves geometry; if repeated or incomplete looks commit; if witness order only changes prose; if fewer than most letters re-form; if later assemblies forget; or if exact lifting fails.

## Deletion condition

Delete v045 if the blind tableau cannot show that typography has become a population with a material reading order, or if the little-critters translation collapses into a cursor halo, character imitation, or decorative attention effect.
