A LIGATURE IS A DISPLACED JOIN

Drag from one open mouth to another with a pointer or touch. The temporary join is discarded on release: only a complete pair is allowed to spend the event. Two untouched routes elsewhere then fuse, kink, and thicken as one produced ligature. The bind control, ArrowRight plus Enter, and pointer path use the same deterministic grammar; Delete lifts the latest fusion.

The body is quiet. Eight open routes are holding.
