# Handwriting v010 — The sentence grows a hinge.

## Hypothesis

If a remembered refusal is rendered as a finite hinge rather than a lane
exchange, handwriting can keep a visible consequence without changing its
identity: a local family will pivot around one seam and carry a changed exit
into the sentence.

## Changed rule

v009 let two routes cross and exchange order. v010 replaces that topology with
an angular pocket: a local family of neighbouring routes approaches one shared
pivot index, folds through it, and leaves with a small downstream offset. The
witness marks are annotation only; the route geometry carries the rule.

## Visible consequence

At rest, the field is a set of slow, imperfect horizontal passages. As the
sequence accumulates, each remembered hinge appears as a finite bright bend
with a common vertical seam. A route does not teleport or swap lanes: its exit
keeps the fold's offset. Pointer placement chooses the seam's position and
affected family. Enter and Space place at the last pointer position. Lifting
the latest hinge recomputes the exact preceding field.

editorial furniture. If the affected routes do not still read as a finite

routes do not share one pivot seam, if the bend is indistinguishable from a
smooth wave, or if the copy explains more memory than the lines can show.
