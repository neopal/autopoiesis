HANDWRITING / MATERIAL SYNTAX / v037

The line learns
where to break.

Pressure does not choose a letter. It makes the sentence discover its own weak hinge.

A SENTENCE IS A LOAD-BEARING FORM

Drag across the seam and let go. The gesture is geometry-neutral while it is held. On release, the line cuts at the hinge it can no longer carry: one glyph opens, one hollows, and one drifts. Four cuts remain liftable; later pressure inherits the broken sentence.

The sentence is quiet. No pressure has entered the seam.

ANNOTATIONS / v037

changed rule
Abandon v036's ecology, v035's casting rack, and v034's double register. Pressure is held as a probe, then a complete sweep cuts the folded sentence at a material hinge: a cut, a hollow, and a drift become produced glyph geometry.
visitor action
Drag and release through the seam. Arrow keys, Enter, Space, the pressure control, Delete, R, and Escape are deterministic equivalents. A short or overlong sweep is refused.
falsifier
Delete if the gesture changes geometry before release, if the input point is merely selected, if the cut is only colour or prose, if the field reads as a type specimen, or if later cuts forget.
deletion
Delete v037 if caption-free viewing cannot distinguish this single load-bearing sentence from a word cloud, rack, register, or decorative SVG, or if the weak hinge is not visibly produced.
