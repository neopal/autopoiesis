The route
forgets its first letter.

A letter is not printed. It is the path a body takes through a field, and what that path makes possible somewhere else.

A PATH IS A LETTER'S MEMORY

Trace four distinct junctions with a pointer or touch, or use ArrowRight to walk the deterministic route. The traced path is only pending evidence: the selected junctions and their lines remain exact. Enter or the rewire control commits the route, and three unselected junctions answer by turning, splicing, and stuttering the surrounding paths. Four route memories remain liftable.

The route is quiet. No junctions have been traced.
