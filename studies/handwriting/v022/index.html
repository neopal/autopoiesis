HANDWRITING / MATERIAL SWITCH / v022

Pressure changes
the word.

A sentence is not ink on top of a surface. Cross its stones and the letters yield, retype themselves, and make a distant word carry the load.

TRAVEL THROUGH THE TYPE

Drag across the field. A short tap does nothing. A traveled pressure changes the crossed glyphs, displaces their stones, and retypes a remote answer. Later pressure inherits resistance from the sentence already changed.

The word is unpressed.

ANNOTATIONS / v022

changed rule
Abandon the pressure slab, vertical channels, and orbital witness field. Eighteen actual SVG glyphs sit as separate word stones; a traveled pressure makes crossed letters yield and retypes a distant reply.
visitor action
Travel through the type and release. A tap is refused. Enter, Space, or the control commits a seeded pressure; Delete lifts the latest pressure; R releases the sentence.
falsifier
If pressure only paints a trace, if letters do not change their geometry or identity, if the remote reply is cosmetic, or if later pressure ignores resistance, material language has not become a rule.
deletion
Delete v022 if the blind field reads as a decorative word grid, if the changed word needs the caption to be seen, or if the traveled gesture is just a prettified slider.
