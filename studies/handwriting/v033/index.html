HANDWRITING / MATERIAL SYNTAX / v033

The blank bends
the sentence.

A gap is not the absence of a letter. It is the pressure that makes the next letter take shape.

LOAD THE NEGATIVE SPACE

Choose two non-adjacent wells along the plate. The first selection only loads the blank. The second creates a material span: the sentence bends at a distance, a copied form appears, and another glyph opens into a counterform. The wells themselves stay exact. Arrow keys move focus; Enter and Space perform a deterministic fold.

The plate is quiet. No blank is carrying pressure.

ANNOTATIONS / v033

changed rule
Abandon v032's cadence, v031's route, and v030's sentence-wide refusal. The work is one typographic plate with five negative wells. A non-adjacent gap span changes how the whole alphabet is produced: the pressure folds remote letters rather than decorating the selected blank.
visitor action
Load two non-adjacent negative wells. Arrow keys move the well focus; Enter or Space loads it. Delete lifts the latest material fold; R or Escape releases the plate. Four folds remain liftable and later folds replay from the changed material.
falsifier
Delete if a single well changes geometry, if adjacent wells commit, if selected wells become the answer, if pressure exists only as colour or labels, if later folds restart from the quiet plate, or if the counterform is not an actual glyph absence.
deletion
Delete v033 if caption-free viewing reads as a decorated word or route diagram instead of a material typographic plate whose blank spaces visibly produce later forms.
