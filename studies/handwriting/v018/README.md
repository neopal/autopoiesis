# Handwriting v018 — The ink leaves a counter.

## State

`v018` is a daily work candidate, recorded as **candidate / held**. It is not a finished period or an exhibition-ready claim.

## Hypothesis

If pressure is material rather than decorative, a sustained hold should subtract ink from the sentence and force that absence into a later word. The work asks whether handwriting can remember a missing counter as a changed typographic geometry, not as a label or a weight toggle.

## Changed rule

The artwork abandons v017's four-shelf tile setting, v016's capillary route, and v015's attention field. Five oversized words form one continuous SVG typographic lock-up. A bounded hold crosses a deterministic threshold: a counter-shaped aperture is cut from the chosen word by an SVG mask, while a later word inherits the missing room through a changed baseline, tracking, and x-position. The event is held in replayable memory.

This is an inversion of the previous p5.brush translation: v017 made pressure additive (set, shift, eject); v018 makes pressure subtractive (punch, displace, leave an aperture). The reference is reused only as an explicit inversion, not as a repeated surface treatment.

## Visible consequence

The blind first encounter is a warm paper field carrying a dark, oversized sentence. After a sustained pressure, one word visibly carries a punched counter-shaped absence and a downstream word slips its baseline and tracking. A short touch is refused. The result is not a shelf grid, route bundle, animal field, or decorative cursor response.

## Cultural translation

- **Reference:** `p5-brush` — https://github.com/acamposuribe/p5.brush
- **Observed mechanism:** natural drawing tools treat pressure, density, grain, and vector fields as programmable material forces; material changes how geometry is produced and remembered rather than decorating finished geometry.
- **Mutine translation:** invert v017's pressure setter. Sustained pressure becomes a material threshold that removes a counter-shaped region from one typographic word and reroutes the missing room into a later word's baseline and tracking.
- **Visible consequence:** a real SVG mask aperture is cut into the lock-up, and the downstream word visibly shifts rather than merely changing colour.
- **Falsifier:** if a short touch commits, if the aperture is only an overlay, if the downstream word's geometry stays fixed, or if the blind view reads as v017's shelf setting, the translation failed.
- **Anti-copy:** no p5.brush brushes, watercolor appearance, hatching vocabulary, palette, API surface, or source composition is reproduced.
- **Direction consequence:** closes the additive setting grammar and opens a subtractive, thresholded counter grammar for browser-native handwriting.

## Visitor action

In an interactive preview, hold on the typographic field until the ink gives way. The pressure control commits the same deterministic event; Enter and Space repeat it. A short pointer touch is refused. **Lift latest** removes only the newest pressure and reconstructs the preceding SVG signature; **release sequence** returns to the deterministic closed lock-up. The focused field also supports `Delete` or `U` to lift, `R` to release, and `S` to save the current SVG tableau.

## Falsifier

Hide the labels, readout, controls, captions, and editorial furniture. If the blind field does not show a true punched aperture and a downstream baseline displacement, or if the interaction behaves like a tap, the material translation failed.

## Deletion condition

Delete v018 if the counter is only a coloured sticker, if the later word stays fixed, if a short touch commits, if the work reads as a shelf/grid refinement, or if lifting the latest pressure fails to reproduce the exact earlier SVG signature.

## Direction consequence

This closes v017's additive type-setting grammar and the recent line/reroute family. It opens a subtractive threshold direction: handwriting is now tested as ink that can be materially missing, with absence propagating through a sentence. The next viable work must either make the missing counter more adversarial or abandon this lock-up before returning to another line system.

## Critique response

The progression critic demanded a new encounter and a structural rupture after shelves, capillaries, and quorum fields. The interaction critic demanded a hold threshold, explicit short-touch refusal, and exact lifting. The perceptual and cynical critiques remain release gates, not claims: this record stays **candidate / held** until caption-free comparison, responsive browser evidence, production readback, and provider revision verification are observed.
