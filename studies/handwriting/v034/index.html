HANDWRITING / SITUATED SYNTAX / v034

The gap keeps
an elsewhere.

A letter can be near you without moving. The sentence changes only after the witness is gone.

LEAVE THE REGISTER

Move toward one fragment and let it register your presence. The attended fragment stays exact. Leave the field, or press the action, and the least-witnessed fragment in the other register answers by shifting its actual scale, angle, and position. The source does not answer itself. Arrow keys move the witness; Enter or Space leaves it.

The register is quiet. No fragment is being witnessed.

ANNOTATIONS / v034

changed rule
Abandon v033's material plate, v032's cadence score, and v031's route organism. The sentence is now a browser-native double register of situated fragments. Attention is recorded without geometry; departure makes a remote fragment answer.
visitor action
Approach one fragment, then leave. Pointer movement and focus are an offer only. Enter, Space, and the leave control make the same deterministic departure; Delete lifts the newest answer and R or Escape releases the register. Four departures remain liftable.
falsifier
Delete if attention moves the watched fragment, if the source answers itself, if the remote response is only colour or UI, if the work reads as a route/grid, if later departures forget, or if exact lifting fails.
deletion
Delete v034 if caption-free viewing cannot distinguish the double register from a decorated word collage, route diagram, or interface; the browser-native fragments must carry the relation before the prose does.
