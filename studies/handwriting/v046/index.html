A SPAN BECOMES A MATERIAL VOID

Drag across a contiguous run of letters, then cut the held pressure. While pending, the inventory stays exact. When cut, the selected run becomes a vertical pressure fibre and an actual counterform; every other letter is re-packed around the new void. Pointer, touch, ArrowLeft / ArrowRight plus Enter, and the deterministic control share the same grammar. Delete lifts the latest cut.

The inventory is quiet. Drag across a contiguous span of three to six letters, then cut it.

ANNOTATIONS / v046

changed rule
Abandon the phrase-axis, carrier, and route grammars. A contiguous span is geometry-neutral while held; cutting it turns the span into a material fibre and re-produces every other letter around a real counterform.
visitor action
Drag or touch across 3–6 adjacent letters, then cut the held span. ArrowLeft / ArrowRight chooses a span and Enter commits. The deterministic control performs the same cut. Delete lifts the latest cut; Escape or R releases. Four counterforms compound.
falsifier
Delete if the cut only moves the selected letters, if the void is an overlay, if short or occupied spans commit, if later cuts reset the reflow, or if lifting is inexact.
deletion
Delete v046 if caption-free viewing cannot distinguish a material counterform and DOM reflow from v041–v045's gaps, routes, carrier, or collective reading axis.
